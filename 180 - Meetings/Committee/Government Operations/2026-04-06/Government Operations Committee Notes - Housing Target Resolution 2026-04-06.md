# Government Operations Committee -- Housing Target Resolution 2026-04-06

## Summary

César presented the Housing Target Resolution to the Government Operations Committee on April 6, 2026 -- its first committee presentation. The resolution was generally well received, but Joe Atkins raised a significant challenge to the underlying county housing numbers, and Peter Brehm offered a potential path around that obstacle.

## Discussion Notes

Katie Nash received the resolution well with no significant objections.

Peter Brehm had a few questions but nothing substantive.

Libby Taylor wants to see stronger renter protection language added to the resolution but was otherwise amenable to it.

Joe Atkins is not fully convinced that the numbers released by the county as part of their housing report -- which is the source of the resolution's figures -- are accurate. The committee will need to either bring county representatives in to defend the numbers or find an alternative approach.

Peter Brehm suggested a potential workaround: remove the specific numbers from the resolution and replace them with language committing to determine what the numbers should be, framing it as a process commitment rather than a hard target.

## Open Items

The central tension is between Joe's skepticism of the county numbers and the need to move the resolution forward. Peter's suggestion offers an escape valve but may weaken the resolution's policy force. Libby's renter protection ask is additive and likely the path of least resistance.

Options on the table:
- Bring county representatives to defend the numbers
- Replace numbers with process language (Peter's suggestion)
- Revise numbers independently

## Relevance

This is the first committee presentation of [[Housing Production Target Resolution]]. The numbers question ties directly to [[Frederick DHCD Housing Production Target]], which has not yet been published. The April 9 FY2027 Housing and Human Services budget session is an adjacent pressure point.
