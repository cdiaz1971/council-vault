# Culture and Community Committee Brief 2026-09-03

**Culture and Community Committee -- September 3, 2026 at 3:00 PM, City Hall Boardroom**
**Format:** Workshop only. No votes.

## Bottom Line

Two workshop items, neither requiring a vote. First is an informational review of the City's six multi-year non-profit MOUs (Downtown Frederick Partnership, East Frederick Rising, Golden Mile Alliance, Visit Frederick/Tourism Council, Boys and Girls Club, Celebrate Frederick), totaling roughly $570K in direct FY27 City funding plus two in-kind partnerships. Staff recommendation is "information only" with no next steps listed, so this reads as a routine annual check-in rather than a renewal decision point -- worth confirming that reading live, since the Downtown Frederick Partnership MOU already went through a separate Council vote on July 2 (5-1-1, Nash opposed, César abstaining). Second is a CTO briefing on public WiFi in city parks and how the City communicates tech trends to residents -- also informational, no fiscal impact listed. Lowest-stakes committee session of the cycle, but the digital divide item touches César's underserved-communities priorities and is worth engaging on substantively even without a vote pending.

## Action Items

None. Workshop-format discussion only; both executive summaries list "INFORMATION ONLY" or "N/A" under Staff/Commission Recommendation and Next Steps.

## Consent Agenda

None. Committee workshops don't carry a consent agenda. Item II is approval of the August 6 minutes, which reflects the Committee's own prior action, not a consent bundle.

## Presentations and Reports

**A. Review of City MOUs and Associated Policies (Richard Griffin, Director of Economic Development).** Executive summary reviews the City's six active multi-year non-profit MOUs -- separate from the dozens of annual Community Promotions Program grant MOUs, which aren't part of this discussion. Note the summary's own text says "seven" multi-year MOUs but only lists and totals six; worth flagging the discrepancy rather than assuming a missing seventh.

- **Downtown Frederick Partnership (2026 MOU).** Main Street program, events, and Ambassador Program. City funding: $117,280 (FY27) rising to $129,301 (FY29), plus $195,000-$205,000 for the Ambassador Program over the same span -- $312,280 total FY27. DFP owes a 1:1 fundraising match. This MOU already cleared a full Council vote on July 2 (5-1-1, Nash opposed on Route 40 corridor equity grounds, César abstaining) -- tonight is a recap of an MOU already in force, not a pending decision.
- **East Frederick Rising (2025 MOU).** Advocacy and revitalization on the East Side; Mayor, a Council member, and the Economic Development Director sit on EFR's board. City funding ramps from $30K (FY27) to $60K (FY29), with EFR required to match 50%.
- **Golden Mile Alliance (2024 MOU).** Corridor promotion, marketing, and safety on the Golden Mile. City funding was $75K-$100K FY24-FY26, plus an additional $100K in FY27 for operations/marketing/events -- $200K FY27 total. Alliance matches 20-50% and reports annually; a Council member sits on its board.
- **Visit Frederick / Tourism Council of Frederick County (2022 MOU).** Manages the Visitor Center and destination marketing for roughly 2 million annual county visitors. Flat $28,000/year operational funding.
- **Boys and Girls Club of Frederick County (2019 MOU, 2023 extension).** City provides the Burck Street Youth Center and South End Park and covers building/grounds maintenance; the Club pays the City $350/month rent and runs after-school and summer programming for disadvantaged youth, with income-based scholarships. In-kind only from the City's side.
- **Celebrate Frederick, Inc. (2022 MOU).** Runs major public events (Independence Day, In the Streets, the Summer Concert Series). City contributes in-kind logistics -- Office of Special Events staff time, facilities, public safety, cleanup -- rather than direct funding.

Equity framing in the executive summary ties the MOU package to NAC districts 5, 8, 9, 10, 11, and 12 and several Strategic Plan goals (sustainable urban planning, social well-being, safe/vibrant community, mobility, civic engagement).

**B. City Digital Divide Review and Proposed Technology Service Enhancements (Matt Bowman, CTO).** Briefing on public WiFi in city parks -- benefits, drawbacks, costs, resident impact, and security considerations -- plus how the City keeps residents informed about relevant technology trends. No fiscal impact, NAC impact, or equity impact statement provided; strategic plan goal listed as "N/A -- Operational." Presentation deck is attached to the packet but its content isn't summarized in the executive summary, so the substantive detail (proposed sites, cost estimates, security approach) will come out live in the meeting.

Confirm whether tonight's MOU review is purely informational or whether any of the five MOUs not already voted on (East Frederick Rising, Golden Mile Alliance, Visit Frederick, Boys and Girls Club, Celebrate Frederick) are approaching a renewal date that will need a Council vote -- the packet doesn't say, and "information only" could mean either "nothing pending" or "renewal decisions are coming separately."
