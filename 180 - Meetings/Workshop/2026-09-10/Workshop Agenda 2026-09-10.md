# Workshop Agenda -- September 10, 2026

**City Council Work Session -- September 10, 2026**

> [!warning] No published Order of Business on file

No cover agenda document with a full Order of Business (call to order, approval of minutes, old business, elected officials' comments) was included in the download, unlike the August 27, 2026 packet. ## Items (as packaged)

**A. Correction to Prior Presentation -- Husky Park Forest Easement (CIP 320044)**
- A.1 Executive Summary Form (Husky Park)
- A.2 Unconditionally Approved Forest Conservation Plan -- Renn Development (Sheet 1)

Submitted by COO Zack Kershner. Corrects information given at the August 27, 2026 work session: the Renn Final Forest Conservation Plan was believed to have extinguished the existing 1995 forest conservation easement on Husky Park (530 Highland Street); it did not. A future City action to release and abandon the existing easement will be required. No fiscal impact stated, no vote associated with this item.

**B. PILOT Agreement -- Joyfield at Worman's Mill Court**
- B.1 Executive Summary Form
- B.3 Standard Communities presentation deck (draft, 8/27)
- B.4 Standard Communities presentation deck (final + addendum, 9/2)
- B.5 Draft joint City-County PILOT agreement

Submitted by Jodie Ostoich, Housing Policy Advisor (HHS), with Lori Gjoni of Standard Communities. Joint City-County Payment in Lieu of Taxes agreement for a proposed 306-unit, 100% affordable (50-60% AMI) rental development at 60/62/64/66 Worman's Mill Court. Fiscal impact: an annual tax credit to the project of $242,964. Staff recommendation: adopt. Next step: City Council approval on September 17, 2026. Note: B.1's attachment list also references a City-prepared PowerPoint on the PILOT itself; it was not found in this packet (only Standard Communities' own decks, B.3/B.4, are present).

**C. Resolution -- Affordable Housing Action**
- C.1 Executive Summary Form
- C.2 Draft Resolution, "Declaring the City of Frederick's Commitment to Action on Affordable Housing"

Submitted by Council President Díaz. Review and discussion only; not yet introduced. Formally acknowledges the Mayor's Affordable Housing Task Force final report ("Opening Doors in Frederick," July 2026) and the TPMA Housing Data Report, adopts a production floor of 4,000 additional units over ten years against a documented need of 13,774 units by 2035, and directs an annual Housing Production Report starting FY2028. Next step per the Executive Summary: Council President to incorporate tonight's feedback and bring an updated draft to a future public meeting for introduction.

**D. Ordinance -- Maryland Housing Certainty Act Implementation**
- D.1 Executive Summary Form
- D.2 Draft Ordinance (G-26-__)

Submitted by City Attorney Rachel Gray on behalf of the Administration; introduced by Council President Díaz on the Administration's behalf. First Read (Introduction) already occurred September 3, 2026; tonight's work session discussion precedes a Second Read. Interim ordinance implementing the Maryland Housing Certainty Act (HB 548/SB 325), effective October 1, 2026: defines "complete application," grants 5-year vested development rights from a complete application's submission, and limits development impact fee collection to no earlier than 30 days before final inspection. Staff recommendation: proceed to public hearing for adoption.
