# Workshop Minutes -- September 10, 2026

**City Council Work Session -- September 10, 2026, 6:00 PM**

## Summary

Council President Díaz called the meeting to order at 6:00 PM. Present: Vice President Nash and Council members Shackelford, Brehm, Adkins, and Hempel Irani. Council member Taylor was absent. Four workshop items were discussed (Husky Park correction, PILOT presentation, the affordable housing action resolution, and the Housing Certainty Act implementation ordinance); no item other than approval of the prior meeting's minutes came to a vote, consistent with the work session format. Meeting adjourned at 7:26 PM.

## Outcomes

**Approval of Minutes.** On a motion by Council member Brehm, seconded by Vice President Nash, the Council approved 6-0 the minutes of the August 27, 2026 City Council Work Session.

**Old Business.** None.

**Workshop Item 1 -- Husky Park Forest Easement Correction.** Zack Kershner, Chief Operating Officer, addressed the Council to correct an error in his August 27, 2026 presentation concerning a forest conservation easement at Husky Park. No vote associated.

**Workshop Item 2 -- PILOT Presentation.** Jodie Ostoich, Housing Policy Advisor, presented on Payment in Lieu of Taxes (PILOT) programs, the City's implementation history, and PILOT policy requirements. Liz Marsh and Caitlin Gossens of Standard Communities provided additional information on the Wormans Mill Court project PILOT proposal. No public comments were received. No vote associated.

**Workshop Item 3 -- Affordable Housing Action Resolution.** Council President Díaz presented a resolution on a commitment to action on affordable housing, referencing the City's Housing Task Force Report and the Frederick County Housing Study. He stated many residents have expressed concern that past studies have not translated into action. Council discussion included a desire to identify housing as critical infrastructure and concern about the limited number of housing permits issued in recent years by Frederick County. Public comments were received (not itemized in the official record). No vote associated -- discussion only.

**Workshop Item 4 -- Maryland Housing Certainty Act Implementation (G-26-11).** City Attorney Rachel Gray reviewed proposed resolution G-26-11 to implement the Maryland Housing Certainty Act, effective October 21, 2026, and the main provisions of the act. She noted the ordinance keeps the City in compliance until City law is revised. No public comments were received. No vote associated.

**Elected Officials' Comments.**
- Council member Brehm: met with the Children of Incarcerated Parents Partnership; announced a fundraiser September 29 at McClintock Distillery.
- Vice President Nash: reminded the Council that the next day marked the 25th anniversary of the September 11 attacks.
- Council member Hempel Irani: the Office of Arts and Culture is developing a cultural strategic plan and directed residents to a City website survey; also noted the City time capsule burial that weekend as part of In the Streets weekend.
- Council President Díaz: spoke about recent ICE enforcement activity in the City, expressed gratitude to community members, Council colleagues, the state delegation, the Frederick Police Department, and his son Ricky; specifically thanked Vice President Nash for her commitment and advocacy for immigrant neighbors.

General public comment was received (not itemized in the official record).

## Action Items / Open Questions

No item from this session required a Notion Legislative Roadmap status change beyond what was already tracked: Item 3 (Affordable Housing Action Resolution) remained pre-introduction and Item 4 (G-26-11) remained pending its Second Read, both consistent with prior status. G-26-11 subsequently passed 5-0 at the September 17, 2026 Regular Public Meeting, per [[Cesar Context -- Council]] -- that vote is not part of this record and should be confirmed against that meeting's own official minutes when available.

The official record does not itemize the substance of public comments received on Item 3, only that comments were received -- worth asking staff or checking the Granicus recording if the content matters later.
