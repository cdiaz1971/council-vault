# Workshop Minutes 2026-10-08

## Summary

City Council Work Session called to order at 6:01 PM by Council President Díaz, adjourned at 7:58 PM. Present: Díaz, Shackelford, Brehm, Adkins, Hempel Irani. Absent: Vice President Nash and Council Member Taylor. Three workshop presentations, no legislative votes.

## Outcomes

**Minutes.** On a motion by Brehm, seconded by Hempel Irani, the Council approved 5-0 the minutes of the September 10, 2026 work session (as recorded; see open questions).

**County Transit presentation.** Victoria Venable (Frederick County Director of Government Relations and Strategic Partnerships) and Roman Steichen (Director of Transit Services) covered service area, funding, growth, planning, and public feedback. 34 bus shelters, 25 in the City. The 2030 census may move Frederick County into the large urban transit system category. No public comment.

**Bond financing.** CAO Katie Barkdoll explained bond financing, debt service, and bonds versus PayGo, and noted the City's strong bond rating. A bond ordinance was to be presented the following week, with a December market date proposed. No public comment.

**Proposed data center moratorium.** Council Member Shackelford led discussion of a two-year moratorium so the City can gather studies before deciding on data center development, noting the LMC has no data center regulations. Public comments were received.

**Elected officials' comments.** Hempel Irani noted the Hispanic Heritage event the evening before and the lobby artwork. Díaz thanked her and Brehm for attending.

## Action Items / Open Questions

The agenda for this meeting listed approval of the September 24 minutes, but the official minutes record approval of the September 10 minutes. Ask the Clerk to correct the October 8 minutes before they are approved on October 22, or confirm which minutes were actually approved.

The minutes record no substance from the data center moratorium discussion or public comment beyond the fact that comments were received.
