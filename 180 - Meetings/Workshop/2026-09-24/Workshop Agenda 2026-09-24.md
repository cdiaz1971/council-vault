**IV. Workshop**

**A. SPARK Tool Overview**
Presented by Ericka Rohrbaugh (Senior Advisor for Opportunity & Innovation) and Lacey Gordon (Manager of Budget). SPARK ("Strategic Priority Alignment & Resource Kickoff") asks each Council member to allocate 100 points across the six CommUNITY 2030 strategic goals and their objectives to signal relative priority for the FY2028 budget cycle. Explicitly not a budget vote or department ranking. Timeline: overview and SPARK worksheet sent out tonight, allocations due back October 9, staff aggregates and scores October 9-21, results return to Council October 22.
- A.1 CommUNITY 2030 strategic plan (background reference, December 2019)
- A.2 SPARK presentation deck

**B. Discussion of Real Property Taxes**
Submitted by Katie Barkdoll, Chief Administrative Officer. Discussion-only review of alternative property tax structures against the current uniform $0.7055/$100 rate: separate residential/nonresidential rates, a tiered rate by assessed value, and a Land Value Tax (pure or 2:1 hybrid split-rate). Preliminary parcel-level modeling shows distributional effects that vary significantly by neighborhood. No staff recommendation; no next steps stated.
- B.1 Executive Summary
- B.2 Real Property Tax presentation deck

**C. Review and Discussion of a Proposed Text Amendment Concerning Final Approval of Major Site Plans**
Submitted by Council Member Peter Brehm. Draft LMC text amendment (Section 309) that would let Council vote, within 60 days of a major site plan application's submission, to assume final reviewing authority over that application (currently a Planning Commission function), with a 30-day Council decision clock once it does. Presented for initial Council feedback before Brehm submits it to the Planning Commission for formal review. Not yet introduced.
- C.1 Executive Summary
- C.2 Draft LMC text amendment language (dated 2026-08-17)
