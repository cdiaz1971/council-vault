# Closed Session Minutes 2026-09-03

**Closed Session -- Thursday, September 3, 2026, 5:30 PM**

## Summary

The Council convened in closed session on a 5-0 vote to discuss the promotion of a Police Department candidate to Captain and Deputy Chief, under the personnel exception (appointment, employment, promotion, etc. of an appointee or employee). Chief Kevin Meyer briefed the Council on Acting Captain John Corbett's qualifications and the resulting vacancy backfill process. The Council approved the promotion 6-0. Meeting adjourned 5:41 PM.

## Outcomes

**Attendance.** Council President Díaz, Vice President Nash, and Council members Brehm, Adkins, and Hempel Irani present for the vote to convene (5-0). Council member Shackelford was absent for that vote but participated in the closed session in its entirety. Council member Taylor was absent from the meeting. Also present: Rachel Gray (City Attorney), Phyllis Hane (City Clerk), Chief Kevin Meyer (Frederick Police Department).

**Vote to convene.** On a motion by Nash, seconded by Adkins, approved 5-0 (Díaz, Nash, Brehm, Adkins, Hempel Irani in favor; Shackelford and Taylor not present for the vote). Stated reason: to candidly discuss the qualifications of a candidate for promotion within the Frederick Police Department.

**Discussion.** Chief Meyer requested approval to promote Acting Captain John Corbett to Captain and Deputy Chief, noting Corbett is the second most tenured member of the command staff, and reviewed his supervisory experience. In response to a question from Council President Díaz, Chief Meyer reviewed the promotion process and how the vacancy created by his own appointment to Chief would be filled.

**Vote.** On a motion by Nash, seconded by Brehm, the Council approved the promotion of Acting Captain John Corbett to Captain and Deputy Chief, 6-0.

Meeting adjourned 5:41 PM.

## Action Items / Open Questions

None identified. The promotion is final and the substance (unlike many closed-session items) is on the public record in full.
