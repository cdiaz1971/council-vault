# Closed Session Minutes 2026-08-06

**Closed Session -- Thursday, August 6, 2026, 5:30 PM**

## Summary

The Council convened in closed session on a 6-0 vote (motion by Brehm, seconded by Adkins) to discuss cybersecurity matters, on the stated basis that public discussion would risk exposing security assessments, deployments, or network security information related to critical infrastructure or security devices. The stated purpose was to protect operational technology network information and mitigate exploitation of network vulnerabilities by threat actors. Two technology purchases tied to this were approved. Meeting adjourned at 5:39 PM.

## Outcomes

**Attendance.** Council President Díaz, Vice President Nash, and Council members Shackelford, Brehm, Adkins, and Hempel Irani present. Council member Taylor absent.

**Item 1.** Others present: Rachel Gray (City Attorney), Phyllis Hane (City Clerk), Matt Bowman (Chief Technology Officer). On a motion by Brehm, seconded by Hempel Irani, the Council approved the requested purchase 6-0.

**Item 2.** Others present: Rachel Nessen (City Attorney), Phyllis Hane (City Clerk), Matt Bowman (Chief Technology Officer). On a motion by Shackelford, seconded by Brehm, the Council approved the requested purchase 5-0-1, Nash abstaining.

Neither item's substance (vendor, amount, scope) is disclosed in the public closed-session summary -- only the vote outcome and stated legal basis for closure are on record.
