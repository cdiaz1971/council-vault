# Community Bridge -- Closed Session Questions -- 2026-04-16

## Summary

Questions for the April 16, 2026 closed session on the Community Bridge Restoration Project. The session will address contract performance, litigation exposure, the tolling agreement, and whether to appropriate additional funds.

## Questions

**On contract performance and payments**

- Cochran has received $152,368.75 under the 2022 Agreement but painted nothing under it. What specifically triggered each payment after the initial 18% mobilization fee, and does staff contend those payments were proper?
- The 2021 Purchase Order was for 8 panels at $48,200; Cochran submitted his final invoice in July 2025. Why did it take until mid-2025 to complete 8 panels authorized in December 2021?

**On the City's own delays**

- The City hired Tom King of Noelker and Hall in August 2024, nearly two years into the 2022 Agreement. What caused that delay, and does staff acknowledge it contributed to any legitimate portion of Cochran's delay claim?
- Has anyone prepared a side-by-side timeline of City obligations versus City performance? If not, why not, and can legal produce one before any settlement or termination decision?

**On the technical prerequisites dispute**

- The scribing cost was "not anticipated" by staff. Who is responsible for that gap in the original scope, and what is the projected cost?
- The City has final authority on material selection under the contract. Has the City formally rejected Cochran's gammastone demand in writing, and if not, why not?

**On the tolling agreement**

- What are the specific terms? How long does it toll, and what are each party's obligations during the tolling period?
- Does legal believe the City has viable counterclaims, and how do they assess the strength of those counterclaims relative to the $692K delay damages claim?

**On termination**

- Has the City issued any formal notice of default to Cochran under Article IX of the 2022 Agreement? If not, why not, and does failing to do so affect the City's litigation posture?
- If the City terminates, what is legal's honest assessment of total litigation exposure, including the $692K delay claim and the inflation adjustment demand?

**On the public-private partnership**

- Which institutions have expressed interest, and at what funding levels?
- What authority does the Mayor's office have to negotiate and commit the City to partnership terms without a Council-approved framework?

**On the Kevin Meyer item**

- Is this a routine extension or is there a timeline for a permanent appointment process? If the acting appointment has been in place for an extended period, when can the Council expect a permanent selection?
