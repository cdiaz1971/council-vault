## VII. Consent Agenda

**A. Special Ordinance**
1. S-26-21 -- amends the FY2027 Budget to include revenue and expenditures for a Governor's Office of Crime Prevention and Policy (GOCPP) Byrne JAG grant, $29,575, for a vehicle forensics kit, training, and Pix4D reconstruction software for FPD.

## VIII. Public Hearing

**A. Purchases**
1. Purchase order of $65,151.00 for B2Gnow contract compliance software implementation.
2. Three-year software services and license agreement with ForceMetrics for law enforcement related technology.
3. Amendment 1 to Task Order 1 with Delta Airport Consultants for additional construction administration services for the south apron project (+$215,000, new total $515,000).
4. Solar Feasibility Study at Frederick Municipal Airport:
   a. Special Ordinance (S-26-22) amending the budget for Maryland Energy Administration Local Government Energy Modernization grant funds.
   b. Contract and purchase order with McClure Engineering, $53,476.00.

**B. Agreements**
1. Lease with Cabana Air II, LLC for construction of a 33,000 square foot aircraft hangar at Frederick Municipal Airport (the lease document itself says approximately 30,000 square feet).

**C. Resolutions**
None.

**D. Ordinances**
1. Second read and approval of G-26-12 -- amends LMC Section 829 (Home Occupations) and Section 1002 (Definitions).
2. Second read and approval of G-26-13 -- amends LMC Section 301 (General Procedural Requirements), Section 721 (Forest Conservation), and Section 1002 (Definitions).
3. Second read and approval of G-26-14 -- amends the annual fee schedule to add a fee paid into the Forest Conservation Fund in lieu of afforestation.

Introduction only, no Council discussion or public comment:
4. G-26-15 -- authorizes the City to issue and sell bonds in an amount not to exceed $39,000,000.
5. G-26-16 -- moratorium on considering the creation of land use categories for data centers and other critical data infrastructure facilities, and authorizing a study of their impacts.
