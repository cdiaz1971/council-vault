# ForceMetrics Questions -- October 15, 2026

## Chief Meyer / Lt. Borns
- [ ] What problem does this solve that FPD can't handle today? A concrete case example.
- [ ] Which agencies would be listed in the data-sharing addendum (§ 28)?
- [ ] Will LPR data keep current limits: six users, case number per search, 30-day retention, no automatic sharing?
- [ ] Will booking photos be used for facial recognition or matching? Will you accept a written ban?
- [ ] Who sees the "mental health or domestic" flags, and what's the guidance on using them?
- [ ] Is there a written policy keeping this data from civil immigration enforcement?
- [ ] Which budget line pays for it?

## Rachel Gray
- [ ] Does feeding LPR data to this vendor comply with Public Safety § 3-509?
- [ ] Does § 18 (users may share with any law enforcement) conflict with the City Values Act judicial warrant rule?
- [ ] Can we strike § 8 (Public Data "for any purpose") and § 21 (vendor owns derived data)?
- [ ] Is the $2,000 per-claim confidentiality liability cap acceptable for police data?

## Matt Bowman
- [ ] What did your review of the LPR, citation, and JMS booking photo integrations find? Why no IT sign-off?
- [ ] Can the City review or disable new AI features before the vendor turns them on?

## Finance / Purchasing
- [ ] How was this procured: bid, sole source, or cooperative contract?
- [ ] Why is fiscal impact blank? Is the escalator 3% or 5%?

## Deciding question
Will the Chief commit in writing to the immigration exclusion and a facial recognition ban? If yes, approve with conditions is realistic. If no, postponement is easier to defend.
