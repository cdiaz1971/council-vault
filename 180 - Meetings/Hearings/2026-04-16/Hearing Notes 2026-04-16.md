# Hearing Notes 2026-04-16

## Summary

Regular public meeting of the Frederick City Council, April 16, 2026. Relatively light agenda. The substantive business centered on three Lucas Village redevelopment items, all of which passed. Routine consent items also cleared without issue.

## Outcomes

**Personnel Board and grant budget amendment** -- The official minutes confirm three Personnel Board appointments/reappointments, each 7-0. S-26-4 accepted Maryland Department of Health Office of Oral Health grant funds into the FY2026 budget, 6-0-1, with Taylor recused. The original personal capture described a fluoride-pump purchase and attributed her nonparticipation to employment with the Department; the official minutes establish the grant amendment and recusal, without that purchase description or explanation.

**Lucas Village -- Parkland dedication waiver** -- Approved the waiver of the parkland requirement on the basis that the development is proximate to existing parks and will provide alternative green space. The tradeoff enables additional affordable housing units. Passed 7-0.

**Lucas Village -- Right-of-way vacation and land donation** -- These were separate votes. Resolution 26-7 vacating portions of Vermont Court and Pennsylvania Avenue passed 7-0. The land donation agreement passed 6-1, Nash opposed, with the settlement date to be determined before execution. The original personal notes attributed her dissent to property-disposition concerns; the official minutes do not state a reason.
