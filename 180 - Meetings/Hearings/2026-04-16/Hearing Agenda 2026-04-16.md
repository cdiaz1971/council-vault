# Council Meeting -- Regular Public Meeting 2026-04-16

## Summary

Regular public meeting of the Frederick City Council, April 16, 2026, 6:00 PM, City Hall. The meeting is dominated by three connected items enabling the Lucas Village LIHTC redevelopment (a land donation, a right-of-way vacation, and a parkland dedication waiver), all carrying Planning Commission support. Other business includes routine board appointments, six alcohol permit consents, a minor grant budget amendment, and a Bee City USA designation.

## Action Items

**Donation Agreement -- Lucas Village SWM Facility (IX.B.1)**
Authorizes the City to transfer a 1.2-acre City-owned parcel (Parkland 11, Tax Map 77C, Parcel 1239A, located between Sagner Avenue and Vermont Court) to the Housing Authority of the City of Frederick (HACF) for nominal consideration of $1.00. The parcel has functioned informally as an unlined stormwater holding area contributing to sinkhole activity. HACF will design and build a code-compliant stormwater management facility at its own cost as part of the Lucas Village redevelopment. The deed includes a reversionary clause: if the New SWM Facility construction is not commenced within three years of the effective date, the parcel reverts to the City. The overall Lucas Village project produces 221 affordable apartment homes at 60% AMI under LIHTC financing, in partnership with New Harbor Development. Settlement deadline is May 2026.

Submitted by Gabrielle Collard (Planning). CFO Barkdoll and City Attorney signed off. Staff recommends approval. Introduced and heard same day -- confirm City Code notice compliance with Rachel Gray before gavel.

**Resolution 26-7 -- Right-of-Way Vacation (IX.C.1)**
Vacates portions of Vermont Court and Pennsylvania Avenue rights-of-way (three areas totaling approximately 0.37 acres) to enable road realignment within the Lucas Village site. Vacated areas revert to HACF and are incorporated into the development footprint. New public streets will be dedicated in their place, so no net reduction in City street maintenance responsibility. Planning Commission voted 3-0 in favor on August 11, 2025. Introduced by Council President Díaz.

**Resolution 26-8 -- Parkland Dedication Waiver (IX.C.2)**
Waives the LMC Section 608 parkland dedication requirement for Lucas Village. Project qualifies as infill development, reducing the standard 1,000 sq ft/unit requirement to 500 sq ft/unit, producing a 1.5-acre dedication requirement for the 130 net new units (222 total minus 92 existing). Parks and Recreation Commission recommended waiver May 13, 2025; Planning Commission recommended waiver August 11, 2025. Staff analysis cites Lucas Village Park and Grove Park as nearby, plus 0.78 acres of on-site amenity space (entry plaza, corner pocket park, art walk). Fee-in-lieu alternative would have been approximately $130,000 at current rates, which is structurally low relative to infill land values -- flag for LMC modernization work. See [[Frederick Housing Policy Baseline]] for MPDU and LMC fee structure context.

**Resolution 26-9 -- Bee City USA Designation (IX.C.3)**
Designates Frederick as a Bee City USA affiliate. Sustainability Department serves as sponsor; Bee Together Frederick subcommittee (already established under the Sustainability Committee) is the implementing body. Requires at least one annual educational event, a website presence, a native plant list, and an IPM plan within two years (draft already in progress, expected completion June 2026). Annual renewal required each February with a population-based fee. Introduced by Council President Díaz on behalf of the administration.

**Special Ordinance S-26-4 -- FY2026 Budget Amendment (VIII.C.1, Consent)**
Amends FY2026 Water and Sewer Fund budget to receive $3,500 federal pass-through grant from Maryland Department of Health Office of Oral Health for a replacement benchtop fluoride meter at the water treatment facility. Budget-neutral. Submitted by Robert Rosenberg; Tracy Coleman (PW), CFO Barkdoll, and City Attorney signed off. The ordinance background recites that the operative FY2026 budget is the Mayor's proposed budget due to the failed Council override of the Mayor's veto of S-25-09 in June 2025.
