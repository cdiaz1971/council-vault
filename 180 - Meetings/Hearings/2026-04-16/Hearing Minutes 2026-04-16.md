# Hearing Minutes 2026-04-16

## Summary

Regular meeting at City Hall, 6:00-7:14 p.m., with Díaz presiding and all seven Council members present; Rachel Gray and Phyllis Hane also attended. Three Lucas Village actions were approved, with Nash opposing only the land donation. Taylor recused from the Maryland Department of Health grant budget amendment. The PDF includes the preceding closed-session summary on page 5.

## Outcomes

**Prior minutes and appointments.** April 2 minutes were approved 7-0 (Nash/Adkins). Bethany Lord and Chris May were reappointed to the Personnel Board, and Marmady Kamara appointed as alternate, for April 17, 2026-April 16, 2029; each passed 7-0 (Nash/Taylor). Matt Hueting's appointment as C. Burr Artz Trust trustee passed 7-0 (Brehm/Hempel Irani).

**Consent: surety and alcohol requests.** The $129,002.64 Ogden Newspapers/Frederick News Post Business Center cash surety release passed 7-0 (Nash/Brehm). Six alcohol requests passed together 7-0 (Brehm/Adkins): Asia on the Creek, In the Streets, Frederick's 4th, Christ Reformed Evangelical Church's picnic, Alive@5, and the Gran Fondo Maryland finish celebration. Event dates and locations remain in the source, page 2.

**S-26-4: grant budget amendment.** Receipt of Maryland Department of Health Office of Oral Health grant funds into the FY2026 budget was approved 6-0-1 (Brehm/Adkins). Díaz, Nash, Shackelford, Brehm, Adkins, and Hempel Irani voted yes; Taylor recused. The minutes describe a grant-receipt budget amendment, not a fluoride-pump purchase authorization.

**Lucas Village land donation.** The donation agreement with the Housing Authority for Lot 2 New AREA passed 6-1 (Adkins/Shackelford), with Díaz, Shackelford, Taylor, Brehm, Adkins, and Hempel Irani in favor and Nash opposed. Approval included a stipulation that the settlement date be determined before execution. The minutes do not state Nash's reason for dissent.

**Lucas Village right-of-way vacation.** Resolution 26-7 concerning parts of Vermont Court and Pennsylvania Avenue passed 7-0 (Brehm/Taylor).

**Lucas Village parkland dedication waiver.** The Section 608 waiver passed 7-0 (Hempel Irani/Adkins). The official minutes print the resolution number as 27-8; that identifier is flagged below rather than silently changed.

**Bee City USA.** Resolution 26-9 designating Frederick as an affiliate passed 7-0 (Brehm/Nash).

**Other business.** A Month of the Young Child proclamation was presented. Díaz announced Donna Kuzemchak's hiring as Constituent Services Coordinator. No ordinances were considered.
