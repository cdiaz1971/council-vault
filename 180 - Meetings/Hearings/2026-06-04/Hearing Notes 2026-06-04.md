# Hearing Notes 2026-06-04

## Summary

Regular Public Meeting, June 4, 2026. Six members present; Adkins absent. The most consequential outcome was the 6-0 failure of G-26-4 (LMC Mixed Use Phasing amendment). Nash put on the record that text amendments should be council-led -- a significant signal for how LMC work proceeds. The pension resolutions were tabled rather than voted on, by 5-0-1, with Shackelford abstaining, and returned to committee. All five consent items passed 6-0 without issue. G-26-3 (Vehicles for Hire) was introduced without substantive vote.

---

## Outcomes

**G-26-4: LMC Amendment, Mixed Use Phasing -- FAILED 6-0**
The ordinance amending LMC Sections 417 and 310 to replace the rigid 75/25 phasing standard with a developer-proposed schedule failed 6-0 with Adkins absent. Nash stated during proceedings that text amendments should be council-led. This is a meaningful political signal: any future LMC text amendments should originate from the council, not from administration or developer applicants, if they are to have a viable path. The Planning Commission had already recommended denial 4-0 in September 2025.

**Resolutions 26-11 and 26-12: Part-Time Employee Pension Restoration -- TABLED**
The official minutes confirm a 5-0-1 vote to table and return the item to committee: Díaz, Nash, Taylor, Brehm, and Hempel Irani favored tabling; Shackelford abstained. No vote on the pension amendments themselves occurred. The original personal capture reported conflicting counts and anticipated the next regular meeting; the official record does not promise that return date. Resolution numbers and the original equity/fiscal assessment are personal briefing context, not findings supplied by these minutes.

**G-26-3: Vehicles for Hire Ordinance (Taxicab Code Overhaul) -- INTRODUCED**
