# Hearing Minutes 2026-06-04

## Summary

Regular meeting at City Hall, 6:00-7:38 p.m., with Díaz presiding. Díaz, Nash, Shackelford, Taylor, Brehm, and Hempel Irani attended; Adkins was absent. Rachel Gray and Phyllis Hane also attended. G-26-4 was denied 6-0; pension amendments were tabled and returned to committee 5-0-1; G-26-3 was introduced. The PDF includes the preceding cybersecurity closed-session summary on page 6.

## Outcomes

**Prior minutes and appointments.** May 7 minutes passed 6-0 (Shackelford/Brehm). Stuart Harvey's appointment and Marc Mitchell's reappointment to the Board of Supervisors of Elections each passed 6-0 on the same mover/seconder, for June 5, 2026-May 31, 2030.

**Consent alcohol requests.** Five requests passed together 6-0 (Taylor/Brehm): the Carrollton Park family reunion, Snallyfest, Carroll Creek Live, Black Frederick Festival, and June 21 City Market Square Lot alcohol consumption.

**Playgrounds.** Playground Specialists purchases under HGAC contract PR11-20 passed separately 6-0: Staley Park, $173,429 (Hempel Irani/Taylor); Willowdale Park, $101,513 (Nash/Taylor).

**Levee certification.** Bayland Consultants & Designers' FEMA levee design-services contract, CIP 350024, with costs not exceeding $395,000, passed 6-0 (Brehm/Taylor).

**Vehicles.** Five replacement-vehicle purchase orders totaling $354,084.98 using vehicle contingency funds passed 6-0 (Brehm/Hempel Irani).

**Street and traffic-sign inventory.** Use of Montgomery County contract 1152448 pricing and an O'Connell & Lawrence purchase order not exceeding $150,000 passed 6-0 (Hempel Irani/Brehm).

**Police firing-range targets.** S-26-11 amending the FY2026 budget to receive FBI reimbursement passed 6-0 (Brehm/Nash). A separate $192,800 purchase order for the turning-target system also passed 6-0 (Brehm/Nash).

**Pension amendments.** The 25- and 30-year pension-plan amendment item was tabled and returned to committee for further discussion, 5-0-1 (Nash/Brehm). Díaz, Nash, Taylor, Brehm, and Hempel Irani favored tabling; Shackelford abstained. There was no adoption vote on the pension amendments. The minutes do not provide the resolution numbers.

**Winpenny Drive.** Díaz announced receipt of a petition to annex approximately 9.08 acres north of Winpenny Drive with requested R4 zoning; no approval vote is recorded.

**G-26-3: vehicles for hire.** Díaz introduced an ordinance removing marijuana from the specified illegal-drug list for taxicab-driver testing, eliminating the permit cap, reducing fees, extending permit validity to two years, and extending the specified-crime lookback from two to four years. Introduction only.

**G-26-4: mixed-use phasing.** On Brehm's motion, seconded by Taylor, the Council denied the proposed amendments to LMC Section 310 (Master Plans) and Section 417(d) (Mixed Use Districts/Phasing), 6-0.

**Other business.** A Pride Month proclamation was presented. Council comments addressed Pride, community trust, and rat-control authority; no separate formal action is recorded on these comments.
