# Hearing Minutes 2026-03-19

## Summary

Regular meeting at City Hall, 6:00-6:57 p.m., with César Díaz presiding and all seven Council members present. Rachel Gray and Phyllis Hane also attended. G-26-1 was introduced without a substantive vote; the Council approved the municipal revenue study resolution and actuarial services agreement, while the pension amendments were removed before the meeting.

## Outcomes

**Prior minutes.** Taylor moved and Adkins seconded approval of the March 5 regular meeting and February 19 closed-session minutes; approved 7-0.

**Appointments.** Luis Montes was appointed to the Planning Commission for March 20, 2026-March 19, 2031 (Taylor/Shackelford; 7-0). David Firman, Gayle Petersen, and Denise Sparks were reappointed as Neighborhood Advisory Council coordinators for March 20, 2026-March 19, 2028 (Nash/Brehm; 7-0). Stephen Colar was appointed to the Zoning Board of Appeals for March 20, 2026-March 19, 2029 (Taylor/Nash; 7-0).

**Ethics Commission reappointment.** Hempel Irani moved, Taylor seconded, and the Council approved tabling Kevin Lollar's reappointment. The official document's vote count and member list are internally inconsistent; no reconstructed count is recorded here. See the source issue below.

**Council liaisons.** Brehm moved and Taylor seconded the following appointments, approved together 7-0: Brehm to the Frederick Community Action Board of Directors; Hempel Irani to the Public Art Commission and Containment Laboratory Community Advisory Committee; Adkins to the Planning Commission; and Shackelford to the Weinberg Board of Directors.

**Surety release.** Adkins moved and Nash seconded release of the $48,127.50 Guarantee Company of North America bond posted by Wormald for 4.2121 acres of off-site afforestation at Worman's Mill/Mill Island; approved 7-0.

**Actuarial services.** An agreement with Bolton Partners Inc. was approved 7-0 (Brehm/Taylor).

**Pension plans.** Proposed Resolutions 26-3 and 26-4, amending the 25- and 30-year pension plans, were pulled from the agenda before the meeting. No vote on those resolutions occurred.

**Municipal revenue.** Resolution 26-5 supporting a State task force to study municipal revenue sources was approved 7-0 (Hempel Irani/Brehm).

**City values and immigration enforcement.** Díaz introduced G-26-1. Introduction only: the minutes record no adoption vote and state that a second reading will occur at a later public hearing.

**Traffic calming.** Traffic Engineer II Shraddha Praharaj presented an update and public comments were received; no Council action is recorded.
