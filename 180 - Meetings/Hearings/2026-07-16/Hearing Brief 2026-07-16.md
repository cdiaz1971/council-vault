### VIII.A.1 -- DeVere Insulation Home Performance Contract (Weatherization)

Submitted by Michele Ott, HHS. Director: Stuart Campbell.

Piggyback contract onto State of Maryland DHCD contract BPM037707 (State Weatherization Contractors, executed January 13, 2025). Three-year initial term with one-year extension option at City discretion. The City leverages DHCD procurement authority rather than running its own competitive bid.

Scope: HVAC repair and replacement, furnace cleaning, appliance replacement, insulation, retrofit lighting, and energy conservation materials for income-eligible households. Work assigned as needed based on pricing and availability.

Fiscal impact: fully grant-funded through FY2027. No general fund exposure.

Legal review: Iris Annan (I.A.), 7/9/2026. Budget review: Katie Barkdoll (K.B.), 7/10/2026. Standard departmental concurrences are blank on the cover sheet -- routine for a piggybacked HHS contract with no capital impact.

Equity note: income-eligible households in lower-income corridors are the primary beneficiaries. This is the type of leveraged state-contract delivery that directly serves District 2 constituents. Supports Strategic Goal SG2 (Social Well-Being). Vote yes.

### VIII.B.1 -- MSD School Resource Officer MOU

Submitted by LT John Corbett, Police Department.

Continuation of the SRO arrangement at the Maryland School for the Deaf (Frederick campus) dating to 2019. Currently filled by MPO Margery Lee and facility dog Fred.

Key terms: MSD-F reimburses the City $133,077 due no later than December 1, 2026, covering salary, benefits, uniform, safety equipment, training, and communications equipment. MSD-F also reimburses on agreed cycles for vehicle replacement (7 years), body armor (5 years), and communications upgrades. SRO works four 10-hour shifts per week, schedule set in collaboration between the MSD Superintendent and the SRO.

FPD retains full supervisory and disciplinary authority. MSD-F has a mechanism to request reassignment if dissatisfied. Overtime above the base is absorbed by the City. Absence coverage: FPD provides school safety checks until return; substitute SRO assigned beginning day six if available.

Term: September 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027. Either party may cancel on 60 days written notice for budgetary reasons. Parties intend to negotiate a new agreement for FY28 covering the September 1, 2027 start.

Fiscal impact: cost-neutral to the City. MSD-F is paying full cost of the position.

Equity note: this is parity with what the City provides to public and charter schools, extended to a state institution serving a disability-identified population. Supports Strategic Plan initiatives 2.05 and 3.02. Vote yes.

### VIII.C.4 -- Introduction of Ordinance G-26-6 (Rodent Control)

Nash's bill. First read introduction only. No ordinance text is included in the packet.

Tonight's action is introduction only -- no vote on substance. The text will circulate before any public hearing.

Known risks to flag as the draft develops:

Interior-access limitation: any enforcement mechanism that requires access to dwelling interiors to inspect for rodents will face practical and legal friction. Landlords have limited right-of-entry under state law and tenant consent issues are real.

Tenant liability exposure: depending on how the ordinance defines responsibility for infestation abatement, tenants in lower-income units could face penalties or costs for conditions caused by building maintenance failures. Watch for any language in Section 12.5-92 that shifts burden to occupants.

COMAR 15.05 preemption: Maryland's agricultural and pest control regulations under COMAR 15.05 have preemption implications for local rodent control ordinances. Rachel Gray's review should specifically address this before the ordinance goes to public hearing.

Chapter 12.5 namespace collision: this bill, like the Real Rent Ordinance, is drafted to add Chapter 12.5, Article VI. If both are advancing simultaneously, there is a direct code collision that must be resolved with Rachel Gray and the Clerk before either ordinance is enrolled. Flag this now.

No action required tonight beyond recording the introduction.
