# Hearing Minutes 2026-07-16

## Summary

Regular meeting at City Hall, 6:00-10:27 p.m., with Díaz presiding and Shackelford, Taylor, Brehm, Adkins, and Hempel Irani present. Nash is omitted from the regular-meeting attendance list and explicitly absent in the bundled closed-session summary; all regular votes were 6-0. Rachel Gray and Phyllis Hane also attended. The Council introduced Gateway rezoning G-26-7 and rodent-control G-26-6, approved proposed Gateway rezoning conditions, and continued the related annexation-resolution item to August 6.

## Outcomes

**Prior minutes and surety.** July 2 minutes passed 6-0 (Taylor/Brehm). Release of Habitat for Humanity's $119,311.55 letter of credit for Phase 1 of the West All Saints Street project passed 6-0 (Adkins/Brehm).

**Weatherization.** A DeVere Insulation Home Performance services contract for eligible households passed 6-0 (Brehm/Taylor).

**School resource officers.** An MOU continuing SRO services at Maryland School for the Deaf passed 6-0 (Adkins/Brehm).

**Gateway rezoning: introduction.** Díaz introduced G-26-7 to rezone approximately 24.33 acres from General Commercial to Institutional for a special-events center. No adoption vote on the rezoning ordinance occurred; it was to return at a future public meeting.

**Gateway conditions.** Shackelford moved and Hempel Irani seconded approval of the conditions, 6-0. The applicant was to consider them and respond. The approved condition set, summarized from pages 2-4, provides:

1. An Events Management Plan for all events, scaled to attendance; all specified components apply when attendance is reasonably expected to exceed 1,500. It must cover parking, access, MD 144 pedestrian protection, towing/prohibited parking, security and emergency services, temporary utilities, attention-getting equipment, compliance measures, circulation/shuttle sketches, and owner payment of additional public-service costs. Consultation includes willing City, County, law-enforcement, HOA, owner, and SHA stakeholders. The COO approves before unconditional final site-plan approval, with annual updates by April 1 and the same process for revisions; the term includes a designee or Mayor-designated employee.
2. A subsequently established Events Management Commission may take on specified COO or stakeholder functions.
3. Special-events vehicles cannot use the SHA Park and Ride without express SHA approval.
4. Professional cricket matches are capped at 15 in the first calendar year; increases in later years require COO approval after stakeholder consultation.
5. Non-cricket events expected to exceed 1,500 attendance are capped at 10 in the first operating year; increases require COO approval after consultation.
6. Field fertilizer and chemical use must comply with the Maryland Fertilizer Use Act.
7. Stormwater management must comply with applicable State/local requirements.
8. An MDE Nontidal Wetlands and Waterways Permit is required before public access/use for construction in the 100-year floodplain; if not issued, relocate outside the floodplain or the project does not proceed.
9. Aerial fireworks, drones, lasers, and bright upward lighting require prior Airport Manager approval and are limited because of aviation hazards.
10. Users must receive airport-overlay disclosures about continuous aircraft operations, noise, and emissions.
11. Events must meet City noise requirements; the applicant provides on-site sound-monitoring equipment for police enforcement.
12. Events must meet City lighting requirements.
13. Site-plan review must address ingress/egress and minimizing disruption associated with the Park and Ride.
14. Development must comply with APFO requirements, including traffic.
15. The cricket field must accommodate at least two additional playing fields for other sports.
16. Events expected to have at least 10,000 people at one time must meet City large-private-event requirements.
17. Cricket matches and other events cannot be scheduled beyond 11:00 p.m.

These are a source summary; use the PDF's full condition text for drafting or compliance decisions. Approval of conditions is distinct from adoption of G-26-7.

**Gateway annexation-resolution amendment.** Consideration of the resolution amending Resolution 09-23, which annexed approximately 52 acres of Gateway property, was continued to August 6, 2026, by a 6-0 vote (Brehm/Shackelford).

**Rodent control.** Díaz introduced G-26-6; no adoption vote is recorded.
