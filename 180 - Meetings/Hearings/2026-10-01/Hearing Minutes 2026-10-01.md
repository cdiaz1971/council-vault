# Hearing Minutes 2026-10-01

**Regular Public Meeting -- October 1, 2026 at 6:00 PM, City Hall**

## Summary

Official minutes of the October 1, 2026 Regular Public Meeting, released by the City Clerk and included as backup material in the October 15 packet. Council President Díaz presided with Vice President Nash and Council members Shackelford, Brehm, Adkins, and Hempel Irani present; Council member Taylor was absent. City Attorney Rachel Gray and City Clerk Phyllis Hane also attended. The dominant business was Resolution 26-15, which passed 6-0 with no public comment. Nine board and commission items were handled, the Joyfield PILOT was pulled before the meeting, and three LMC and fee ordinances were introduced for October 15 second reads. Adjourned at 6:49 PM.

## Outcomes

**Approval of September 17 minutes.** Approved 5-0 (Brehm/Nash), with Taylor and Hempel Irani not present for the vote. Hempel Irani is listed as present at the meeting and moved or seconded later items, so she appears to have arrived after this vote.

**Appointments and reappointments.** All approved 6-0:
- Melissa Cooper, Fair Housing Commission, October 2, 2026 to October 1, 2029 (Brehm/Adkins)
- Lee Hand, Zoning Board of Appeals reappointment, October 2, 2026 to October 1, 2029 (Brehm/Adkins)
- Kathryn Stewart, Planning Commission, unexpired term ending September 4, 2030 (Brehm/Adkins)
- Rob Fox, Parks and Recreation Commission reappointment, October 2, 2026 to October 1, 2029 (Shackelford/Brehm)
- John Cetrone, Neighborhood Advisory Council 3 Coordinator, October 2, 2026 to October 1, 2028 (Brehm/Hempel Irani)
- Marlene Barge, Weinberg Center Board, October 2, 2026 to June 30, 2029 (Brehm/Nash)
- Shuan Butcher, Weinberg Center Board, same term (Hempel Irani/Brehm)
- Chris Parsell, Weinberg Center Board, same term (Shackelford/Brehm)

**Ethics Commission, Philip Bowers.** Tabled 6-0 on a motion by Vice President Nash, seconded by Brehm.

**Consent agenda.** Release of $21,000 cash surety posted by FCSCTF, Inc. for 109 West 13th Street (35th Student House project) approved 6-0 (Shackelford/Brehm). A second consent item not shown on the vault's agenda note, alcohol consumption on the City Market Square Lot adjacent to the Church Street Parking Garage on October 10, 2026, approved 6-0 (Brehm/Hempel Irani).

**Purchases.** None.

**Agreements.** The Joyfield at Wormans Court PILOT agreement with Standard Wormans Venture LP and Frederick County was pulled from the agenda before the meeting.

**Resolution 26-15 concerning Housing Action.** Approved 6-0 (Brehm/Nash). No public comments.

**Annexation petitions announced.** About 156.446 acres on the west side of US Route 15, north of Sundays Lane, requested R8 Medium Density Residential; and about 200.011 acres north of Winpenny Drive and Yellow Springs Road, requested R4 Low Density Residential.

**Ordinances introduced.** G-26-12 (LMC § 829 Home Occupations and § 1002 Definitions), G-26-13 (LMC § 301, § 721 Forest Conservation, and § 1002), and G-26-14 (FY2027 Fee Schedule, fee in lieu of afforestation), all introduced by Council President Díaz.

**Council comments.** Hempel Irani on the opening of the new Yellow Springs Elementary School (the old building was at 140% capacity). Adkins on a North Market Street bike lane open house and on earlier comments about his relationship with certain Ethics Commission members. Brehm on the Weinberg Center's 100th anniversary. Nash expressed solidarity with a sexual assault victim at Cornell University and commented on a police report and her actions regarding signs on Route 40. Díaz discussed the 2026 United Way ALICE report, noting that the number of struggling households rose in the City while falling in the County, and said those families should be Council's focus.

**Public comment.** Comments were received; the minutes do not summarize them.
