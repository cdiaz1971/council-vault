# Hearing Minutes 2026-08-20

**Regular Public Meeting -- August 20, 2026, 6:00 PM, Annex** (City Hall board room was under work)

## Summary

Official minutes of the August 20, 2026 Regular Public Meeting, released by the City Clerk's office and included as backup material in the September 3, 2026 agenda packet. Council member Taylor was absent; all other members present. The meeting was low-conflict: four special ordinances and two purchase items passed 6-0, and the three second-read ordinances (Fee Schedule, Automated Enforcement, Mixed Use Vertical) all passed, with Vice President Nash abstaining on Automated Enforcement. Meeting adjourned at 6:25 PM.

## Outcomes

**Attendance.** Council President Díaz presided. Also present: Vice President Nash, Council members Shackelford, Brehm, Adkins, and Hempel Irani. Council member Taylor was absent. Also present: City Attorney Rachel Gray, City Clerk Phyllis Hane, Assistant City Clerk Caylee Winpigler.

**Approval of Minutes.** Minutes of the August 6, 2026 Regular Public Meeting approved 6-0, on a motion by Brehm, seconded by Adkins.

**Consent Agenda -- Special Ordinances (Items A.1-A.4), approved 6-0** on a motion by Brehm, seconded by Nash:
- S-26-15: FY27 budget amendment, $4,500 CLG grant (Maryland Historical Trust) for planning staff education and training.
- S-26-16: FY27 budget amendment, $27,613 DOJ Byrne JAG grant for FPD radar unit and Special Response Team shields.
- S-26-17: FY27 CIP 120014 budget amendment, $150,000 MD DHCD Community Legacy grant for National Museum of Civil War Medicine building improvements.
- S-26-18: FY27 CIP budget amendment, $500,000 MD DHCD Strategic Demolition Fund grant for predevelopment activities at the Downtown Frederick Hotel and Conference Center.

**Public Hearing -- Purchases, both approved 6-0, no public comment on either:**
- Summit Anchor Company purchase order, $1,448,601.57 (contract) / $1,545,571 (PO), for fall protection upgrades at 13 City water and sewer facilities.
- A. Morton Thomas change order, not-to-exceed $325,120.30, for design of the Worman's Mill Court Apartment pedestrian bridge crossing.

**Ordinance G-26-8, Fee Schedule.** Passed 6-0, no public comment. Confirms [[Hearing Brief 2026-08-20]] and matches Cesar's personal notes.

**Ordinance G-26-4, Automated Enforcement.** Passed 5-0-1, Nash abstaining, no public comment. Matches Cesar's personal notes on the vote count and the reason for Nash's abstention (her outside employer sells speed cameras, not the model the city uses). This was the one non-unanimous vote of the night, and was not anticipated in [[Hearing Brief 2026-08-20]].

**Ordinance G-26-10, Mixed Use Vertical LMC Amendment.** Passed 6-0, no public comment. Confirms [[Hearing Brief 2026-08-20]] and personal notes. Effective date August 30, 2026.

**Council Business/Comments.** Council member Hempel Irani noted the start of the school year and encouraged drivers to be mindful of students at bus stops. Not captured in personal notes.

**Public Comments.** Official minutes record only "Public comments were received," with no detail on speakers or topics. Cesar's personal notes recorded several speakers in support of ranked choice voting -- that substance does not appear anywhere in the official record.

## Action Items / Open Questions

The official minutes confirm every vote count from Cesar's personal notes exactly: G-26-8 and G-26-10 both 6-0, G-26-4 at 5-0-1 with Nash abstaining. Notion's Legislative Roadmap already reflects "Passed" with the correct vote counts for all three ordinances -- no status sync needed.

Two things the official record does not preserve, worth flagging: Council member Taylor's absence from the meeting (not noted in the personal notes), and the substance of the ranked-choice-voting public comment (the minutes only note that public comments were received, with no detail). If the RCV comment is meant to be cited later as a public-record data point for the Ranked Choice Voting / Charter Reform thread with Nash, Taylor, and Brehm, it will need to rest on Cesar's personal recollection or the meeting video/transcript, not the written minutes.
