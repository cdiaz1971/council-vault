# Council Meeting - Regular Public Meeting 2026-08-20

## Summary

Regular public meeting of the Frederick City Council, August 20, 2026, 6:00 PM, City Hall. Low-conflict agenda: four grant-acceptance special ordinances on consent, two procurement items, and three ordinances at second read. Mixed Use Vertical (G-26-10), sponsored by Council President Díaz, is the item requiring the most preparation. Full analysis, risk flags, and watch list in [[Hearing Brief 2026-08-20]].

## Consent Agenda -- Special Ordinances

1. S-26-15: FY27 budget amendment for a $4,500 CLG grant (Maryland Historical Trust) for planning staff education and training.
2. S-26-16: FY27 budget amendment for a $27,613 DOJ Byrne JAG grant for FPD radar unit and Special Response Team shields.
3. S-26-17: FY27 CIP 120014 budget amendment for a $150,000 MD DHCD Community Legacy grant for building improvements at the National Museum of Civil War Medicine, 48 East Patrick Street.
4. S-26-18: FY27 CIP budget amendment for a $500,000 MD DHCD Strategic Demolition Fund grant for predevelopment activities at the Downtown Frederick Hotel and Conference Center, 20 South Carroll Street.

## Public Hearing -- Purchases

1. Contract with Summit Anchor Company, $1,448,601.57, and purchase order of $1,545,571, for fall protection upgrades at 13 City water and sewer facilities (IFB 26-26, CIP 130003).
2. Contract change order with A. Morton Thomas, not-to-exceed $325,120.30, for design of a pedestrian bridge crossing at the Worman's Mill Court Apartment project (East Street Rails with Trails Phase 3, CIP 380004).

## Public Hearing -- Ordinances (Second Read)

1. G-26-8, Fee Schedule: amends the FY27 fee schedule to reflect taxicab fee changes from G-26-3 (Vehicles for Hire).
2. G-26-4, Automated Enforcement: amends City Code Sec. 13-5 to replace the flat $40 school-zone speed camera fine with the tiered penalty structure required by Maryland HB 182.
3. G-26-10, Mixed Use Vertical: amends LMC Sections 604, 838, and 1002 to allow residential use on the first floor of mixed-use buildings in MU-1 and MU-2 districts, subject to a 35,000 sq ft first-floor area threshold. Driven by the Brickworks project. Planning Commission recommended approval 4-0 on December 8, 2025. Effective date if passed: August 30, 2026.
