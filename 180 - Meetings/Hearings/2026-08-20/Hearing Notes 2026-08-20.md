# Council Meeting -- Notes 2026-08-20

## Summary

Personal notes from the August 20, 2026 Regular Public Meeting. The meeting was held at the annex because work was being done at the City Hall board room, and it was lightly attended. Most items on the agenda passed unanimously. The Automated Enforcement Ordinance was the exception, passing 5-0 with Nash abstaining over an outside employment conflict. Public comment included several speakers in support of ranked choice voting. These are personal notes, not official minutes.

## Outcomes

**Location.** Held at the annex, City Hall board room was under work.

**Attendance.** Lightly attended.

**Ordinance G-26-8, Fee Schedule.** Passed 6-0. Confirmed for second read per [[Hearing Brief 2026-08-20]].

**Ordinance G-26-10, Mixed Use Vertical LMC Amendment.** Passed 6-0. Confirmed for second read per [[Hearing Brief 2026-08-20]]. Effective date August 30, 2026.

**Ordinance G-26-4, Automated Enforcement.** Passed 5-0, Nash abstaining. Nash's non-council employer sells speed cameras, not the model the city uses, but she abstained to avoid any claim of bias. All other votes on the night passed 6-0, making this the one exception. Not anticipated in [[Hearing Brief 2026-08-20]], which expected a straightforward yes with low political risk.

**Public comment.** Several speakers spoke in support of ranked choice voting.

## Action Items / Open Questions

These are personal notes, not official minutes. Vote counts confirmed against the official city minutes, released in the September 3, 2026 agenda packet -- see [[Hearing Minutes 2026-08-20]]. One gap: the official record does not preserve the substance of the ranked-choice-voting public comment noted below, and it separately shows Council member Taylor as absent, which was not captured here.

The RCV public comment adds a data point of public pressure to the Ranked Choice Voting / Charter Reform thread already moving with Nash, Taylor, and Brehm.
