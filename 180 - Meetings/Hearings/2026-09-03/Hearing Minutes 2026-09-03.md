# Hearing Minutes 2026-09-03

**Regular Public Meeting -- September 3, 2026, 6:00 PM, Annex**

## Summary

Council President Díaz presided over a light-business Regular Public Meeting: five nonprofit grant agreements, a bond release, and a Weinberg Center special ordinance cleared on consent, along with two routine purchases and a Frederick Arts Council grant on the public hearing calendar, all 6-0. The one contested vote was a 5-1 approval (Nash opposed) of a Celebrate Frederick MOU. Council President Díaz introduced Ordinance G-26-11 implementing the Maryland Housing Certainty Act. Council member Taylor was absent for the full meeting.

## Outcomes

**Attendance.** Council President Díaz presided. Present: Vice President Katie Nash, and Council members Derek Shackelford, Peter Brehm, Joe Adkins, and Sarah Hempel Irani. Council member Libby Taylor absent. Also present: City Attorney Rachel Gray, City Clerk Phyllis Hane, Assistant City Clerk Caylee Winpigler.

**Approval of Minutes (Item IV).** Approved 6-0 on a motion by Brehm, seconded by Nash. The official minutes text distributed the August 20, 2026 Regular Public Meeting minutes for approval but the recorded motion approves "the minutes of the August 6, 2026 Regular Public Meeting" -- an internal inconsistency in the released document. See Action Items below; not resolved here.

**Appointments (Item V).** Reappointment of Lee Hand to Neighborhood Advisory Council 12 Coordinator, two-year term November 22, 2026 -- November 21, 2028. Approved 6-0, motion Brehm, second Nash.

**Council Member Comments (Item VI).** Nash noted former Council member Scott Lasher would have turned 80 that Sunday; recalled his Orange Theory studio and their time on the campaign trail. Brehm (with Shackelford, Hempel Irani, and Mayor O'Connor) attended Chamber of Commerce Director Rick Weldon's retirement celebration. Adkins noted the North End Civic Association's 80th anniversary celebration at Staley Park. Hempel Irani commented on the Mayor's State of the City address, thanking the Mountain City Elks Lodge #382, Daughters of Madame CJ Walker, and City staff. Council President Díaz spoke about an incident earlier that day in which federal agents intimidated citizens lawfully observing and recording law enforcement activity; the Frederick Police Department responded and deescalated after residents called.

**Consent Agenda (Item VII) -- all 6-0:**
- A.1: Released Bond, $241,815.16, posted by Renn Quarter TH LLC, guaranteeing Renn Quarter Phase 3 East Church and Highland Street signalization improvements (motion Adkins, second Nash).
- B.1-5 (motion Brehm, second Adkins): Grant agreements -- Asian American Center of Frederick, $100,000 (Community Health Worker Program) and $163,000 (Early Childhood Education Workforce Program); Frederick County Public Schools, $50,000 (Dolly Parton Imagination Library) and $75,000 (extracurricular/enrichment); Frederick Health Hospital, $150,000 (Family Connects program).
- C.1 (motion Hempel Irani, second Brehm): Special Ordinance S-26-19, grant funds from the Maryland State Arts Council for the Weinberg Center.

**Public Hearing (Item VIII):**
- A.1: Change Order #2 to the Conewago Enterprises construction contract, $184,083, for additional pavement. Approved 6-0 (motion Brehm, second Adkins). No public comment.
- A.2: Piggyback of Frederick County Agreement No. 22-17A with Mid-Atlantic Utilities Inc. for water valve bolt replacement in the North Crossing Community; purchase order $466,765. Approved 6-0 (motion Hempel Irani, second Brehm). No public comment.
- B.1: Grant agreement with the Frederick Arts Council. Approved 6-0 (motion Hempel Irani, second Brehm). No public comment.
- B.2: Memorandum of Understanding with Celebrate Frederick. Approved 5-1 (motion Brehm, second Hempel Irani) -- Díaz, Shackelford, Brehm, Adkins, and Hempel Irani in favor, **Nash opposed**. No stated reason for the dissent is recorded in the minutes. No public comment.
- C: No resolutions.
- D.1: Council President Díaz **introduced** Ordinance G-26-11 to implement the Maryland Housing Certainty Act (introduction only -- not a vote). See [[Maryland Housing Certainty Act -- Developer Vesting Rights 2026]] and [[Maryland Housing Certainty Act ↔ Frederick LMC Reform Synthesis]] for the underlying state law and its collision with the LMC review timeline. Second Read is scheduled for the September 17, 2026 meeting.

**Additional comments (Items IX-X).** None.

Meeting adjourned 6:21 PM.
