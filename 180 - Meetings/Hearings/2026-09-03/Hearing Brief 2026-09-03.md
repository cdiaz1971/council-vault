# Hearing Brief 2026-09-03

Under Public Hearing, an airport pavement change order and two agreements (Frederick Arts Council public art grant, Celebrate Frederick MOU) are clean. The item needing real attention: Introduction of Ordinance G-26-11. The Drive packet shipped with no backup for it, but César obtained the ordinance text directly (uploaded 2026-09-01). Despite the "Concerning Impact Fees" title, it is not a new fee or fee increase, it is an uncodified stopgap implementing the Maryland Housing Certainty Act (HB 548/SB 325), which the vault already flagged as a watch item back in May: [[Maryland Housing Certainty Act and Transit-Oriented Development Law Signed]]. ## Action Items

### VIII.D.1 -- Ordinance G-26-11 (draft), Maryland Housing Certainty Act Compliance (Introduction, First Read)

Text now on file (uploaded by César 2026-09-01; the Drive packet omitted it entirely). This is an uncodified bridge ordinance, not a new impact fee. It implements the Maryland Housing Certainty Act, effective statewide October 1, 2026, while the City drafts the full Code and LMC amendments the Act actually requires. Three operative pieces:

1. **Vesting.** A housing development application is governed only by the laws in effect when a *complete* application was submitted. Once a project has all required approvals, the developer holds a vested right to that use and development for five years. Each phase of a phased project counts as a discrete project for this purpose.
2. **Application clock.** Planning has 30 days to determine an application complete or incomplete, and must notify the applicant either way, with a deficiency list and cure window if incomplete.
3. **Impact fee timing.** The City may only collect development impact fees as a precondition to final inspection, and not more than 30 days before that inspection. The ordinance names all six fee programs this covers: park facilities (City Code § 11-2), water/sewer (Ch. 25, Art. IX), school mitigation (§ 4-22), Downtown Frederick mobility (§ 4-23), fees in lieu of parking (LMC § 607), and fees in lieu of parkland dedication (LMC § 608).

This is state-mandated, not discretionary. The ordinance's own conflict clause says state law controls, so Frederick's Code is out of sync with state law starting October 1 regardless of what happens tonight. What tonight's introduction buys is a stopgap so the Code isn't visibly contradicting state law while the full LMC codification catches up later.

For your housing agenda specifically, the vesting mechanism is double-edged, exactly as flagged in [[Maryland Housing Certainty Act and Transit-Oriented Development Law Signed]] in May. It removes a tool the City currently uses to negotiate development agreements or add conditions after approval, which cuts against layering affordability or tenant-protection conditions onto a project late. It also means any conditions you do want attached, on Real Rent, MPDU reform, or elsewhere, need to be locked in at approval rather than added afterward. Worth keeping in mind as those ordinances get structured.

Two gaps: no fiscal impact statement and no equity impact statement came with this ordinance, unlike every other substantive item on tonight's agenda. The delayed fee-collection timing has real cash-flow implications for the park, water/sewer, school mitigation, and mobility fee funds, and nobody has quantified that yet. Confirm with Rachel Gray whether this cleared legal review; the signature block is blank, which is normal for a First Read draft but doesn't confirm review actually happened, especially given the state-preemption interaction with six separate City fee programs.

Not yet on the Notion Legislative Roadmap. Worth adding once tonight's introduction is confirmed rather than guessing a status now.

### VIII.A.1 -- Change Order #2, Conewago Enterprises, Airport South Apron ($184,083)

Unit-price increase on pavement and erosion/sediment control materials for the south apron hangar development (CIP 380090, Ramp Rehabilitation), following the original 2025 contract and an April 2026 Change Order #1. Contract total after this change: $2,448,778, funded FAA ($1,472,225), MAA ($458,000), County ($200,000), City ($318,706). The project budget balance goes to $0.00 after this change order, there is no further contingency left in this CIP line if another cost increase surfaces before the November 15, 2026 completion date. NAC 12. Staff recommends approval; no political risk, just worth knowing the cushion is gone.

### VIII.B.1 -- Frederick Arts Council Grant Agreement ($250,000)

Reallocates a 2022 Sky Stage capital award to two new projects: a permanent public artwork at Westside Regional Park and Community Center ($154,000) and a Utility Box Wrap and Artist Professional Development pilot placed in public-art-underserved parts of the city ($58,500), with 15% capped for FAC overhead. Westside Regional Park sits in NAC 8, your own district. This is the item the Culture and Community Committee agreed to advance to full Council per the August 6 minutes being approved as Item II at this afternoon's 3:00 PM Committee workshop, so the sequencing today is: informational committee review at 3:00, vote at Council at 6:00. Staff and the Offices of Arts & Culture, the Mayor, and Opportunity and Innovation all recommend approval. Low risk. [[Culture and Community Committee Brief 2026-09-03]] has the fuller Committee-side context.

### VIII.B.2 -- Celebrate Frederick MOU

Formalizes the City's long-standing events partnership with Celebrate Frederick (Independence Day, In the Streets, the Summer Concert Series, the Holidays in Historic Frederick series, and others). No direct City funding, in-kind only (facilities, street closures, public safety, event coordination). This is also one of the six multi-year nonprofit MOUs getting an informational review at this afternoon's Culture and Community Committee workshop, same double-billing pattern as the FAC item above. No fiscal impact, no opposition on record.

## Consent Agenda

Ten items, all routine.

**Release of Surety (A.1):** $241,815.16 bond release for Renn Quarter TH, LLC, guaranteeing the East Church and Highland Street signalization project. As-builts already accepted (5/6/2026). Purely administrative closeout.

**Grant Agreements (B.1-5):** Five nonprofit grants totaling $538,000, all pre-approved in the FY2027 budget: Asian American Center of Frederick, $100,000 (Community Health Worker Program) and $163,000 (Early Childhood Education Workforce Program); Frederick County Public Schools, $50,000 (Dolly Parton Imagination Library) and $75,000 (extracurricular/enrichment); Frederick Health Hospital, $150,000 (Family Connects, free in-home nurse visits for newborn families through 12 weeks postpartum). Each executive summary states the grant does not negatively affect the City's equity goals. Nothing here warrants pulling from consent.

**Special Ordinance S-26-19 (C.1):** $17,423 FY2027 budget amendment for a Maryland State Arts Council operating grant to the Weinberg Center, exceeding the amount originally budgeted. You are the formal sponsor, on behalf of the Administration, which is the standard convention for grant-driven budget amendments routed to consent. Requires five votes under Local Government Article Section 5-205(b) since it spends money not appropriated at the original levy; nothing suggests that's in doubt.

**Appointments (V):** Reappointment of Lee Hand as an NAC 12 Coordinator, two-year term (Nov 22, 2026 - Nov 21, 2028). Mayor and the Board both support. Routine.
