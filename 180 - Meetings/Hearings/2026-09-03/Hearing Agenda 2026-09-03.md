**B. Agreements**
1. Grant agreement with Frederick Arts Council, $250,000, for public art at Westside Regional Park and a Utility Box Wrap/Artist Professional Development pilot program.
2. Memorandum of Understanding with Celebrate Frederick, formalizing the City's special-events partnership.

**C. Resolutions**
None.

**D. Ordinances**
1. Introduction of Ordinance G-26-11 (draft), an uncodified ordinance implementing the Maryland Housing Certainty Act (HB 548/SB 325) ahead of its October 1, 2026 effective date: establishes developer vesting rights (5 years, laws in effect at complete-application submission), a 30-day application-completeness clock, and restricts collection of the City's six development impact fee programs to within 30 days before final inspection. Text obtained directly from Council President Diaz; not included in the Drive packet.
