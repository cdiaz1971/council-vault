## VIII. Public Hearing

**A. Purchases**
1. Monocacy Blvd Improvements, Storage Way to South Street (CIP #320037) -- contract with Lingg Property Consulting, not-to-exceed $77,500, for design of road improvements, stormwater management, and a railway pedestrian gate crossing. Additional design scope beyond original contract, requested by MDSHA/MTA.
2. Airport Crack Seal Project (Runway 12/30): (a) Special Ordinance to amend the FY2027 budget for the project; (b) purchase order to Airfield & Pavement Maintenance, LLC, $94,555, for crack seal and paint markings. Total project $124,555 with a $30,000 contingency; MAA grant expected to cover 75%.
3. Contract with HMF Paving Contractors, Inc. (IFB 27-01) for indefinite-delivery, indefinite-quantity miscellaneous concrete work (sidewalk, curb, gutter, ramps).
4. Contract for Services with Epic Home Solutions, General Maintenance II, and Zerodraft Maryland to provide weatherization services to eligible households (grant-funded, FY2027).

**B. Agreements**
1. Agreement with Carroll Park Manor Community Association to reimburse pest control services, up to $65,280 (covers all 204 properties, $320/unit/year).
2. Review and opportunity for public comment on the PY2025 CDBG Consolidated Annual Performance and Evaluation Report (CAPER). No Council vote required; submission to HUD due September 30, 2026.
3. Payment in Lieu of Taxes (PILOT) Agreement with Frederick County and Standard Wormans Venture LP for the Worman's Mill Court Apartments (Joyfield at Wormans Mill) development.
4. Moderately Priced Dwelling Units (MPDU) Agreement with Standard Wormans Venture LP -- Worman's Mill Court Apartments.

**C. Resolutions**
1. Resolution 26-13 -- establishes the fee-in-lieu amount ($240,000, for 2.93 acres) owed under LMC Section 608 parkland dedication requirements for the Worman's Mill Court Apartments Mixed Use Development.

**D. Ordinances**
1. Second Read of Ordinance G-26-11, implementing the Maryland Housing Certainty Act (HB548/SB325). Introduced September 3, 2026.
