# Hearing Minutes 2026-09-17

**Regular Public Meeting -- September 17, 2026 at 6:00 PM, City Hall**

## Summary

Council President Díaz presided with VP Nash and Council members Shackelford, Brehm, and Hempel Irani present; Taylor and Adkins were absent. The dominant business was housing: Ordinance G-26-11 implementing the Maryland Housing Certainty Act passed 5-0, and two of the three Wormans Mill Court Apartments items passed 5-0 while the PILOT was pulled before the meeting. Council Business was dominated by criticism of Council leadership tied to the Nash ethics complaint. Adjourned 7:34 p.m.

## Outcomes

**Attendance.** Díaz, Nash, Shackelford, Brehm, Hempel Irani present. Taylor and Adkins absent. Also present: City Attorney Rachel Gray, City Clerk Phyllis Hane, Assistant City Clerk Caylee Winpigler.

**Proclamations.** Deaf Awareness Month (video introduced by Lakisha Wade, accepted by the Maryland Deaf Community Center). Maryland Economic Development Month (accepted by Mary Fore Naill).

**Minutes.** September 3 Regular Public Meeting approved 5-0 (Brehm/Shackelford). September 3 Closed Session approved 5-0 (Hempel Irani/Shackelford).

**Appointments and consent.** None.

**VIII.A.1, Monocacy Boulevard design contract, Lingg Property Consulting, not-to-exceed $77,500 (CIP 320037).** Approved 5-0 (Brehm/Hempel Irani). No public comment.

**VIII.A.2, Airport runway 12/30 crack seal.** Special Ordinance S-26-20 amending the FY2027 budget approved 5-0 (Brehm/Hempel Irani). Purchase order to Airfield & Pavement Maintenance LLC, $94,555, approved 5-0 (Brehm/Hempel Irani). No public comment.

**VIII.A.3, HMF Paving Contractors, IFB 27-01, miscellaneous concrete.** Approved 5-0 (Brehm/Shackelford). No public comment.

**VIII.A.4, Weatherization contracts.** Zerodraft Maryland 5-0 (Hempel Irani/Brehm), General Maintenance II 5-0 (Brehm/Hempel Irani), Epic Homes 5-0 (Hempel Irani/Shackelford). Public comments received.

**VIII.B.1, Carroll Park Manor pest control reimbursement.** Approved 5-0 (Nash/Brehm). Public comments received.

**VIII.B.2, CDBG CAPER.** Presented by Jodie Ostoich and Rebecca Gramling for review and public comment. No public comment, no action.

**VIII.B.3, Wormans Mill PILOT.** Pulled from the agenda before the meeting. Returns October 1.

**VIII.B.4, Wormans Mill Court Apartments MPDU Agreement, Standard Wormans Venture LP.** Approved 5-0 (Brehm/Hempel Irani). No public comment.

**VIII.C.1, Resolution 26-13, parkland fee-in-lieu for 2.93 acres (Wormans Mill Court Apartments).** Approved 5-0 (Hempel Irani/Brehm). Public comments received.

**VIII.D.1, Ordinance G-26-11, Maryland Housing Certainty Act implementation.** Approved 5-0 (Brehm/Shackelford). No public comment.

**Council Business.** Shackelford spoke about leadership and the ethics and behavior of certain members of Council leadership. Nash reported on In the Streets and raised concerns about Care Net Pregnancy Center. Brehm reported on a Frederick Industrial ribbon cutting and upcoming Economic Development videos. Hempel Irani responded to prior public comment by Tianna Masseqoi, summarized the Frederick News-Post article on the ethics complaint, and called for VP Nash to resign her leadership role and relinquish the Government Operations Committee chair.
