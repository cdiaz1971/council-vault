# Hearing Minutes 2026-08-06

**Regular Public Meeting -- August 6, 2026, 6:00 PM, City Hall**

## Summary

Official minutes of the August 6, 2026 Regular Public Meeting, retrieved from Google Drive. This was the decisive night for the Gateway Property rezoning, which passed 5-1 with Adkins opposed. The companion annexation amendment, the Rodent Control ordinance, and the Hood College athletic facility lease (with a naming-rights amendment) all passed 6-0. Three ordinances -- Fee Schedule (G-26-8), Special Events Commission (G-26-9), and Mixed Use Vertical (G-26-10) -- were introduced only, with votes to follow at later meetings. Council member Taylor was absent; Council President Díaz announced the birth of her son, Finley Jack Taylor, the day before, and that she would be on maternity leave. Meeting adjourned at 10:02 PM. This note replaces an earlier version built from personal notes, which had a numbering error (see Action Items).

## Outcomes

**Attendance.** Council President Díaz presided. Also present: Vice President Nash, Council members Shackelford, Brehm, Adkins, Hempel Irani. Council member Taylor absent (on maternity leave as of this meeting). Also present: City Attorney Rachel Gray, City Clerk Phyllis Hane.

**Approval of Minutes.** Minutes of the July 16, 2026 Regular Public Meeting and the July 16, 2026 Closed Session both approved 6-0, motion by Shackelford, seconded by Nash.

**Council Business and Comments.** Díaz announced Taylor's son Finley Jack Taylor was born the previous day and that she would be away from the dais on maternity leave. Brehm announced three planned text amendments: requiring all text amendments be filed by the Planning department or a Council member; allowing City Council to approve master site plans; and allowing affordable housing as a permitted use in the IST zone. Nash stated Council members had received a list of Charter changes under consideration and that it would be published for public review.

**Consent Agenda -- Special Ordinances (Items A.1-A.3), approved 6-0** on a motion by Nash, seconded by Brehm:
- S-26-12: FY27 budget amendment, Community Grant Project Fund (CGPF) grant from the Governor's Office of Crime Control Prevention and Policy.
- S-26-13: FY27 budget amendment, Internet Crimes Against Children (ICAC) grant, same state office.
- S-26-14: FY27 budget amendment, Police Retention and Recruitment (PRAR) grant, same state office. (The prior personal-notes version of this file listed this as S-26-15 -- corrected here against the official record; S-26-15 is a separate ordinance passed at the August 20, 2026 meeting, see [[Hearing Minutes 2026-08-20]].)

**Public Hearing -- Purchases:**
- OpenGov Budgeting and Procurement applications (SHI), $116,276 (Budgeting) and $66,358.63 (Procurement) in year 2, $130,907.53 (Budgeting) and $74,708.77 (Procurement) in year 3. Approved 6-0, no public comment.
- Northeast Technical Sales, $75,549, three nitrate instruments/controllers for the wastewater treatment plant. Approved 6-0, no public comment.
- Sydnor Hydro, $86,375, vertical turbine pump for the Linganore Water Treatment Plant. Approved 6-0, no public comment.
- McClure Company amendment, $74,535, rooftop and ductless units at Talley Recreation Center (RFP 25-C). Approved 5-0, Nash not present, no public comment.
- AXON Enterprises, master service agreement and contract for law enforcement technology. Approved 5-0, Nash recused, no public comment.

**Public Hearing -- Agreements:**
- Golden Mile Alliance grant agreement, $100,000. Approved 6-0, no public comment.
- Historical Society of Frederick County (Heritage Frederick) grant agreement, $100,000. Approved 6-0, no public comment.
- Hood College lease, Westside Regional Park. Approved 6-0 with an amendment to Article 7(c): Hood's choice of Naming Rights sponsor requires City Council approval before Hood may enter such an agreement. Additional information from Hood College President Debbie Ricker. No public comment.

**Ordinance G-26-6, Rodent Control.** Passed 6-0, with an amendment removing the word "registered" before "vacant property" in Section 12.5-92(e). Additional information from resident Marion Browne. Public comments were received.

**Ordinance G-26-7, Gateway Property Zoning Map Amendment.** Passed 5-1, Adkins opposed, rezoning 24.33 +/- acres from General Commercial to Institutional for the special events center, with five findings entered into the record (no additional population generated; adequacy of public facilities to be evaluated at final site plan/subdivision; transportation impacts to be managed through a facility-specific Events Management Plan; compatibility with surrounding uses; consistency with 2020 Comprehensive Plan policies). Additional information from Bruce Dean and Rohit Annan for the applicant. Public comments were received. See [[Gateway Property Zoning and Annexation]] for full project detail.

**Resolution 26-11 (amending Resolution 09-23, Gateway annexation terms).** Passed 6-0, addressing the required regional transportation contribution and provision of publicly accessible parkland. Public comments were received.

**Ordinance G-26-8, Fee Schedule.** Introduced only, no vote. (Passed 6-0 at the August 20, 2026 meeting -- see [[Hearing Minutes 2026-08-20]].)

**Ordinance G-26-9, Special Events Commission.** Introduced only, no vote.

**Ordinance G-26-10, Mixed Use Vertical LMC Amendment.** Introduced only, no vote. (Passed 6-0 at the August 20, 2026 meeting -- see [[Hearing Minutes 2026-08-20]].)

**Council Business and Comments.** Brehm thanked Adkins for flagging two issues in the G-26-7 zoning conditions, noting they could be addressed by the Special Events Commission.

**Public Comments.** Additional public comments were received at the end of the meeting (item X), beyond those received during the G-26-6 and G-26-7 items.
