# Hearing Minutes 2026-07-02

## Summary

Regular meeting at City Hall, 6:00-6:31 p.m., with Díaz presiding and all seven Council members present; Rachel Gray and Phyllis Hane also attended. G-26-3 passed unanimously. The Downtown Frederick Partnership MOU passed 5-1-1, and G-26-5 passed 6-0-1 with a deletion to its text.

## Outcomes

**Prior minutes.** June 18 regular and closed-session minutes were approved in separate 7-0 votes (Brehm/Nash on each). Approval of earlier closed-session minutes is not a July 2 closed-session record; no same-date closed summary is bundled.

**Election board.** Rupert Gardener was appointed to the Board of Supervisors of Elections for July 6, 2026-May 31, 2030; approved 7-0 (Brehm/Nash).

**Surety release.** The $10,000 NDR Realty cash surety for the Bowers Park water/sewer project at 6907 Baltimore National Pike was released 7-0 (Taylor/Brehm).

**Downtown Frederick Partnership MOU.** Approved 5-1-1 (Brehm/Hempel Irani). Shackelford, Taylor, Brehm, Adkins, and Hempel Irani favored it; Nash opposed; Díaz abstained. Nash later explained that she would oppose items from Economic Development until that department recognized the Route 40 corridor's economic significance. The minutes provide no reason for Díaz's abstention.

**G-26-3: vehicles for hire.** Approved 7-0. The official entry does not identify the mover or seconder. This follows its introduction recorded in [[Hearing Minutes 2026-06-04]].

**G-26-5: school zones.** Approved 6-0-1 (Hempel Irani/Shackelford): Díaz, Shackelford, Taylor, Brehm, Adkins, and Hempel Irani favored it; Nash abstained. Section 13-15(n) was deleted and subsequent sections renumbered. The minutes provide no reason for Nash's abstention.
