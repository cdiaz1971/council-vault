# Council Meeting Brief 2026-07-02

## Bottom Line

Light agenda. Two ordinances ready for final action, one housekeeping agreement, one clean appointment. No contested items. The Nash social media incident surfaces in the June 18 minutes for approval and is the only political watch item tonight.

---

## Action Items

### VIII.B.1 - Downtown Frederick Partnership MOU

This was pulled from the June 18 agenda and is back for action. Three-year agreement (FY27-FY29) with City contributing $117,280 rising to $129,301 in general operating support, plus $195,000-$205,000 for the Ambassador Program separately. Non-binding, so no legal exposure. The 1:1 match requirement is a reasonable accountability floor given the Partnership's FY25 match ratio was reportedly $15.90 per City dollar. No equity concerns flagged. The Partnership maintains a minority/woman/veteran business directory and board diversity commitments. Standard approval.

### VIII.D.1 - G-26-3 Vehicles for Hire (Third Read)

Administration ordinance, introduced by César on their behalf. Key changes: marijuana removed from the drug-testing definition (drivers still cannot operate while impaired); permit cap eliminated; permit fee cut from $2,500 to $500; permit term extended from one to two years; criminal lookback extended from 24 months to four years for serious offenses (rape, murder, kidnapping, first-degree assault, felony second-degree assault), while keeping 24 months for lesser offenses (misdemeanor assault, theft, DUI). Gov Ops Committee will now sit as the Taxicab Commission. The equity statement acknowledges the fee reduction helps drivers being squeezed by rideshare competition. Ready for final passage.

### VIII.D.2 - G-26-5 School Zones (Second Read)

Hempel Irani's ordinance. Administrative cleanup reflecting school relocations: Banner School moved to the old Monocacy Valley Montessori site; Monocacy Valley Montessori moved to Thomas Johnson Drive; Rock Creek moved out of the City; Yellow Springs Elementary is new and opening fall 2026; Frederick Classical Charter School added. The Yellow Springs zone has a delayed effective date of August 1, 2026, timed to the school opening. The Waverley zone gains an extension on Shookstown Road from Bowers Road to Gillespie Drive, which touches District 2. Ready for final passage on second read.

### V - Board of Supervisors of Elections: Rupert Gardner

BBC and NPR background with elections emphasis. One vacancy, zero other applicants interviewed. Board recommends approval. The Mayor's support section is blank on the backup sheet, a minor documentation gap but not a barrier to action.

### IV - Minutes Approval

The June 18 regular meeting minutes and closed session minutes are both on for approval. The regular minutes document Nash's social media apology and Shackelford's response on the record. The closed session minutes confirm the Bowman/Etzler/Roarke appointments and the Community Bridge walk-away authorization (5-1, Brehm opposed). Nothing in the minutes conflicts with the vault record. Routine approval.

### VII - Consent: NDR Realty Surety Release

$10,000 cash surety for a water and sewer project at 6907 Baltimore National Pike. Standard public works close-out. No anomalies.
