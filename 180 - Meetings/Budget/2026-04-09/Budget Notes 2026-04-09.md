# Budget FY2027 -- Work Session 2 April 9 Notes

## Summary

Second FY2027 budget work session, April 9, 2026. Single department presenting: Housing and Human Services. Director Stuart Campbell presented. Short session -- approximately 30 minutes. No new positions or major new initiatives. Three substantive moments: a delivery van capital request, a geographic equity challenge on west side health services, and a discovery of $100,000 in unallocated capital funding for a west side health project.

## Attendance

Absent:[[Joe Adkins]], [[Sarah Hempel Irani]], [[Katie Nash]]. Present included César,[[ Peter Brehm]], and [[Libby Taylor]] (confirmed by post-meeting hallway conversation).

## Key Points

**Housing and Human Services -- Stuart Campbell**

Presentation was routine. No new positions, no major programmatic changes. One capital request: a new delivery van to replace a current vehicle that is 24 years old and lacks a liftgate. César flagged this as an easy approval.

**West side health services -- geographic equity**

Both César and Peter Brehm raised the need for more health services on the west side of Frederick. Campbell gave a vague response indicating the department is "thinking about it." Budget manager Lacey Gordon then surfaced a significant detail: $100,000 is already budgeted for a health-related capital improvement project on the west side that has never been allocated. This is a live opportunity worth pursuing.

**Post-meeting conversation**

After the session, César, Peter Brehm, and Libby Taylor discussed a shared concern: the council should not just fund concepts but also fund policies, and should have policies to back up the things they want to get done. This is a useful framing for the broader FY2027 budget approach.

## Action Items

TickTick: Investigate the $100,000 unallocated west side health capital -- identify what project it was budgeted for, why it was never allocated, and whether it can be directed toward a specific west side health service expansion

## Relevance

The unallocated $100k is the most immediately actionable item from this session. Geographic equity on the west side -- Hillcrest, Shookstown, West Patrick corridor -- is a recurring theme and this funding represents a concrete lever. Connects to [[FY2027 Budget -- Cycle Index]] and the broader west side equity work. The post-meeting framing (fund policies, not just concepts) is a useful north star for remaining budget sessions.
