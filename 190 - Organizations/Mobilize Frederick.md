# Mobilize Frederick

Mobilize Frederick is a Frederick-based nonprofit civic organization focused on equitable and sustainable solutions to local policy challenges. Their Government Operations Committee has been active on data center zoning and water resource issues and submitted a research memo directly to the City Council in April 2026.

## Contact

**Kathy Kinsey**, Chair, Government Operations Committee
kmkinsey@comcast.net | 240-608-5954

## Role in Data Center Policy

Mobilize Frederick commissioned and submitted a detailed technical research memo to the full City Council on April 3, 2026, covering data center water consumption, advanced cooling technology alternatives, and state legislative models. The memo was submitted as the Council prepares to deliberate a proposed zoning text amendment that would allow data centers as a by-right use in M1 and M2 zones. Kinsey explicitly framed the submission as an input to that deliberation. See [[Data Centers -- Potomac River Basin Water Impact Projections]], [[Data Centers -- Advanced Cooling Technology Options]], and [[Data Centers -- State Legislative Models for Cooling Mandates]].
