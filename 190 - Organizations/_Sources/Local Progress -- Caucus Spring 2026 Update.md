# Local Progress Caucus Spring 2026 Update

## Summary

Spring 2026 update from Local Progress identity caucuses covering Black, Women's, Nuestro, and Pride caucuses, plus a policy corner on pre-eviction notice extensions and municipal data center opposition.

## Key Points

**On the Calendar**

- All Caucus Meeting: Thursday, April 30 at 6:30 PM ET via Zoom
- Nuestro Caucus General Meeting: Monday, May 18 at 5:00 PM ET via Zoom
- Pride Caucus General Meeting: Monday, June 1 at 2:00 PM ET via Zoom
- National Convening: July 9-11 in Baltimore, MD (Black and Women's caucus meetings held there)

**Black Caucus**

The Black Caucus convened early in 2026, welcoming seven new Leadership Committee members and aligning on 2026 priorities. Members engaged with the No Secret Police toolkit and held a candid conversation on how anti-Blackness shows up in governance. The caucus is mourning the loss of Coral Springs Vice Mayor Nancy Metayer, a founding LP Women's Caucus member and the first Black and Haitian American woman to serve on her governing body.

**Women's Caucus**

The Women's Caucus held its first general meeting in March, featuring guests from All Above All, an organization working with state and local leaders on proactive legislative strategies for abortion access and reproductive justice. They shared the Abortion Justice Playbook as a resource for LP members.

**Nuestro Caucus**

The Nuestro Caucus has been building momentum, holding space in general meetings to process the impact of federal immigration enforcement in communities while also lifting up local electeds who are standing up for their communities. Leadership Committee is deepening work in Puerto Rico and across the diaspora.

**Pride Caucus**

The Pride Caucus is resuming under new staff lead Michael Whitesides (LP Deputy Communications Director). First general meeting is June 1, kicking off Pride Month with community building and planning input.

**Policy Corner**

Two items flagged for LP members:

- **Pre-eviction notice period extension:** St. Paul recently extended its pre-eviction notice period, giving tenants more time before landlords can file proceedings. LP frames this as a racial and gender justice issue given disproportionate impact on Black women and mothers.

- **Data center opposition:** San Marcos stopped a proposed data center, citing utility cost increases, water resource strain, and public health risks. LP explicitly ties data center siting to environmental and racial justice, citing AI Now as a resource for local policy interventions. See also: [[Data Center Zoning Text Amendment -- Frederick]].

## Relevance

The National Convening is in Baltimore this July -- worth attending or sending a representative. The Nuestro Caucus meeting on May 18 is directly relevant to the City Values Act and immigrant community engagement work. The data center policy corner is directly useful for the ongoing data center zoning text amendment and community benefit agreement work.
