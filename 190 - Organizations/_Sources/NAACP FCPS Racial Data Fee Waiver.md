# No Price on Protecting Our Students from Racial Hatred

## Summary
Frederick NAACP flyer demanding FCPS waive a $2,022 fee and release public records on racial harassment and disciplinary actions in schools.

## Key Points
The NAACP filed a public records request with Frederick County Public Schools for data on racial harassment and disciplinary actions. FCPS is charging $2,022 to release the records and has declined to waive the fee, despite Maryland law allowing waivers when release serves the public interest. The NAACP argues that pricing out transparency prevents the community from understanding the scope of racial harassment in schools and organizing a response.

The flyer calls for two actions: emailing Superintendent Dr. Cheryl Dyson and the Board of Education at superintendent@fcps.org to demand the fee be waived and the data released, and contacting the Frederick NAACP to get involved in the broader push for the waiver and educational equity.

## Relevance
Touches Frederick public records law and school system transparency, adjacent to the council's GovTech and governance work on public access to government data. Also connects to the county's ongoing racial equity and civil rights advocacy landscape that intersects with immigrant community support work.
