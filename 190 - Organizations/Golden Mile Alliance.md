# Golden Mile Alliance

## Summary and Contact

The Golden Mile Alliance (GMA) is a Frederick-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit incorporated in February 2012 and dedicated to the revitalization of the Route 40 West corridor, historically known as the Golden Mile. Its mission is to create a welcoming, economically vibrant, and desirable commercial and residential community along the corridor. GMA is a grassroots effort bringing together businesses, residents, and property owners and is a key civic partner to the City of Frederick on corridor development.

## Programs

**Facade Improvement Grant** -- Matching funds for facade improvements to Golden Mile businesses, up to $10,000 per applicant.

**Golden Mile Commercial Property Tax Credit** -- City-administered program designed to incentivize rehabilitation of commercial buildings along the corridor; established as a result of the early task force work.

**Business Directory** -- Online directory allowing residents to find and support local Golden Mile businesses.

**Mixer on the Mile** -- Recurring networking event pairing food samples from local restaurants with business community engagement.

**Golden Mile International Festival** -- Annual celebration of the corridor's diversity; first edition held January 2025 at Warehouse Cinemas.

**Status of the Golden Mile Briefings** -- Board-hosted public briefings held at least three times per year to update businesses and residents on corridor activities.

**Start-Up and Growth Assistance** -- Resources and referrals for new and growing businesses along the corridor.

## Why This Matters for César

The Golden Mile is the most ethnically and socioeconomically diverse corridor in Frederick County, with over 50 languages spoken in the surrounding neighborhoods. As Council President, the corridor is directly relevant to several active priorities. The GMA is the primary civic partner for [[Golden Mile Small Area Plan]] implementation and the designated community liaison for Route 40 revitalization. The Facade Grant program and the Commercial Property Tax Credit are tools already authorized by the city and worth understanding in the context of any future LMC or corridor policy conversations. Derek Shackelford's seat on the GMA board makes this a relevant relationship for council alignment. The International Festival and the corridor's demographic composition also connect to the immigrant community engagement work embedded in the [[Municipal ID]] effort and potential [[City Values Act]] outreach.
