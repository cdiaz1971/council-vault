# Asian American Center of Frederick

## Summary

The Asian American Center of Frederick (AACF) is a minority-led 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded in 2005 as the L.I.F.E. (Learning Institute of Enrichment) and Discovery, Inc. It was later renamed to reflect its expanded scope serving the Asian American community before broadening further to serve all immigrants and minorities in Frederick County. Its mission is to enrich the future of Maryland by equipping and empowering all immigrants to become independent and self-sufficient contributors to their communities. AACF operates three locations across Frederick County and employs a staff of 30+ in a multilingual, multicultural organization.

## Programs

AACF operates six main program areas:

**Family Support Services** -- Early childhood education for children up to age 4, parenting classes, nutrition, Adult Basic Education, Financial Literacy, ESL, and mental health services. Funded by Maryland Family Network, Maryland State Department of Education, and the U.S. Department of Education.

**Community Health Services** -- Health literacy, Community Health Worker training and state-accredited certification, Frederick Community Health Fair (annual, in partnership with Frederick Health), Kaiser Permanente Community Health Access Program (CHAP) enrollment (enrolled 800+ undocumented/underdocumented individuals in 2023), and language resource services.

**Citizenship and Integration Services** -- Citizenship and naturalization support, ESL, civics instruction, refugee resettlement assistance, and the [[Refugee Welcoming Coalition of Frederick]].

**Senior Services (55+)** -- AmeriCorps RSVP and SCP programs, service navigation, and Senior Companions Program.

**Workforce Development (WISE)** -- Workforce Integration Services for Employment; CNA/GNA training; Community Health Worker Training Program (CHWTP).

**Emergency Assistance** -- Financial assistance for medical, food, and shelter emergencies, supported by Frederick County Community Foundation.

## Why This Matters for César

AACF is one of Frederick's two primary immigrant-serving nonprofits alongside [[Spanish Speaking Community of Maryland]], and the only one with such depth in health services, workforce development, and refugee resettlement. Several policy areas César is actively working on intersect directly with AACF's work:

- [[Municipal ID]] legislation would directly benefit the undocumented and underdocumented immigrants AACF serves, including the 800+ individuals enrolled in Kaiser CHAP who lack documentation.
- [[City Values Act]] positions the City as a welcoming community -- AACF is a key partner in operationalizing that commitment.
- AACF's west-side concentration along the Golden Mile aligns with housing and land use concerns in the [[LMC Review]] and any equity-focused zoning work.
- The Refugee Welcoming Coalition of Frederick is relevant to any council engagement with refugee resettlement policy at the local level.
- Dr. William Reid (board member) is Chair of Frederick County Progressives and VP of the Frederick County NAACP -- a politically connected figure worth knowing.
- Charlotte Davis (board secretary) is Executive Director of the Rural Maryland Council -- a state-level connection useful for regional policy conversations.

Primary contact for council engagement: **Elizabeth Chung**, Executive Director -- echung@aacfmd.org / (301) 694-3355 Ext. 1002.
