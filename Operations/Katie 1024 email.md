Administrative and Legislative Support

  

- Add staff capacity; add part time positions for each member (7) for January 2026 through budget amendment in January (for new Council); each Councilmember will outline what the job description will be for each part time employee

    
- Add intern stipends for every Councilmember for the FY27 budget ($14,000 per year)

    

- Reformat the Legislative Committee Manager position to improve workflow and coordination; dedicated policy analyst or researcher to assist members with background, comparative studies, and data driven decision making


    
- Reshape the Legislative Assistant position to serve as the council's main constituent services manager (with a plan to promote this position to manager in two years); this position will support Commissions and intergovernmental work such as MML and County initiatives.

    
- Consideration of improved legislative tracking software to manage bills, amendments, and votes efficiently (for new members)
    - OpenGov?


    
- Structured training sessions from Departments in advance of budget meetings in Spring 2026 (what functions they serve, what they would like the Councilmembers to know).
    

  
  

2. Technology and Accessibility

- Upgrade hearing room technology (microphones; this is planned).


    
- Assigned Communications/Public Affairs employee to support Council work.
    - Dedicated page on city website
    
- Consideration of improved online transparency tools to be determined by the new council.
    

- Consideration of digital workflow for constituent correspondence to be determined by the new council (centralized tracking and response platform).
    

  

3. Facilities and Member Experience

- Dedicated Council offices (7), workspace with modern desks and fixtures (purchased after 2020), storage, and dedicated meeting conference room for members and staff.


    
- Shared administrative assistant or scheduler to help coordinate calendars, travel, and meeting logistics (Office Manager).
    
- Parking arrangements for Councilmembers at City Hall.
    
- Access to a members’ lounge behind the hearing room or collaborative workspace for informal meetings and constituent calls.
    
- Security enhancements for members and staff in City Hall and at public meetings; communication of threats to each member.
    - Already failing
    - Officer in room is a problem


    
- Budget for supplies, City of Frederick-branded clothing, and other related items currently provided to the Mayor’s Office.
    

4. Communication and Coordination
  

- Monthly coordination meetings between the Mayor’s Office and the Council President and Vice President to align legislative priorities.
    
- Quarterly retreats or workshops to discuss shared goals, upcoming projects, and interdepartmental coordination (budgeted within Council funds)
    
- Quarterly regular department briefings for Councilmembers to receive updates on key city initiatives before they reach legislative stages.
    
- Dedicated point of contact in each department for Council inquiries to ensure timely and accurate responses.
    

  

5. Training and Professional Development

- Budget for professional development; attendance at MML, NLC, or ICMA conferences and leadership programs.
    
- Annual training tailored for Frederick’s City Council to include trainings re: land use/planning and performance budgeting.
    
- Workshops on community engagement, media relations, and legislative best practices.
    
- Support for an education benefits stipend that matches City employee provisions.
    

  

6. Policy and Governance Improvements

- Adopt a clear legislative calendar to provide predictability for members and the public.
    
- Develop an annual Council legislative agenda in collaboration with the Mayor’s policy team.
    

  

7. Recognition and Wellness

- Formal recognition program for Councilmembers’ service milestones and community engagement.
    

- Council community engagement events to build relationships outside formal meetings.
