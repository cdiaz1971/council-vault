Good Evening,

  

I write this while on the couch with my Daughter and we are watching the Secret Lives of Mormon Wives. This show has a lot of drama. Hulu is an appropriate place for drama: not the City Council.

  

Tonight I'd like to volunteer some ideas regarding ways your Council Leadership can design and build better systems for your success. Acts of service will strengthen our relationships, heal our Council, and our collective output from our actions will speak louder than words.

  

1. Personalized Legislative Support Plans for Each Councilmember

  

I would like to work to create a brief, individualized plan with each member outlining:

- policy priorities;

- preferred communication style;

- support needed from staff; and,

- how to best highlight this work.

  

2. Monthly “Councilmember Spotlight Briefs” and District Communication

  

As your Council President I would like to work with staff to prepare short summaries of your policy and constituent work to share publicly on social media and communication channels. We will also work together to improve District-level communications and take City Hall to our districts. I would love to see us implement a rotating schedule that ensures events are covered and we work collaboratively to maximize our representation.

  

Additionally, I have ideas to liven up our hearings and Board Room:

- let's showcase each member by rotating an "inspirational moment" to start our work each hearing that reminds us why we do this work;

- we can broaden our use of proclamations to involve more groups and invite more voices to City Hall; and,

- can we create a warmer and welcoming space with music and art?

  

3. A Standing “Legislative Check-In”

  

I propose we hold quarterly internal discussions, both public and in closed session, on:

- how independently the Council is functioning and where administrative overreach may need to be checked;

- what obstacles are slowing our work and what do we need that you’re not currently getting (what resources, coordination, or staff support would help?); and,

- SWOT analysis for measurement.

  

We will seek opportunities to create an honest dialogues with each other to measure our collective successes and opportunities for improvement.

  

4. Council Learning & Development Series

  

We have already identified some sessions that will be tailored to policy development needs.

  

I propose legislative lunch and learn sessions that include instructions for how to craft strong amendments and legislation, ins and outs of the 90 Day Legislative Session including a shadow day in Annapolis, and the various ways our County and State agencies intersect with our City residents.

  

Together we have brainstormed deep learning opportunities for budget analysis, land-use fundamentals and our neighborhood advisory councils. I look forward to building a learning program with staff and external experts.

  

5. Strengthened Committee Structure

  

I have learned a lot in our first attempt to create legislative committees this year, the work plans I drafted that outlined our policy work, and the flow of work.

  

My initial thought is to streamline the Committees to two and build out a policy workgroup structure to enable simultaneous work on different critical topics. We can invite experts from the community to provide legislative recommendations that equal draft ordinances for Committee work and ultimately, passage and enactment.

  

Going forward with our collective input, I would like to implement clear committee goals and timelines. Enhancing committee output is one of the best ways to increase councilmember impact - this is something I would like us to build together and my role as your President is to then implement for our success. The work plan concept stems from this desire to communicate the path forward and establish accountability: our brains and efforts together will do so much!

  

Thank you for this additional opportunity to share some thoughts about how the Council President can design and build better systems. As your Council President, I will be a facilitator and someone who sees leadership as service, not spotlight. My next emails will be my final list of thoughts regarding the Council President’s focus on our individual and collective success, establishing legislative autonomy, and a final wrap up.
