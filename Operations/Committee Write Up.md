### Full Council

  

Topics to be considered before full Council in a work session: 

- Land Use Cases/Text Amendment/Zoning

- Education (0-100)
    
- Health
    

- Council Rules and Procedure/Organization
    

  

### Committees

  

There are three committees proposed: Government Operations, Public Works, and Communities and Culture. 

  

Government Operations addresses governance and internal systems. 

  

Public Works addresses the built environment. 

  

Community & Culture addresses quality of life issues that impact residents. 

  
  

#### Government Operations Committee
    

How the City governs, manages resources, and ensures accountability.

  

Committee Topics

- Budget Monitoring, Purchasing Guidelines, and Contracts that Require Committee Discussion
    
- Elections
    
- Technology
    
- Justice and Police Reform
    
- Land Management Code/Charter Rewrite
    
- Employees (including Police)
    
- Taxes
    

  

Why: These areas relate to governance, internal controls, democratic processes, public safety systems, and operational integrity of city government.

  
  

#### Public Works Committee
    

The physical city: infrastructure, systems, and environmental stewardship

  

Committee Topics

- Infrastructure
    

- Land Use–related infrastructure concerns
    
- Transportation
    
- Environment
    
- Parks and Recreation
    
- Disability access & inclusion
    
- Education - Built Environment
    

  

Why: This committee focuses on the built and natural environment, mobility and safety issues, land use impacts, and public spaces.

  

#### Community and Culture Committee
    

People, opportunity, culture, and quality of life

  

Committee Topics:

- Family Support Services, Youth Programing, Senior Services
    

- Culture (arts, historical preservation, equity)
    
- Economic Development and Opportunity
    
- Education - Childcare as Infrastructure
    
- Neighborhood Placemaking
    
- Festivals and events
    

  

Why: This committee centers residents’ lived experience — learning, neighborhood improvements, culture, and economic mobility.
