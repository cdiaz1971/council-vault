# Forest Conservation Ordinance -- Section 721 Update (PC24-224ZTA)


## Tracker context

Discussed at the August 13, 2026 work session (workshop item, no vote). Updates Section 721 to conform to state SB234/SB526, extends short-term maintenance bonding from two years to five, and revises specimen tree mitigation and street tree bonding rules. Planning Commission voted 4-0 in favor on July 13, 2026, with two items sent back to staff for further review. Frederick Builders Association letter raises housing-cost concerns (extended bonding period, larger required tree stock, no grandfathering) that sit in tension with the affordability message of the same night's MAHTF presentation.

### Items to reconcile
- No queue was recorded; assigned Unassigned for review, without inferring active ownership.

### Evidence log
- 2026-10-14: Official minutes confirm G-26-13 was introduced October 1, 2026; the October 15 packet schedules second read and approval, with companion fee ordinance G-26-14. Revised version mitigates specimen trees at twice mature canopy area, drops street trees from bonding, and sets penalty fees at $3.64 per square foot. The revised executive summary does not address the topsoil standard or grandfathering. Stage unchanged pending the October 15 vote. See [[Hearing Brief 2026-10-15]].
