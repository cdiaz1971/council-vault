# Automated Enforcement Ordinance (G-26-4)


## Tracker context

Introduced by Council Member Hempel Irani June 18, 2026. Amends City Code Sec. 13-5 to replace the flat $40 school-zone speed camera civil penalty with the tiered structure Maryland HB 182 requires statewide ($40 to $425 by speed over the limit, effective October 1, 2025). Passed 5-0 August 20, 2026, Nash abstaining. Nash's non-council employer sells speed cameras, not the model the city uses, but she abstained to avoid any claim of bias.
