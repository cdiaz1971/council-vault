**Do not surface until November (moved from October, 2026-09-27).** ZTA PC25-626ZTA proposes adding Critical Digital Infrastructure (data centers) as permitted use in M1 and M2 zones. Still at Planning Commission; October hearing at earliest.

César opposes this ZTA. Vote math with Nash recused: six voting members, 3-3 tie defeats the ZTA. Current alignment: César (No), Brehm (No), Taylor (No).

Opposition record strong and strengthening: Amazon withdrew from Calvert Cliffs (August 4) after voter backlash ousted three pro-data-center commissioners in the June 23 primary; Calvert County moratorium vote scheduled August 18; CCAN reports 77% of Marylanders now live under some form of data center moratorium and predicts statewide moratorium legislation in the 2027 General Assembly session; PG County extended its hyperscale moratorium two years (July 8); three Maryland agencies filed an FERC complaint on ratepayer harm (July 2); BGE rate increase proposal ($8/month residential) with a January 2027 PSC timeline; health impacts research gap documented (July 18); NY community toolkit filed (July 18); Maryland SB 116 state study was due September 1, 2026, status not yet confirmed. A concrete negotiating fallback also surfaced this week: four cities (Festus MO, Joliet IL, Memphis TN, St. Louis MO) have used negotiated comprehensive development agreements rather than moratoriums to extract enforceable community benefits, St. Louis's roughly $15M deal covering digital inclusion, environmental justice, transportation, and hiring/wage commitments, see [[Comprehensive Development Agreements as an Alternative to Data Center Moratoriums]] and [[National League of Cities Data Center Moratorium Tracker Finds 76 Localities]].
