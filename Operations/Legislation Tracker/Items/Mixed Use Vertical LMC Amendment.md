# Mixed Use Vertical LMC Amendment


## Tracker context

Submitted by Planning Director Gabrielle Collard. Amends LMC Section 604 (Building and Urban Design Standards), Section 838 (Mixed Use Development, Vertical), and Section 1002 (Definitions) to allow residential uses on the first floor of mixed-use buildings in MU-1 and MU-2 zones. Immediate driver is the Brickworks project (NAC 11). Planning Commission voted 4-0 for approval December 8, 2025, citing consistency with the 2020 Comprehensive Plan. Uses a bright-line 35,000 sq ft first-floor area threshold rather than a discretionary standard. Passed 6-0 at the August 20, 2026 Regular Public Meeting. Effective date August 30, 2026.
