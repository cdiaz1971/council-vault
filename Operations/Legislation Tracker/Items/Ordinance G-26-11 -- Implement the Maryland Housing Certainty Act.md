# Ordinance G-26-11 -- Implement the Maryland Housing Certainty Act


## Tracker context

Interim ordinance implementing HB548/SB325 (effective Oct 1, 2026) ahead of full LMC conflict cleanup: complete-application definitions, 30-day completeness determination, 5-year vesting on approval, phased projects treated as separate projects, impact-fee collection timing restricted to within 30 days of final inspection. Introduced by Council President Diaz at the Sept 3, 2026 meeting via City Attorney Rachel Gray. Passed 5-0 at the September 17, 2026 Regular Public Meeting (second reading). Directly intersects the LMC review timeline and Libby Taylor's proposed building moratorium -- see vault note 'Maryland Housing Certainty Act ↔ Frederick LMC Reform Synthesis'.
