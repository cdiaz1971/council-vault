# Maryland Statewide Housing Revolving Fund

## Working summary

Watch for a formal proposal in the 2027 state session and potential connections to Frederick housing funds.

## Coverage source

Added from [[Council Watch List#Maryland Statewide Housing Revolving Fund]] on October 13, 2026. The recorded stage is based on that saved workstream description, not a new check of current agendas or outcomes. A blank lead means the source does not identify one.

**Watching since:** 2026-08-04
Marks and Coale's Maryland Matters op-ed proposes a $250M statewide housing revolving fund administered by DHCD, modeled on Montgomery County HOC. Watch for 2027 session legislation. If it advances, a Frederick fund (Trout's Housing Accelerator Fund or Nash's HACF production fund) would be positioned to draw state capital.
