# LMC Amendment -- Convenience Stores with Gas Sales (PC25-248ZTA, Wormald)


## Tracker context

Imported from the [Notion record](https://app.notion.com/3c6109f3c155817996d8cc25c5da8840) on October 13, 2026. The imported stage is a recorded status, not a fresh verification of the current agenda. Original properties are retained in [[Council Tracker - Notion Capture 2026-10-13]].

Filed by The Wormald Companies, LLC after their Monocacy Center site plan was constrained by pump-count rules. Planning Commission voted 5-0 (4/13/26) to recommend approval; staff supports. Reclassifies Convenience Stores with Gas Sales in MU districts to a Mixed-Use Conditional Use; replaces the 200-ft proximity-based pump restriction with a flat 100-ft setback plus a new per-district pump table. Net effect loosens siting near residential zoning in MU/PND specifically even as it tightens elsewhere -- worth confirming no residential-impact analysis was skipped. Scope grew from a narrow Sec. 816 pump-count clarification (as first tracked in the vault in March 2026) to a full Use Matrix (Sec. 404) + Sec. 824 rewrite.
