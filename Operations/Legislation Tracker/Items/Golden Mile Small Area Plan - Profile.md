**Status:** Active -- engagement phase launched August 5
The Golden Mile Small Area Profile is the first release under FOCUS Frederick, a new two part small area planning process that separates existing conditions research (the Profile) from the policy recommendations that follow (the Plan). The Profile itself requires no council action. Plan Area spans District 1 and District 2, confirmed against the City's live GIS district data: Route 40 itself runs entirely within District 2 along this stretch, but the wider ~2,100-acre Plan Area reaches north into District 1 neighborhoods (Stonegate, Lake Coventry, Willowdale Park, Taskers Chance Park) and south into District 2 neighborhoods (Golf View, Monarch Ridge, Emerald Farm, where César lives). The Profile's NAC assignment is wrong, however: it describes "NAC 8 to the north and NAC 5 to the south," but the City's own GIS data confirms NAC 5 sits north of Route 40 and NAC 8 sits south, matching César's own NAC 8 membership. Worth flagging to Planning Director Gabrielle Collard's office as an error in a published city document. Full data points and cross thread connections captured in [[Golden Mile Small Area Profile]]. Strongest connections: renter cost burden (63 percent, versus 52.5 percent citywide) and ALICE data strengthen the Real Rent Ordinance and Affordability Beyond Housing; the 694-unit residential pipeline and the Prospect Center and Westridge unit counts should feed the annual Housing Production Report that Resolution 26-15 requires; corridor crash data and the in-design Multimodal Access Plan support the E-Bike Ordinance; underutilized commercial parcels are relevant to the Inclusionary Zoning thread.

**Community engagement phase launched August 5:** City released the Small Area Plan engagement schedule -- resident survey open, steering committee forming. César wants himself and VP Nash on the steering committee. Next action: confirm steering committee application process with Planning staff; flag reversed NAC labels to Gabrielle Collard's office.
