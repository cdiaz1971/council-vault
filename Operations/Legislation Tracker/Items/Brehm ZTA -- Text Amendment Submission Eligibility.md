# Brehm ZTA -- Text Amendment Submission Eligibility

## Working summary

Confirm whether the separate proposal limiting who may submit text amendments has a draft or Planning Commission submission.

## Coverage source

**Status:** Active -- initial feedback taken at the September 24 work session
Brehm announced at the August 6 hearing that he will work on two zoning text amendments: one to limit who can submit text amendments, and one to give Council the ability to review site plans. Peter confirmed drafts will be ready by end of week. **Update 2026-09-27:** The council site plan review amendment (LMC Section 309, letting Council assume final review authority over major site plans within 60 days of submission) was Item C at the September 24 work session, for initial feedback only. Peter takes the comments into the draft before submitting it to the Planning Commission. César reports the session went fine. Open tension carried forward: it moves the same approval-process question the LMC Review Commission would own, and adds up to three months of discretionary review to 5+ unit projects. Next action: watch for Peter's revised draft and the Planning Commission submission.
