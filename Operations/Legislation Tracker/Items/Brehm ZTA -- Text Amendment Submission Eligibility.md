# Brehm ZTA -- Text Amendment Submission Eligibility

## Working summary

Amendment to LMC Sec. 306 co-sponsored by Council President Díaz and Council Member Brehm (executive summary D.1). It removes City residents and property owners from the list of who may file a text amendment, leaving an elected City official or the Planning Division. Two versions exist. The October 22 packet version (D.2, dated 10/9) sends every application to the Council at a public hearing, where the Council approves or denies it by majority vote before the Planning Commission. The later Drive draft (header 10/19) replaces that with a non-voting review at a public meeting the Council President schedules, with Division filings requiring notice only. The draft also replaces "Board of Aldermen" with "City Council." Zoning map amendments are not affected.

## Analysis and open questions

Full analysis, opposition map, and options in [[Text Amendment Filing Authority Policy Brief 2026-10-19]]. Drafting issues to resolve before introduction:

- The packet version colleagues received has the binding vote; the revision being presented does not.
- The justification promises Council approval even in the no-vote draft.
- Executive summary leaves fiscal, NAC, and equity impact blank.
- No deadline for the Council President to schedule the review meeting.
- "Elected City official" includes the Mayor; the justification refers only to Council members.
- Division filings get notice only, while official filings get a review meeting.
- No transition clause for pending private applications, most importantly [[Data Center ZTA PC25-626ZTA -- Trammell Crow]].
- No public process or disclosure for requests that a Council member sponsor an amendment, and no fee recovery for sponsored amendments.
- Cleanup of a garbled sentence in the Commission hearing subsection and list lettering.
