# E-Bike (EPMD) Ordinance


## Tracker context

Imported from the [Notion record](https://app.notion.com/3e4109f3c1558180b7ecc71b3b9a0fdc) on October 13, 2026. The imported stage is a recorded status, not a fresh verification of the current agenda. Original properties are retained in [[Council Tracker - Notion Capture 2026-10-13]].

Regulates Electrically Powered Micromobility Devices (e-bikes, e-scooters, e-cargo bikes, related devices). Repeals Chapter 22, Art. VI; adds Chapter 33, Arts. II-III. Council Member Brehm named sponsor on the Sept 14, 2026 draft (earlier May/July briefs described it as a Díaz introduction). Public Works Committee rounds May 26 and Sept 8, 2026; workshop July 9. Sept 14 draft allows EPMDs by default on City shared-use paths unless signs restrict, with two exceptions: Baker Park Shared Use Path (Baughman's Lane to Bentz St) barred outright; Carroll Creek Linear Park barred unless signs permit. E-motos and commercial dockless rental banned; downtown sidewalk ban. Not yet formally introduced.
