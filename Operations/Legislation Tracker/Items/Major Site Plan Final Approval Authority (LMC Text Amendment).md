# Major Site Plan Final Approval Authority (LMC Text Amendment)


## Tracker context

Imported from the [Notion record](https://app.notion.com/3e2109f3c15581079af5d2bd74ecd221) on October 13, 2026. The imported stage is a recorded status, not a fresh verification of the current agenda. Original properties are retained in [[Council Tracker - Notion Capture 2026-10-13]].

Gives Council the option (not obligation) to assume final reviewing authority over major site plans -- currently a Planning Commission function -- within 60 days of application submission, with a 30-day Council decision clock once it does. Applies to 5+ unit residential, all new non-residential construction, and major expansions/redevelopments. Rationale: annexation/zoning votes happen before traffic, parking, and design details are known. Watch: adds a discretionary Council layer and up to ~90 days on qualifying projects (5+ unit townhouse/multifamily is exactly missing-middle territory); overlaps with the pending LMC Review Commission's mandate to review zoning approval processes for predictability and equity (see LMC Review Commission -- Draft Resolution Analysis) -- worth flagging that overlap rather than letting two vehicles move on the same question in parallel.
