# Resolution on Affordable Housing Action in the City of Frederick


## Tracker context

PASSED 6-0 at Oct 1, 2026 Regular Public Meeting as Resolution 26-15. Fully revised from Housing Target Resolution. Renamed 2026-07-20. Numbered findings structure replaces whereas clauses (per legal dept guidance). Grounded in TPMA City of Frederick Housing Data Report (Feb 2026) and Mayor's Affordable Housing Task Force Final Report (July 2026). Workshopped Sept 10, 2026. Packet version (Oct 1) titled 'Declaring the City of Frederick's Commitment to Advancing Affordable Housing Production': 4,000 units over 10 years adopted as target production goal (no longer 'minimum floor'); 13,774 total need by 2035; nine priority recommendations in Section 5; FCAMI in Section 6; anti-displacement Section 7; HHS annual report from FY2028 in Section 8; County call-out in Section 9. Non-binding (Section 10). See vault Hearing Brief 2026-10-01.
