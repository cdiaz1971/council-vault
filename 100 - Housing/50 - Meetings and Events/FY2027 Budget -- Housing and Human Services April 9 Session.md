# FY2027 Budget -- Housing and Human Services April 9 Session

## Summary

The April 9 departmental review session covers Housing and Human Services, the highest-priority session for housing policy leverage in the FY2027 budget cycle. H&HS is budgeted at $12.6M. The session is the primary opportunity to press on what that funding actually delivers against Frederick's 8,133-unit documented shortage.

## Questions to Bring

- What does the City Housing Fund contain in FY2027 -- how much is allocated, for what programs, and how is it structured?
- What does $12.6M in H&HS spending produce in terms of direct affordability outcomes vs. administrative overhead?
- How does Nash's $250k affordable housing RFP line relate to this departmental budget -- is it an add-on or embedded?
- Is the RFP oriented toward new production only, or does it have a preservation component?
- If the housing study is being used to defer the production target resolution, what is the expected release date and who controls it?
- What tenant protection programs, if any, are funded in H&HS -- and is there a budget hook for a future rental junk fees ordinance?

## Key Context

- May 14 is the hard deadline for council leverage on the budget
- The $250k legal analysis consultant line is unspecified -- press whether it can cover preemption review for tenant protection work
- Housing preservation framing: durability is affordability policy; the $12.6M question is not just production
