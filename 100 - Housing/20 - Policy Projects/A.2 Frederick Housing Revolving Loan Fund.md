HOW IT WORKS
What Is a Revolving Loan Fund?
A pool of public capital that lends — not gives — money to
developers.
Raised through a one-time bond issuance, it closes the financing gap on mixed-income rental
housing, then gets repaid so it can be lent again.
FREDERICK HOUSING ACCELERATOR FUND

HOW IT'S DIFFERENT
A Capital Tool, Not a Subsidy
Grant or Fee Waiver Revolving Loan Fund
→
Spent once. Gone. Repaid. Re-lent. Forever.
Every dollar that leaves as a construction loan returns as repayment — and is re-lent.

HOW IT WORKS
Why Projects Need “Patient” Capital
Lenders wait for stabilization
Permanent lenders won't fully finance a project until it is built and leased.
Affordable units earn less rent
Less revenue is available to service debt, even though the unit costs the same to build.
An RLF fills the gap, cheaply
At a lower cost than private mezzanine debt (12–20% annually), in exchange for affordability.

Public Capital Does Three Jobs at Once
