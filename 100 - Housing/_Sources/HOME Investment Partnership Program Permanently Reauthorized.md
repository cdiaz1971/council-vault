# HOME Investment Partnership Program Permanently Reauthorized

## Summary
The 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act permanently reauthorizes the HOME Investment Partnership Program, whose authorization had lapsed since 1994, and directs HUD to study Build America Buy America impacts on HOME-funded developments.

## Key Points
HOME is one of the oldest federal gap-financing programs for affordable housing production and preservation, but its statutory authorization had technically expired in 1994 even though Congress kept appropriating funds for it year to year. The ROAD to Housing Act closes that gap with permanent reauthorization and adds several administrative updates intended to streamline the program. It also directs HUD to analyze how Build America Buy America domestic-sourcing requirements affect HOME-funded developments and issue clarifying guidance based on the findings. Because HOME is frequently paired with LIHTC as gap financing, permanent reauthorization is being read in the industry as a signal of durable federal commitment to the program rather than a one-time boost.

## Relevance
HOME is exactly the kind of federal gap-financing tool that shows up in Frederick's own affordable housing finance gap discussion. It is worth checking whether Frederick or Frederick County currently draws HOME funds, and if so, whether the BABA sourcing study could affect timelines or costs on any HOME-financed projects in the pipeline. For the Housing Production Target Resolution, permanent reauthorization is a small but real data point supporting the resolution's framing that federal policy is moving toward, not away from, sustained production investment. Less directly relevant to the CDFI/TIF financing recommendations in Section 4, but worth a mention as adjacent federal context.
