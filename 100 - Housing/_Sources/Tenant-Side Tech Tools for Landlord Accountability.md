# Tenant-Side Tech Tools for Landlord Accountability

## Summary
A Shelterforce article surveyed more than a dozen technology tools built specifically to help tenants push back against problematic landlords. These tools address a real power imbalance: landlords have long used algorithmic rent-pricing and opaque tenant screening, but a growing set of tools now gives tenants tools for identifying landlord ownership structures, documenting conditions, and navigating eviction proceedings without an attorney.

## Key Points
Landlords have used rent-pricing software to coordinate above-market rents and tenant screening reports that often include inaccurate data. They also use payment portals that can lock tenants out. The Shelterforce piece by Sandra Larson documents the tenant-side response:

**Ownership transparency tools:** Landlord Mapper and Who Owns What help tenants identify the actual owners behind complex multi-entity business structures, which is common among large landlords trying to avoid accountability.

**Legal self-help tools:** Tenant Power Toolkit (California-focused) and MADE from Greater Boston Legal Services walk tenants through eviction responses step by step. These are designed for the reality that most tenants go to court without a lawyer.

The piece acknowledges these tools do not solve structural power imbalances. Real protections in law, aggressive government monitoring of landlord performance, and a tenant right to counsel are still necessary. But in the interim, these tools matter.

## Relevance
Frederick does not yet have a robust set of tenant-facing resources. As the housing production target work moves forward, the renter protection side of the equation remains underdeveloped. These tools are worth surfacing for Centro Hispano de Frederick and SSCM as community resources, and potentially for a future council communication or constituent resource page. The landlord ownership transparency angle also connects to any future effort to track large landlord concentration in Frederick.
