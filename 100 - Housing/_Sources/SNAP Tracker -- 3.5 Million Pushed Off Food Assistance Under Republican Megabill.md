## Key data points

- **3.5 million people** pushed off SNAP as the Republican megabill (One Big Beautiful Bill Act) is implemented
- **Hundreds of thousands of children** no longer receiving food assistance (confirmed by CBPP)
- The mechanism: transfer of SNAP financial responsibility to states (with work requirement red tape as an additional barrier)
- CBPP is tracking this in real time via a public **SNAP Tracker**: https://www.cbpp.org/research/food-assistance/snap-tracker-people-are-losing-food-assistance-as-the-republican-megabill

## Policy fix identified

Catlin Nchako's recommendation: the federal government needs to press pause on the transfer of SNAP financial responsibility to the states. That would ease short-term suffering while longer-term repeal of work requirements is pursued.

## Why this matters locally

Frederick families are in these numbers. The Golden Mile and East Frederick communities are the populations most exposed to SNAP loss -- the same communities the council's housing, immigration, and equity work is oriented around. As federal safety net programs are dismantled, local government faces increased pressure without increased resources.

This data is useful for: budget and finance framing, advocacy testimony, coalition work with Centro Hispano and SSCM (who are seeing this in casework), and the Infrastructure of Democratic Survival framing (epistemic infrastructure gap -- people need to know their neighbors are losing food access).
