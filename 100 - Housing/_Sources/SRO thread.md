What is an SRO?

SRO stands for Single Room Occupancy. It is a small private room that someone rents, usually with a shared kitchen or bathroom.

SROs can be one of the lowest cost housing options in a city. They are often used by seniors, working adults, and people transitioning out of homelessness.

**2/5**

Why consider SROs in Frederick?

Because we have residents who cannot afford a one-bedroom apartment at current market rents.

SROs can lower monthly costs by reducing private space and sharing common facilities. That tradeoff can make housing attainable for people earning lower incomes.

But affordability does not happen automatically. It depends on the rules we write.

**3/5**

What needs more fleshing out in the draft ordinance?

Several areas:

• How many people share a bathroom or kitchen  
• Property management standards  
• Whether units must be affordable to lower income residents  
• Inspection and enforcement rules  
• Tenant protections

Details determine whether this becomes dignified housing or a problem.

**4/5**

Other important questions:

• Should existing apartments be allowed to convert into SROs?  
• What design standards help buildings fit into neighborhoods?  
• Should supportive services be required, optional, or incentivized?  
• How do we monitor results and adjust if needed?

These are policy choices, not technicalities.
