# Supportive Housing Is Being Asked to Absorb System-Wide Failures

## Summary
The real crisis in supportive housing is not a design flaw in Housing First -- it is that supportive housing has become the default destination for people leaving hospitals, jails, shelters, and emergency rooms because every other part of the care system has shrunk. Programs built for serious mental illness and addiction are now serving people with multiple co-occurring conditions including cognitive impairment, medical frailty, and aging-related decline.

## Key Points
Programs that started with a defined population (serious mental illness, addiction, HIV-related illness) have gradually absorbed populations with broader medical and behavioral complexity than they were financed or staffed to handle.

The surrounding systems that were supposed to provide backup have weakened: fewer psychiatric beds, weakened long-term rehabilitation programs, inadequate addiction treatment capacity. Each reduction pushed more people toward supportive housing as the last resort.

Frontline workers -- often peer specialists and counselors -- are expected to coordinate across the entire care ecosystem for each resident. The ratio of complexity to resources is structurally unsustainable.

The pattern Lieberman identifies is worth internalizing: a promising intervention works, expands, gets loaded with the caseload of failing adjacent systems, deteriorates under the weight, gets blamed for failing, and then gets replaced by something more coercive that also can't work because it doesn't fix the underlying systems either.

This is a general failure pattern for social policy interventions -- not specific to Housing First. Understanding the pattern helps with anticipating how it will play out in other domains.

## Relevance
For Frederick's context: any homelessness or supportive housing program the city or HACF runs will face the same pressures at smaller scale. The preventive argument -- build sufficient surrounding infrastructure before the supportive housing model gets overloaded -- is worth making early.
