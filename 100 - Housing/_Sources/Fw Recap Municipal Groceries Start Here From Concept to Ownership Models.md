# Fw: Recap: Municipal Groceries Start Here: From Concept to Ownership Models

César Díaz

-------- Original Message --------
On Monday, 03/23/26 at 11:28 Alex Birnel <abirnel@localprogress.org> wrote:

> Hey y’all,
>
> Thank you for joining us for the second installment of our municipal grocery series this past Thursday, March 19th, focused on implementation and ownership models. It was great to dig into the nuts and bolts of what it actually takes to move from vision to operation, thinking through everything from full-service municipal stores to nonprofit operators, co-ops, and hybrid models.
>
> This session was really about getting governments back into the business of delivery, not just setting goals around food access, but building the “right fit” institutions that can make muni-grocery real for the places that need it. I’m excited about the continued momentum here and the range of approaches folks are exploring across jurisdictions.
