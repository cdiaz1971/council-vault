# H.R. 1 -- SNAP Work Requirements and State Impact

## Summary

H.R. 1 expands SNAP work requirements and removes prior exemptions for veterans, homeless individuals, and former foster youth. States lose waiver flexibility. Maryland's implementation decisions will affect Frederick residents who rely on SNAP, including many in the Latino community.
