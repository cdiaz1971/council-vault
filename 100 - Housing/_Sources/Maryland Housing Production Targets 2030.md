# Maryland Housing Production Targets 2030

DHCD projects Maryland needs 184,784 additional units by 2030 — nearly double what current permitting rates would produce. Per-jurisdiction targets were published December 31, 2025.

Frederick City has land use authority and will have a specific published target. Jurisdictions meeting targets are eligible for the Maryland Housing Leadership Award; those falling short face scrutiny and potentially stronger preemption arguments from state legislators.

**Frederick action item:** Confirm Frederick City's specific published unit target and assess whether the current development pipeline closes the gap.
