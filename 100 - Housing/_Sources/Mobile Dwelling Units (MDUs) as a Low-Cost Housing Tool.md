# Mobile Dwelling Units (MDUs) as a Low-Cost Housing Tool

From Sightline Institute, January 2025. MDUs are an emerging housing type positioned between ADUs and manufactured housing -- faster to produce, lower cost, and more flexible in siting than conventional construction.

## The Core Idea

MDUs (mobile dwelling units) are factory-built, movable dwelling units designed for permanent or semi-permanent residential use. Sightline frames them as faster to build and significantly cheaper than ADUs, which have proven prohibitively expensive for many homeowners despite broad zoning legalization. MDUs occupy a different cost tier and production timeline, making them a candidate for rapid housing production when speed and cost matter most.

## Why ADUs Alone Aren't Enough

ADU legalization has been a major gentle-density policy win in states like Washington, Oregon, and California. But construction costs -- even for small ADUs -- have remained high enough that many eligible property owners don't build them. The policy is right but the economics don't close. MDUs address this by shifting more of the production to factory settings where labor costs and construction time are substantially lower.

## Policy Relevance for Frederick

Frederick is in the middle of LMC reform. The question of whether MDUs fit within existing or proposed zoning categories -- or require their own definition -- is worth flagging to staff during LMC review. If Maryland allows MDU siting on residential lots (the state preemption question is worth verifying), this could be a low-cost affordability tool that doesn't require capital expenditure from the city.

## Open Questions

Whether Maryland state law preempts local MDU restrictions, or whether Frederick's LMC would need an explicit MDU definition to permit them, is not addressed in this clipping. This is a gap worth researching before proposing anything.
