# Executive Summary
A comprehensive overview of the early childhood education and childcare landscape in Frederick County, Maryland, has been provided. The data includes details about registered family child care providers, licensed child care centers, public pre-kindergarten programs, Head Start programs, educational institutions, and demand for childcare.

# Key Points
- There are 260 registered family child care providers and 116 licensed child care centers in Frederick County.
- A real-time map identifies Frederick County as a childcare desert.
- Most children served are school-age (6 years and older), followed by infants/toddlers, preschoolers, and kindergarteners.
- The majority of families need childcare due to work demands or seeking employment.
- A significant number of family child care providers and child care centers in Frederick County are willing to care for children receiving Child Care Scholarships.
- There is a high demand for full-time care, with a smaller need for part-time care.
- Data collection is done through surveys like the ACS and Census of Population and Housing.
- Childcare can be provided in facilities other than homes, such as schools.

# Action Items
- Address the identified childcare desert in Frederick County.
- Increase funding for child care scholarships to accommodate more families in need.
- Expand public pre-kindergarten and Head Start programs in the county.
- Improve accessibility of child care centers and family providers, especially near schools and employment areas.
- Continue data collection through surveys like ACS and Census of Population and Housing to monitor the childcare landscape in Frederick County.
