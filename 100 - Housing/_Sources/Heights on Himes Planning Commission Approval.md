# Heights on Himes Planning Commission Approval

## Summary
The City Planning Commission approved The Heights on Himes, a 145-unit affordable multifamily project by Conifer Realty on county-owned land at 585 Himes Ave beside the Prospect Center. Full article is paywalled; this note records the approval and the visible facts.

## Key Points
The City of Frederick Planning Commission approved The Heights on Himes on Monday, October 12, 2026, moving the 145-unit affordable multifamily project forward.

Frederick County selected Conifer Realty, a New York-based developer, in December 2025 to build on county-owned land next to the Prospect Center at 585 Himes Avenue. The county keeps ownership of the land.

The site sits beside the Prospect Center, and two bus stops are near it, per the FNP site map caption.

The rest of the article (AMI targeting, any amenities, Planning Commission discussion, public comment) sits behind the FNP paywall and was not captured. Pull it from the Planning Commission minutes or the e-edition if the details matter.

## Relevance
This is in the Golden Mile Plan Area and a project César has already tracked since the March 2026 Conifer LIHTC meeting. The approval moves it from proposal to approved site plan, and the 145 units belong in the pipeline count feeding the annual Housing Production Report that Resolution 26-15 requires, alongside the Prospect Center and Westridge figures in the Golden Mile Small Area Profile. A county land partnership producing deeply affordable units is also a working local example of the "City land disposition" and financing-tool recommendations in Resolution 26-15.
