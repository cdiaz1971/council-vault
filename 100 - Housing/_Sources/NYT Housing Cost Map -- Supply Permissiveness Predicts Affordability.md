# NYT Housing Cost Map -- Supply Permissiveness Predicts Affordability

In May 2026 the New York Times published an interactive map of housing costs relative to area median income across the United States. The pattern the map surfaces is consistent with the existing body of evidence: cities that allow more housing to be built have lower housing costs than cities that limit home construction.

The CITYBUILDER Brief highlights this as part of a "mountain of evidence" that increasing housing supply is the most important factor in lowering rents and home prices. This is not new research -- it joins prior studies on the same core relationship -- but the interactive visualization makes the data accessible for public and policymaker audiences in a new way.

## Why This Matters for César

This is usable evidence for the Housing Production Target Resolution and for any public framing around LMC reform. The argument that supply permissiveness predicts affordability is the strongest empirical foundation for the zoning reform track. The NYT map is a mainstream, credible visualization of the same case César is making in the Frederick context. Worth bookmarking as a public-facing reference.
