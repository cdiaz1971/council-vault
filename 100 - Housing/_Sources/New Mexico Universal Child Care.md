# New Mexico Universal Child Care: Funding Care as a Public Good

## Summary
In 2025 New Mexico became the first state to offer universal child care and found a dedicated way to fund it. Dēmos is hosting a webinar with the organizers on what other states can learn.

## Key Points
Dēmos frames the New Mexico win around revenue, not just values. Treating care as a public good means committing sustained public investment, and there is no single formula. Each state has to pick revenue strategies that fit its own resources, politics, and priorities.

The organizing behind the win came from Organizers in the Land of Enchantment (OLÉ) and the Center for Civic Policy. The webinar, "Public Goods for the Public Good: Winning Universal Child Care in New Mexico," is co-hosted by Dēmos and the State Revenue Alliance on Wednesday, October 14, 2026. It covers how the coalition built power, how it secured the funding, and how the lessons could carry to other states.

## Relevance
Directly relevant to the childcare work in [[Childcare and the Care Economy]] and the peer city options in [[Childcare and Care Economy -- Frederick Peer City Research and Council Options]]. New Mexico is the clearest state level proof that universal care can be funded, and the coalition model (community organizers plus a policy shop) maps onto how housing and tenant work has been built in Frederick.
