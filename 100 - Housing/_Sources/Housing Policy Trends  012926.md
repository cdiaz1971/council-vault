# National Housing Policy Trends Digest  
**Audience:** Frederick City Council  
**Focus:** National housing policy trends (last 6 months) with Maryland applicability section

---

## 1) Federal Legislative Activity on Housing Supply & Affordability

Federal legislative activity continues to emphasize housing supply, zoning reform guidance, and technical assistance rather than direct federal production mandates.

**Key Developments**
- The “ROAD to Housing Act” and related bipartisan proposals direct HUD to develop best practices for boosting housing supply and require reporting on removing land-use barriers.  
  https://ternercenter.berkeley.edu/blog/2026-federal-housing-policy-preview/  
  https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/new-bipartisan-housing-bill-21st-century/808165/

**Implications for Frederick**
- Reinforces the policy direction of using federal guidance to support municipal zoning reform and planning.

---

## 2) HUD Program Updates and Regulatory Shifts

HUD and federal administrative updates continue to shape how affordable housing funds and fair housing enforcement operate.

**Key Developments**
- HUD released new 2025–2026 HOME and Housing Trust Fund value limits, which affect maximum purchase prices eligible for funding.  
  https://www.opendoor.com/articles/hud-releases-2025-2026-affordable-housing-program-updates  
- HUD and affiliated organizations are proposing regulatory changes, including adjustments to disparate impact standards.  
  https://naahq.org/news/federal-regulatory-changes-seek

**Implications for Frederick**
- These administrative rules influence how federal dollars can be used locally and should be incorporated into funding strategies.

---

## 3) Focus on Reducing Regulatory Barriers and Permitting Delays

Federal and policy research have identified regulatory barriers (e.g., zoning restrictions, permitting delays) as a key target for housing policy reform.

**Key Developments**
- Federal guidance and policy analyses emphasize streamlining permitting processes and advancing more permissive land-use frameworks.  
  https://www.huduser.gov/portal/pdredge/pdr-edge-featd-article-120425.html

**Implications for Frederick**
- Aligns with municipal reforms such as administrative approvals, reduced parking requirements, and form-based code updates.

---

## 4) Market and Affordability Conditions Highlight Need for Policy Response

Economic analyses and market reports continue to show affordability challenges and uneven housing market conditions.

**Key Developments**
- Reports from economic research offices highlight ongoing cost burdens and affordability pressures.  
  https://www.marylandcomptroller.gov/content/dam/mdcomp/md/reports/research/housing-economy-print.pdf  
- National market insights suggest shifts in housing access and affordability metrics in 2025.  
  https://www.greystone.com/insights/affordable-housing-in-transition-2025-insights-from-yardi-matrix/

**Implications for Frederick**
- Data reinforces the policy rationale for expanding supply and addressing affordability within municipal planning.

---

## Maryland-Specific Applicability (Last 6 Months)

### A) Governor Moore’s 2026 Housing Agenda

Maryland’s 2026 housing legislative agenda includes proposals tied to transit-oriented development, zoning modernization, and leveraging state land for housing.

**Key Developments**
- Governor Wes Moore announced a statewide housing growth and affordability agenda for the 2026 legislative session.  
  https://governor.maryland.gov/news/press/pages/Governor-Moore-Announces-Housing-Growth-and-Affordability-Agenda-for-2026-Legislative-Session.aspx  
- The Daily Record reports on legislative proposals, including the Maryland Transit & Housing Opportunity Act.  
  https://thedailyrecord.com/2026/01/06/moore-2026-housing-affordability-bills/

**Implications for Frederick**
- Aligns with national trends on zoning reform and transit-related housing policy. Frederick can coordinate local zoning incentives and planning to align with these state priorities.

---

## Summary of Key Trends

1. **Federal legislative proposals** are advancing housing supply frameworks and technical assistance.  
2. **HUD program and regulatory updates** affect how federal housing dollars are used locally.  
3. **Reducing regulatory barriers and permitting delays** is a major policy emphasis.  
4. **Housing affordability pressures** remain acute and continue to influence policy discourse.  
5. **Maryland’s state housing agenda** dovetails with national trends and offers coordination opportunities.
