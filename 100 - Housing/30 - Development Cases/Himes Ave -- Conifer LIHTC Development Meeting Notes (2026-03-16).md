# Himes Ave -- Conifer LIHTC Development Meeting Notes (2026-03-16)

## Summary
Meeting with Victoria Tenable and Matt Ojla from Conifer on March 16, 2026, regarding a LIHTC affordable housing development on Himes Ave adjacent to the Prospect Center and directly behind César's house. 145 units planned with community and commercial space. Timeline estimated at 2028, contingent on LIHTC process.

## Key Details

**Project scope:** 145 apartments, community building(s), and commercial space. Three or more community buildings planned. LIHTC financing. Developer is Conifer.

**Parking variance request:** Conifer will seek a reduction from the standard 1.5 parking spaces per unit to 1.25 to maximize unit count.

**Trail connection:** Possible trail link from Sugarbush neighborhood to the tot lots, community building, and commercial space.

**Design adjustments:** Unit count was reduced due to grading constraints. A parking pavilion was relocated to the community building to assist with financing.

**Traffic:** Himes Ave carries significant fast-moving traffic. César flagged this as a likely source of community pushback. Conifer committed to a traffic study.

**Community engagement:** Conifer committed to robust community input, including outreach to the NAC and Golden Mile Alliance.

## Watch Items

- Track the parking variance application when it comes before Council or Planning.
- Follow up on traffic study results before any formal hearing.
- Monitor NAC and Golden Mile Alliance engagement -- confirm it happens.
- Timeline is 2028 pending LIHTC award; watch for LIHTC application status.

## Relevance

This project is adjacent to César's home, which creates both a direct constituent interest and a potential conflict of interest disclosure obligation depending on the vote posture. Connects to [[Housing Home|Housing]] and the Golden Mile affordability agenda. The parking reduction request and traffic study will likely generate public comment.
