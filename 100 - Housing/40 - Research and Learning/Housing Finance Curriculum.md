# Housing Finance Curriculum

## Goal

Be able to sit across from a developer, housing finance expert, or potential program partner and hold an informed conversation about how Frederick could build or adopt innovative housing finance programs -- including models like Invest Chattanooga, the Montgomery County Housing Production Fund, and similar public finance mechanisms.

Not the expert. The informed principal who can ask the right questions, understand the answers, and push back when needed.

---

## Source Policy

The Google Drive folder contains sample documents that serve as starting material, not the limit of research. Each stage draws freely from the full internet: practitioner publications (Shelterforce, Urban Land Institute, National Housing Conference, CDFI Coalition, HUD), academic primers, case studies from peer cities, YouTube explainers from housing finance professionals, and any other resource that best serves the learning goal for that stage. Suggested videos and external reading lists are part of the curriculum.
