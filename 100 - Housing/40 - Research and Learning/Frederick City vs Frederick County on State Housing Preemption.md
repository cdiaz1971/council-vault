# Frederick City vs. Frederick County Interests on State Housing Preemption

MACo's 2026 housing preview explicitly flags counties as central to the outcome of any state preemption push. Frederick City and Frederick County have historically had jurisdictional friction on development boundaries.

If the state overrides local zoning statewide, Frederick City's ability to manage density, infrastructure capacity, and school impact could be constrained differently than Frederick County's concerns — meaning the city and county may find themselves on opposite sides of the same state legislation.

**Watch for:** County government testimony on Moore's housing bills. Frederick City should not assume Frederick County shares its position.
