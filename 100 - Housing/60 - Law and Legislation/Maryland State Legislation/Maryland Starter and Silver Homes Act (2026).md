# Maryland Starter and Silver Homes Act (2026)

Governor Moore's 2026 bill would allow smaller single-family homes on lots as small as 5,000 sq ft statewide, projecting new homes up to 30% cheaper than current construction.

**Key implication for Frederick:** The bill would override stricter local lot size standards — preemption risk means Council needs to evaluate whether Frederick's current minimums would be superseded and what that means for infrastructure capacity and school impact.
