# Maryland Housing Certainty Act (2026)

Locks in zoning and development rules at the time of project approval, eliminating a city's ability to modify conditions after approval. Also streamlines impact fee collection.

**Key implication for Frederick:** Removes a tool Frederick currently uses to negotiate development agreements. Council should understand which active projects would be affected and whether Frederick's development agreement structure needs to adapt before the bill passes.
