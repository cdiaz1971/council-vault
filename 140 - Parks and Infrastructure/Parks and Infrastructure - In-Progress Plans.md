## Working discipline

For each initiative, record the owner, exact decision sought, dated stage, next action, authoritative document, implementation cost and review date. Move an item out of the planning list when a verified introduced or enacted measure warrants its own project/implementation record. The concept pages' options are analysis, not commitments or newly created legislation.
