# Why the New School for City Builders -- Seth Zeren on Urbanism Education

## Source

Email cross-post via onHousing (Aaron Lubeck's Substack). Minimal body content -- points to podcast episode and Seth Zeren's work.

## What This Is

Seth Zeren launched a podcast called "The New School for City Builders" focused on practical urbanism education -- how to actually build places, not just theorize about them. Aaron Lubeck interviewed him about the project's origins and goals.

The framing is about a gap in urbanism education between academic planning theory and the practical, on-the-ground knowledge needed to develop, permit, finance, and build urban places. Zeren's project is an attempt to democratize that knowledge.
