# Why the New School for City Builders -- Seth Zeren Interview

**Source:** CITYBUILDER NC (Substack), cross-posted from Build the Next Right Thing | June 16, 2026 | Aaron Lubeck interviews Seth Zeren

Aaron Lubeck interviewed Seth Zeren about the motivation behind his podcast *The New School for City Builders*. Zeren's framing centers on the gap between formal planning/architecture education and the on-the-ground, relationship-driven work of actually building cities well.

## Why It Matters

The New School for City Builders is oriented toward practitioners -- people doing zoning reform, infill housing, corridor revitalization -- rather than academic planning theory. Relevant to Frederick's housing production work and LMC reform as both require translating policy into actual built outcomes.
