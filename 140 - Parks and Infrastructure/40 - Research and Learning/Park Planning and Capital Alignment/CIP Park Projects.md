This document summarizes the major **active and programmed Parks & Recreation projects** in the City of Frederick Capital Improvement Program (CIP). These projects represent the City’s current capital commitments for park infrastructure, modernization, and equity-based improvements.

---

## 1. Playground Replacement Program (CIP #410034)
Multi-year citywide program to replace aging playground equipment and surfaces.

Recent and planned sites:
- South End Park
- Cobblestone Park
- Additional parks scheduled based on condition and equity priorities

Focus:
- Safety upgrades
- ADA accessibility
- Modern play equipment

Strategic role:
Ongoing reinvestment in basic park infrastructure across neighborhoods.

---

## 2. Hillstreet Park Renovation (CIP #410039)
Comprehensive renovation of a major west-side park.

Scope includes:
- Restroom and facility upgrades
- Skate park improvements
- Playground replacement
- Field and pathway improvements
- Seating and public amenities

Strategic role:
Large-scale modernization project aligned with equity and community revitalization goals.

---

## 3. Sports Court Replacement & Renovation (CIP #410032)
Rolling program to rehabilitate courts citywide.

Examples of sites:
- College Estates Park – basketball
- Lucas Village Park – skate surface
- Overlook Park – basketball/tennis
- Clerestory Park – basketball
- Lake Coventry Park – basketball

Focus:
- Surface replacement
- Safety and durability
- Expanded recreation capacity

Strategic role:
Preserves existing assets and prevents deferred maintenance.

---

## 4. Park Improvements – Equity Emphasis Areas (CIP #410038)
Targeted park upgrades in underserved neighborhoods.

Includes:
- Facility enhancements
- Amenity improvements
- Accessibility upgrades

Strategic role:
Direct response to Parks & Recreation Master Plan equity objectives.

## 6. Trail & Connectivity Projects (Related CIP)
While managed under transportation or mobility programs, these directly support parks:

- Shared Use Path Plan Improvements
- Rails-to-Trails Program
- North Branch Trail
- Golden Mile Multimodal Access projects

Strategic role:
Expand access to parks and connect neighborhoods through trail networks.

---

## Capital Investment Themes
Current CIP park spending emphasizes:
- Renovation over new park construction
- Safety and accessibility
- Equity-based improvements
- Multi-year asset management programs
- Trails and connectivity

---

## Strategic Observations
- CIP aligns closely with the Parks & Recreation Master Plan priorities of modernization and equity.
- Most projects focus on maintaining and upgrading existing facilities rather than expanding park acreage.
- Trails and greenways are treated as cross-department infrastructure.
- Large projects (Hillstreet Park, playgrounds, courts) dominate near-term capital spending.

---

## Bottom Line
The City’s current CIP reflects a shift toward:
1. System-wide reinvestment in existing parks  
2. Targeted equity projects  
3. Recreation surface and playground renewal  
4. Trail and connectivity expansion  

These projects operationalize the Parks & Recreation Master Plan through concrete capital actions.
