# US 15 US 40 Road Improvement Open House October 15 2026

## Summary
SHA is holding a drop-in public open house on October 15, 2026, 5 to 7 p.m., at Governor Thomas Johnson Middle School on proposed safety improvements to about four miles of US 15 and US 40 between I-70 and MD 26 in Frederick.

## Details
The project covers roughly four miles of US 15 (Jefferson National Pike) and US 40 (Frederick Freeway) from I-70 to MD 26 (Liberty Road). The open house has no formal presentation; attendees view maps and displays and give feedback to SHA staff, which may be used to refine and advance the project. It runs Thursday, October 15, 2026, 5 to 7 p.m., at Governor Thomas Johnson Middle School, 1799 Schifferstadt Blvd., Frederick.

Contacts: John Delaney, Project Manager, 410-545-8053, JDelaney@mdot.maryland.gov. Holly Pearl, Community Relations Manager, 443-202-0934, HPearl@mdot.maryland.gov. Translation assistance is available by emailing SHATitleVI@mdot.maryland.gov with the desired language in the subject line.

## Relevance
The freeway segment runs along the west and north side of the city, and the venue is in west Frederick. It is a chance to see what SHA is proposing before designs harden and to ask how interchange changes affect local streets feeding the Golden Mile and West Patrick Street. The translation contact is worth passing to Centro Hispano and SSCM so Spanish-speaking residents can take part.
