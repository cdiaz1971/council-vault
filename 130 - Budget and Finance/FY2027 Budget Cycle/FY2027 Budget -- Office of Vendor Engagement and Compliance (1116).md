# FY2027 Budget -- Office of Vendor Engagement and Compliance (1116)

## Summary

Brand-new office with no FY2026 budget and no prior actuals, proposed at $529,387 for FY2027. The dominant item is a $300,000 one-time disparity study -- a legitimate and overdue municipal tool for documenting contracting inequity. The office is equity-adjacent and directionally justified, but the structural choice to stand up a full standalone office rather than contract or absorb into Procurement needs scrutiny, and the council should extract a commitment to binding reform before funding the study.

## Department Overview

The Office of Vendor Engagement and Compliance is proposed as a new FY2027 function reporting from the Mayor's cluster. It has one staff FTE, compliance software, and one large one-time contract for a disparity study. There are no prior actuals anywhere in the budget document. This is a Year 1 build-out.

## Key Budget Lines

**Staff salary ($94,962):** One FTE. Benefits bring total personnel cost to roughly $150K.

**Disparity study ($300,000 one-time):** A disparity study documents whether city contracting patterns disadvantage minority- and women-owned businesses (MWBEs). This is a standard municipal equity tool. Many comparable cities have done them. The study is defensible as a starting point. The risk is that disparity studies frequently produce reports that sit on shelves with no binding follow-through. The council should require a follow-up action plan as a condition of funding.

**B2GNow compliance software ($65,161):** $6,069 one-time setup plus $59,092 annual recurring fee. B2GNow is a specialized MWBE contract compliance platform. This is a significant recurring software commitment for a new function -- $59K/yr in perpetuity. Worth confirming competitive procurement process.

## Questions to Ask at Hearing

- What is the timeline and deliverable structure for the disparity study?
- What specific reforms will follow the study -- set-aside percentages, preference points, bonding assistance, prompt payment requirements?
- Why stand up a full standalone office vs. contracting the disparity function or absorbing it into Procurement (1222)?
- Was B2GNow competitively procured or sole-sourced?

## Flags

**Follow the study:** Disparity studies routinely generate reports without binding action. Get a public commitment to specific reforms before the $300K is appropriated.\
**Structure question:** $529K year-one ask for one FTE plus one study plus software is a heavy footprint. The recurring commitment (software + salary + benefits) is roughly $230K/yr after Year 1.\
**Equity inversion context:** This office is being created while the Equity and Inclusion office (1112) is being cut 29% in the same budget. See [[FY2027 Budget -- Equity and Inclusion (1112)]].

## Relevance

From a progressive procurement standpoint, a disparity study and MWBE compliance function are genuinely valuable. Frederick almost certainly has contracting inequity that a study would document. The question is whether this office is structured to produce real change or to produce a report. César should press for binding commitments in the hearing and consider amending the budget to add a reporting requirement back to the council before any procurement reforms are finalized. See [[Budget Brief 2026-04-08|FY2027 Budget -- April 8 Hearing Index]] for full hearing context.
