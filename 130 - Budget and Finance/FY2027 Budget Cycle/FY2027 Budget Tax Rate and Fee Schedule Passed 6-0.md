# FY2027 Budget Tax Rate and Fee Schedule Passed 6-0

## Summary

The FY2027 budget, tax rate, and fee schedule all passed 6-0 at the May 28, 2026 special session. Council Member Joe Adkins was not present for the vote.

## Record

**Date:** May 28, 2026
**Session type:** Special session (budget adoption)
**Vote:** 6-0 on all three items (budget, tax rate, fee schedule)
**Absent:** Council Member Joe Adkins

All three items -- the full FY2027 budget, the tax rate, and the fee schedule -- passed by identical 6-0 margins. No dissent recorded. Adkins's absence is noted but does not affect the outcome.

## Relevance

This closes the FY2027 budget thread that ran through the spring. César's analytical work on the budget -- the equity inversion pattern, the fund balance framing, the priorities letter to Mayor O'Connor -- is documented in the council context note. The 6-0 passage without Adkins suggests no last-minute opposition materialized. The [[Rental Licensing Ordinance and Fee Rate]] thread, which has downstream implications for Housing Fund revenue, remains open as a separate legislative matter.
