# FY2027 Budget -- Mayor's Office (1111)

## Summary

The Mayor's Office is proposed at $1,690,950 for FY2027, up $106,618 (6.7%) from the FY2026 adopted budget of $1,584,332. The increase is primarily salary-driven. The office's professional services and travel/conference spending warrant scrutiny -- combined they approach $430K with limited itemization and significant year-over-year increases in the travel cluster.

## Department Overview

The Mayor's Office covers the Mayor's core staff and administrative operations. FY2026 actuals came in at $1,121,533, well below the $1,584,332 adopted budget, suggesting significant unspent appropriation last year. FY2025 actuals were $1,466,129. The office eliminated a part-time position that appeared in FY2025 actuals ($156K) which is why total salaries look lean despite the increase in full-time productive time.

## Key Budget Lines

**Salaries:** Full-time productive time $675,308 (+5% from $641K). Part-time position eliminated vs. FY2025. Net salary picture is leaner than prior years despite the increase.

**Professional services ($166,100):** Three items -- federal grant consultant ($81,600), legislative services ($75,000), US Conference of Mayors membership ($9,500). The grant consultant line is recurring and unaccompanied by any ROI narrative in the budget document. No grants are cited, no outcomes are listed.

**Travel and conferences ($260K+ combined):** Dues and registration fees $202,262 (WASHCOG alone $108,937, MML $45,100, NLC $6,410, plus $25,000 in unitemized "misc dues and conferences"). Lodging $30,000 (up 50% from $20K adopted). Transportation $15,000 (up 50% from $10K). Meals $7,500 (up 50% from $5K). Every travel line increased 33% with no specific justification in the narratives.

**Equipment rental ($43,200):** Listed as recurring. Zero in FY2025 actuals. No narrative explanation whatsoever.

## Questions to Ask at Hearing

- What is the federal grant consultant ($81,600) delivering -- what grants have been secured and what is the ROI?
- What is the $25,000 misc dues and conferences line actually spent on year to year?
- What justifies a 33% increase across all travel lines simultaneously?
- What does the $43,200 equipment rental cover and why does it have no prior-year actuals?

## Flags

**Value unclear:** Federal grant consultant has no documented output in the budget narrative. This is $81,600/yr with no accountability mechanism visible.\
**No itemization:** The $25K misc dues catch-all is the only line of this size in the cluster without a breakdown.\
**Unexplained:** Equipment rental is a recurring line with no explanation and no actuals history.

## Relevance

This is the first budget hearing of the FY2027 cycle. The Mayor's Office budget sets the tone for executive branch spending priorities. The travel cluster size and the unexplained equipment rental are fair targets for reduction if the council needs to find offsets for additions elsewhere.
