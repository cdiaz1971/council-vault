# Budget and Finance - In-Progress Plans

This page collects proposals and unresolved questions already recorded in the budget and finance wiki. It does not establish approval, introduction, funding, staff assignments, or implementation dates. Current legislative status lives in the Notion Legislative Roadmap. Budget-cycle notes retain the dates and proposal stages of their underlying evidence.

## Priority-driven budgeting and program inventory

[[Priority-Driven Budgeting]] records options for hearing questions, a pilot, full implementation with resident input, and budget-format requirements separating ongoing and one-time resources and reporting outcomes. [[Priority-Driven Budgeting -- Strategic Position]] records the preference to co-champion while Nash controls the pace and proposes a costed General Fund program inventory.

Confirm coordination, existing inventories, staff capacity, and the implementation stage before advancing a request. The April strategy's remaining-hearing references are historical. [[Priority-Driven Budgeting -- Frederick Application]] connects the method to fiscal balance, equity, housing, and capital priorities; its proposal figures and geographic measures need the context notes supplied there.

## Structural balance and reserve reporting

[[City Budget and Fiscal Condition]], [[FY2027 Budget -- Cycle Index]], and [[FY2026 Q3 Budget Update Analysis 2026-08-10]] record questions about recurring costs funded from one-time reserves, unrestricted fund balance, the reserve-policy floor, and multi-year sustainability.

Reconcile proposed versus adopted reserve draws, transfers, and restricted versus unrestricted balances with Finance before using them as evidence for a new budget decision. Obtain current projections rather than carrying FY2026 quarterly or spring FY2027 proposal figures forward as present balances.

## Program outcomes and procurement equity

[[Priority-Driven Budgeting]] records program outcome reporting as an implementation option. The FY2027 analyses of [[FY2027 Budget -- Office of Opportunities and Transformation (1115)]] and [[FY2027 Budget -- Office of Vendor Engagement and Compliance (1116)]] record questions about measurable outcomes, recurring commitments, and disparity-study follow-through.

Establish adopted funding, implementation progress, study deliverables, and reporting arrangements before proposing a further condition or amendment. [[Why Local Governments Still Need Diversity in Public Contracting]] supplies dated background on participation and barriers; it is not a legal determination or an adopted City program design.

## Independent audit and fiscal oversight

[[City Budget and Fiscal Condition]] records independent audit as a Council responsibility. [[FY2027 Budget -- Finance (1221)]] records concerns about the proposed audit allocation, fund-balance reporting, and a software purchase.

Confirm adopted appropriations, audit scope, findings, procurement records, and current follow-through before proposing more resources or reporting requirements. Hearing questions and inferred concerns are not findings that a violation occurred.

## PILOT, TIF, and debt policy

[[Revenue, Taxes, and Debt]] records options for written PILOT and TIF standards, public-benefit reporting, and a debt policy with capacity targets. [[Bond Concepts]] and [[TIF]] provide general learning references; [[Chicago Tax Increment Financing Study Finds Minimal Growth Attribution]] provides dated research coverage from another jurisdiction.

Confirm current agreements, actual performance, outstanding debt, ratings, and available capacity. The older [[Frederick Bond Information]] states an 8% debt limit while the core page states 3%; verify the governing Charter provision and applicable exclusions before using a capacity figure. Preserve the distinction between the City's position and Frederick County's ratings or obligations.

## Revenue options and distributional effects

[[Revenue, Taxes, and Debt]] records options to review business personal property rates and exemptions and advocate for State-authorized local revenue tools. [[FY2027 Budget -- Finance (1221)]] records a question about credit-card processing costs passed to residents.

Confirm present rates, exemptions, fee arrangements, and State authority before evaluating a proposal. Identify who pays by income and neighborhood, and distinguish recurring resources from one-time receipts. [[Maryland Faces a Projected 3 Billion Dollar Structural Budget Gap]] and [[Maryland Is One of Nine States Without Local Sales Tax Authority]] summarize the same August 2026 article; they are not independent confirmation. [[Blueprint Costs Exceed Dedicated Revenue in FY2028]] supplies further dated background on State pressure.
