## Using the tracker

- **Queue** describes attention: Active, Queued, Backlog, Monitoring, Closed, or Unassigned. **Stage** describes the recorded procedural/work stage. Passed legislation can remain Monitoring.
- **Next action** is one concrete step. Set **Review on** when you want it to return to the attention view. Bases displays due reviews; it does not send reminders.
- **Blocker** is empty when none is known. Put context and dependencies in the body.
- **Legal review** is Unknown, Pending, Cleared, or Not applicable. Unchecked Notion flags imported as Unknown; legal review is advisory, not a pipeline gate.
- **Target period** preserves quarter-only planning (FY2027 Q1 means Jul–Sep 2026). Exact target dates supply a computed fiscal quarter when a planning period is absent. A target date is not automatically a passage date.
- Use **Tracker title** for the displayed name. Existing `status` properties describe other note roles and were retained; **stage** is the tracker property.
- After resolving an item, clear **Migration review**, and record the evidence/date in its note. Import dates and source edit dates do not prove a current stage.
- For a new initiative, use [[Templates/Council Initiative]] and set `council_tracker: true`. Link drafts and sources; do not track every draft as a separate initiative.

## Scope and continuity

This migration covers the Notion roadmap, not every Council workstream. [[Council Active Threads]] and [[Council Watch List]] retain wider work, narrative context, and initiatives absent from Notion (including Ranked Choice Voting / Charter Reform, LMC Review Commission, and Data Center ZTA). Keep those notes; adding such initiatives to this tracker requires a deliberate record and evidence rather than automatic inference.

For all records, quarter views, and backlog, open [[Operations/Legislation Tracker/Council Tracker.base|Council Tracker views]]. See [[Council Tracker Migration 2026-10-13]] for the mapping and reconciliation list, and [[Council Tracker - Notion Capture 2026-10-13]] for original properties.
