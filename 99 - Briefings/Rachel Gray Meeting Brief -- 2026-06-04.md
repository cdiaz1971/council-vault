# Rachel Gray Meeting Brief -- 2026-06-04

## Summary

Pre-meeting briefing for City Attorney Rachel Gray on June 4, 2026, covering six items across active pipeline legislation requiring legal action, review, or clarification.

## Items

**1. LMC Review Commission** *(Active Pipeline | Resolution | Ready for Committee)*
Awaiting final draft from legal. This is the most direct legal dependency in the pipeline. Need to know the timeline for the final resolution draft before it can move to Gov Ops Committee. Ask: when can we expect the draft, and are there any outstanding questions from legal on the Taylor companion proposal?

**2. Real Rent Ordinance** *(Active Pipeline | Drafting | Target: June 18)*
Revised draft is complete with pet provisions added. The blocker is the downstream link to Rental Licensing fee rates. Need legal's read on whether to resolve that dependency before introduction or advance independently. The June 18 target is tight.

**3. Housing Production Target Resolution** *(Active Pipeline | Pre-Legislative)*
Drafted, no introduction date set. Get Rachel's read on the legal footing for a housing production target resolution before moving it forward. Confirm any charter or state law constraints on Council setting a numeric housing production target.

**4. E-Bike (EPMD) Ordinance** *(Active Pipeline | Ready to Introduce)*
Status is Ready to Introduce but legal review is the blocker. Check whether Rachel has completed her review and whether any AMAC concerns require a legal response before introduction.

**5. New Council Attorney Position** *(Pre-Legislative | President Letter)*
Vehicle is a President Letter, which implicates how this gets framed relative to Rachel's office. Get a read on her posture before this moves. This conversation may need to happen carefully.

**6. Community Bridge Restoration** *(Closed Session | Litigation Risk)*
Get a status read on where this stands legally before the next public-facing move.
