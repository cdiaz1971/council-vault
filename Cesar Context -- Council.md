# Cesar Context -- Council

## Who César Is

Frederick City Council President, 64th Administration, District 2. Elected November 2025, elected Council President December 11, 2025. See cesar-context for identity and policy priorities.

District 2 covers west and southwest Frederick: Hillcrest, Emerald Farm, Monarch Ridge, West Patrick Street. It lies entirely within ZIP codes 21703 (about 69% of its homes) and 21702 (about 31%), but makes up only a small share of either ZIP code, so ZIP-level data is not a District 2 figure. Majority-minority by combined demographics. Foreign-born population 1.4x the county rate. Poverty rate 1.5x the county rate. Median HHI $91,326 (75% of county median). Poverty rate 10.1%. Residential mobility 14.9%/yr.

## ALICE and Equity Baseline

United Way 2026 report (2024 data). City ALICE rate: 43% vs. 32% county (the 45% figure previously used is Frederick County Election District 2, a county subdivision larger than the City, not City Council District 2). The City rate held while the number of City households below the threshold rose by about 500. County survival budget: $50,868 single adult, $121,080 family of four. Child care ($2,500/month) now exceeds housing ($2,250) for a family with two young children. 75% of single mothers and 44% of senior households are below the threshold; seniors are the largest age group below it. County race/ethnicity: 44% Hispanic, 41% Black, 31% White. From the 2025 report (2023 data, not yet refreshed): 22,067 county children in ALICE/poverty households, 72% of children in rental units, and the veteran gap (35% Black, 30% Hispanic vs. 17% white). See [[ALICE 2026 Report Policy Brief 2026-09-30]].
