# GovTech - In-Progress Plans

This page collects proposals and questions already recorded in the GovTech wiki. These are planning and research options; this index does not establish approval, introduction, funding, assignments, or implementation dates. Check the Notion Legislative Roadmap for current legislative status.

## Technology inventory and municipal strategy

[[Municipal Technology Strategy]] proposes a City technology governance report, a departmental data and workforce assessment, and a technology coordination function.

Before carrying those proposals forward, establish which tools the City uses, what resident data they process, who is accountable, and what strategy and policies already exist. The May synthesis predates the CTO appointment recorded in [[Cesar Context -- Council]]; confirm the existing role and responsibilities before proposing another position.

Decision references: [[AI Governance and Procurement]], [[Digital Services and Accessibility]], and [[Municipal Cybersecurity]].

## AI and technology procurement governance

[[Municipal Tech Consent Architecture -- Contract Language, Audit Standards, and Resident Notification]] proposes vendor access limits, data retention and secondary-use restrictions, audit rights, public notice, and review before significant changes. [[When AI Governs vs When AI Assists Procurement Distinctions for Local Government]] records the proposal to classify uses and draft procurement governance language.

Recorded next questions are whether existing City procurement rules cover these uses, which requirements belong in contracts or policy, and which require ordinance-level action. Confirm the authority and approval process before relying on the older framework's implementation sequence. A public tool inventory should identify purpose and oversight as well as the tool's name.

Decision reference: [[AI Governance and Procurement]]. Police surveillance planning connects to [[Police Surveillance Technology]], [[Governance - In-Progress Plans]], and [[Immigration - In-Progress Plans]].

## Workforce readiness and structured pilots

[[Municipal Technology Strategy]] proposes assessing data, policy, workforce skills, human oversight, and public communication before deployment. [[AI Governance and Procurement]] records training and controlled pilots as options.

Identify a defined service problem, staff capacity, evaluation measures, and human review arrangements before selecting a pilot or committing funding.

## Accessibility and language access

[[Digital Services and Accessibility]] records options for an accessibility audit and remediation plan, accessibility review in purchases, plain-language and multilingual standards, and digital navigator support. [[AI Governance and Procurement]] records translation safeguards and interpreter support.

Establish the City's current compliance and service-access baseline before setting scope or costs. Use [[AI Translation in Government Services -- Minnesota DHS Framework]], [[AI Translation in Government Requires Procurement Discipline, Not Blanket Adoption]], and [[Real-Time AI Translation at Public Meetings -- Government Accessibility Tool]] as source references. Translation tools remain proposals to evaluate; these links do not confirm a City pilot or purchase.

## Cybersecurity oversight and resilience

[[Municipal Cybersecurity]] records options for an annual Council briefing, a dedicated security budget line, contract security terms, participation in State and information-sharing programs, and utility assessments.

Its recorded oversight questions concern certifications, incident response, managed email, vendor dependencies, independent testing, and insurance. Confirm current compliance, program availability, and funding before developing a budget or proposal. Operational details and sensitive findings belong in the appropriate protected review process.

## Public accountability and digital service design

[[Municipal Technology Strategy]] proposes a public technology advisory process and a housing production dashboard. [[Digital Services and Accessibility]] records service dashboards and resident support as options; [[AI Governance and Procurement]] records public inventories and community participation.

Clarify which existing advisory and reporting structures could serve these purposes, who would maintain the data, and how residents could challenge errors. Housing-related proposals connect to [[Housing Home]]; participation connects to [[Civic Participation and Co-Governance]].
