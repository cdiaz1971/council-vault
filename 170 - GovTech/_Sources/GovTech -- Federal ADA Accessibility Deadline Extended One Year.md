# GovTech -- Federal ADA Accessibility Deadline Extended One Year for State and Local Governments

## Summary

The DOJ extended the Section 508 / ADA digital accessibility compliance deadline by one year for state and local governments; the same newsletter covers a new report on how Anthropic's Mythos AI complicates cybersecurity strategy, and an NLC initiative providing AI guidance to local governments.

## Key Points

**ADA / accessibility deadline:** Just days before the digital accessibility compliance deadline was set to take effect, the U.S. Department of Justice filed an Interim Final Rule giving state and local governments a one-year extension. The rule covers WCAG-based digital accessibility requirements under the ADA. This has direct implications for the City of Frederick's web properties, public-facing portals, and any citizen-facing digital tools managed by César's IT team.

**Mythos AI and cybersecurity:** A new report from cybersecurity heavyweights warns that Anthropic's Mythos AI will compress the time between vulnerability discovery and exploit, pushing government cybersecurity teams to rethink defensive postures and operational risk. Small open-source models are reportedly replicating Mythos' vulnerability-finding capabilities, meaning this threat surface is widening. Federal agencies are reportedly using Mythos despite Pentagon blacklisting of Anthropic as a supply-chain risk.

**NLC AI Forum:** The National League of Cities launched a new AI and Emerging Technology Forum to help cities, towns, and villages understand AI tools and deployment. This is a resource worth flagging for Frederick's GovTech strategy.

## Relevance

For César's IT admin role: the one-year reprieve on ADA compliance is actionable. Frederick's public digital properties likely need an accessibility audit before the extended deadline arrives. This is worth adding to the IT work queue.

For the council role: the Mythos AI cybersecurity dynamic is relevant to any policy conversation around municipal AI adoption and procurement. The NLC forum is a direct resource for Frederick's own AI strategy development.
