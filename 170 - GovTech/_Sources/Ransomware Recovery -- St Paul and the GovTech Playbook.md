## Summary

GovTech's June 1 issue leads with St. Paul, Minnesota's ransomware recovery story, framed as a best-practice sharing opportunity for other governments. - Key takeaway: recovery process and decisions are shareable assets for other governments facing similar incidents
- Relevant to Frederick's IT posture and any future incident response planning

**Octave IPO:**
