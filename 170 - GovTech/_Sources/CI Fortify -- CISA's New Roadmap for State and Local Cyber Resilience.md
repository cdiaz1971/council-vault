# CI Fortify -- CISA's New Roadmap for State and Local Cyber Resilience

## Summary

CISA unveiled "CI Fortify" as a framework to help state and local governments secure critical infrastructure against increasing international cyber threats. The framework was covered by GovTech columnist Dan Lohrman, who has covered state and local cybersecurity for years. This is a reference-level note for the work track -- worth knowing the framework exists and following up on the full guidance.

## Key Points

CI Fortify is CISA's current roadmap for state and local critical infrastructure protection. It is designed to help governments of varying sizes and resource levels understand their threat surface and build resilience.

The context of "increasing international cyber threats" reflects the current environment of escalating state-sponsored cyberattacks targeting government systems, utilities, and communications infrastructure.

## Relevance

Relevant to both the M365/Azure sysadmin role (organizational security posture) and to Frederick's municipal technology strategy (city systems security). State and local governments are frequent targets of ransomware and other attacks -- a current CISA framework is a useful professional reference.
