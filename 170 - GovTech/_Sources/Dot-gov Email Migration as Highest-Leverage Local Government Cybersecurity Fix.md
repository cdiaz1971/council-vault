# .gov Email Migration as Highest-Leverage Local Government Cybersecurity Fix

## Summary
South Dakota's SecureSD program treats migrating local governments off unmanaged email onto federally compliant .gov domains as its single highest priority, since more than half of county email addresses currently aren't on one.

## Key Points
Program director Mike Waldner calls moving local governments onto professionally managed, cloud-based email systems that meet federal security standards the "hands down number one priority" of SecureSD, ahead of every other service the program offers. A South Dakota Searchlight analysis of county auditor email addresses found more than half of county-level addresses are not on government domains, which makes them easier to spoof or impersonate. Only verified U.S.-based public sector organizations can obtain a .gov address, and getting one requires advanced security protocols that make official messages harder to fake. Rapid City and Pennington County are both mid-transition to managed government email using SecureSD funding.

## Relevance
This is a concrete, relatively low-cost benchmark, not a large infrastructure ask, and it sits close to your own M365 and cloud administration background even though that work stays separate from the council role. If Frederick or peer Maryland jurisdictions haven't already standardized on managed, verified email domains, this is the kind of specific, checkable fact worth having in hand for a municipal technology governance conversation rather than a general cybersecurity posture question.
