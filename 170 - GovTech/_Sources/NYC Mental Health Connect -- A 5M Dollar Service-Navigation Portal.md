# NYC Mental Health Connect -- A 5M Dollar Service-Navigation Portal

## Summary
New York City's health department launched NYC Mental Health Connect, a questionnaire-driven finder that matches adults and teens to more than 600 mental health and substance use providers by age, language, and insurance. Built with 5 million dollars in one-time funding, it targets the finding that many residents simply do not know where to go.

## Key Points
- **Platform:** NYC Mental Health Connect (mentalhealthconnect.nyc.gov), launched by the NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene and announced by Mayor Zohran Mamdani the week of October 9, 2026.
- **How it works:** a short online questionnaire matches adults and teens to providers by age group, language, and insurance. Searchable directory of outpatient clinics, rehabilitation programs, and crisis services, including support for people with intellectual and developmental disabilities. Filters for local or virtual care and appointment availability.
- **Wraparound links:** connects users to related help for housing, food security, employment, and legal assistance.
- **Scale and cost:** 600+ mental health and substance use providers; 5 million dollars in one-time DHMH funding. Vendor not disclosed.
- **The problem it targets (2024 DHMH report):** 24% of NYC teens who needed or wanted mental health care did not know where to find it; more than 40% of adults struggling to function due to mental health cited "not knowing who to contact or where to go."
- Mamdani: "Finding an accessible, affordable mental health provider should be simple." Commissioner Ashwin Vasan has called for confronting stigma and inequities in care.
- Builds on a 2021 city mental health innovation challenge.

## Relevance
The design lesson is that a navigation layer, not new services, can close a large access gap when the barrier is "where do I go." A Frederick-scale version would sit at the city-county boundary since behavioral health is a county function, so the realistic play is a shared front door with Frederick County Health Department rather than a city build. The wraparound links to housing and food help echo the affordability work: the same residents hit several systems at once. Questions to carry into any GovTech procurement: who maintains provider data, what the questionnaire collects and retains, and whether it meets the ADA digital accessibility deadline.
