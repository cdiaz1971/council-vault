# AI in Civic Services -- Workflow Integration vs Replacement

## Summary

Stub note. Recurring reference in notes about AI adoption in government. The core tension: when a municipality deploys AI in a civic service, is it augmenting a human workflow or replacing a human role -- and how should that distinction shape policy, procurement, and public communication?
