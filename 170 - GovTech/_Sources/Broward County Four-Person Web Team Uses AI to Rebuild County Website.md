# A Team of 4 Rebuilds Broward County, Fla.'s Digital Front Door

## Summary
Broward County's four-person web team is using AI to rebuild Broward.org on the SitecoreAI platform and will modernize the county intranet next. A useful benchmark for how a very small municipal web team can use AI to take on a rebuild that would normally need an agency.

## Key Points
Broward is Florida's second most populous county and has grown more than 20 percent since 2010, yet its public website is run by a web organization of just four people. GovTech reports the team is leaning on AI to handle a large site rebuild and to rethink how residents find information online, with the internal intranet queued as the next project.

The platform side of this traces to a March 2026 procurement in which the county selected SitecoreAI to modernize Broward.org. The county's framing was that official content should be organized once and applied consistently, so that it stays accurate not only on the county site but also when search engines and AI tools summarize or cite it. Site analytics are meant to show what residents search for and where county information is unclear. County CIO Domenic DiLullo tied the effort to trust, and Mayor Mark Bogen pointed to automated maintenance work for the internal team and data-driven decisions.

The newsletter body only carried the headline and a one-line summary of the GovTech feature itself. The detail above comes from the county's public procurement announcement.

## Relevance
Frederick's web presence is a natural comparison. The interesting part is not the vendor but the staffing ratio and the explicit goal of making official information hold up when residents get it secondhand through AI search. That connects to the accessibility and digital services thread in [[Digital Services and Accessibility]], and to the argument in [[Digital Service Teams as Government Modernization Infrastructure]] that small dedicated teams are the actual modernization asset. It is also a caution: a platform choice like SitecoreAI is a long vendor commitment, which belongs in the procurement governance conversation, not just the web team's.
