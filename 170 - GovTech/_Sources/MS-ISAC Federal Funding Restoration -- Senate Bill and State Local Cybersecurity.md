## Summary

The MS-ISAC (Multi-State Information Sharing and Analysis Center) is a federally supported program that has delivered cybersecurity intelligence, tools, and incident response support to state and local governments for roughly two decades. During the second Trump administration its federal funding was cut, creating uncertainty for smaller jurisdictions that rely on it heavily for threat intelligence they cannot otherwise afford.

Sen. Mark Warner of Virginia introduced legislation to restore that funding. The bill is in early stages.

## Key Points

**MS-ISAC and what it does**: Provides threat intelligence sharing, vulnerability assessments, security monitoring, and incident response resources to state, local, tribal, and territorial governments. Particularly valuable for smaller municipalities without dedicated cybersecurity staff.

**Funding status**: Cut under the second Trump administration. Warner's bill would restore it. No timeline or Senate floor status noted in this digest.

MS-ISAC funding restoration is directly relevant to Frederick's municipal technology posture.
