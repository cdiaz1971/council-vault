## Summary

Two items from the May 30 GovTech digest converge on AI for permitting:

Avolve (sponsored content) claims up to 30% of electronic plan review submissions fail early automated checks, and frames AI as a mechanism to catch errors before they cause review delays. The pitch is targeted at municipalities with electronic plan review workflows.

Separately, an AI studio described as offering "free guidance" to cities and counties interested in using AI to streamline permitting -- allowing officials to learn about the technology's potential for agency-specific problems before committing to a vendor relationship.

Neither story included independently verified figures on permitting time reductions or cost savings.

## Why This Matters for César

Frederick has active interest in permitting modernization as a housing production lever. Pre-approved architectural plans and electronic permitting have already been flagged as supply-side tools in the housing stack. The question of whether AI can meaningfully reduce permitting friction -- and at what governance risk -- is live. Worth distinguishing vendor-sponsored claims from independent research before citing in policy discussions.
