# Stryker Cyber Attack -- Iran-Linked Threat to State and Local Government

## Summary

A recent Iran-linked cyber attack on Stryker prompted government cybersecurity discussions about state and local exposure. The incident raised questions about supply chain risk and the resilience of public sector systems against adversarial nation-state threats.

## Key Points

The attack is notable for its origin (Iran-linked, tied to broader geopolitical conflict) and its target (a medical device and defense contractor), which underscores that supply chain attacks on private sector entities create downstream risk for government partners. GovTech's coverage notes that the incident dominated a recent gathering of public and private sector cybersecurity experts, where AI and critical infrastructure gave way to frontline defense discussions.

Key themes from the coverage: state and local governments are increasingly in scope for sophisticated threat actors, particularly those targeting critical infrastructure adjacent industries. Building cyber resilience -- not just prevention but recovery -- was the dominant takeaway.
