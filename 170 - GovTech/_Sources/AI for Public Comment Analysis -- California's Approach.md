# AI for Public Comment Analysis -- California's Approach

California is supplementing its traditional public comment process with AI tools designed to extract actionable insight from large volumes of citizen feedback. The model is intended to help government staff identify patterns, prioritize substantive input, and reduce the burden of manual review -- while preserving the legitimacy of the public participation process.

The approach is notable because it treats AI as an augmentation layer on top of existing democratic process rather than a replacement for it. The question of how to handle public comment volume at scale is a real problem for local governments, not just states.

## Why This Matters for César

Frederick's public engagement processes -- comment periods, public hearings, online submissions -- generate feedback that is rarely synthesized systematically. As Council President, César shapes which voices get heard and how staff summarizes public input. AI-assisted comment analysis could be a concrete GovTech proposal worth exploring, particularly as the city considers participatory budgeting or major zoning changes that attract high comment volume.
