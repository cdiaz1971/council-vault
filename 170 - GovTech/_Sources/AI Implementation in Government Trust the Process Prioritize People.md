# AI Implementation in Government Trust the Process Prioritize People

## Summary

Practitioners at the California Public Sector CIO Academy offered three key principles for government AI implementation: ensure repeatability before production, identify service challenges early, and confirm leadership alignment.

## Key Points

From the California Public Sector CIO Academy panel on AI in government:

Before moving an AI use case into production, practitioners advised asking whether the method is repeatable. One-off demos that cannot be reliably reproduced at scale are a common failure point in public sector AI pilots.

Identifying service delivery challenges early means scoping AI projects around real operational pain points rather than novelty. Agencies that start with a problem to solve rather than a technology to deploy have better outcomes.

Leadership alignment was flagged as a prerequisite, not an afterthought. If executive sponsors are not clearly committed, AI initiatives stall during procurement, legal review, or change management.
