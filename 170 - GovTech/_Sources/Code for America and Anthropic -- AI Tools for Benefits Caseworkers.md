# Code for America and Anthropic -- AI Tools for Benefits Caseworkers

Code for America and Anthropic have launched a pilot aimed at equipping government caseworkers with AI tools to more efficiently administer public benefits. The goal is to help staff navigate complex, frequently updated policy landscapes without requiring deep expertise in every program -- reducing errors, improving consistency, and freeing caseworker time for direct client support.

The partnership is a concrete example of civic tech organizations using frontier AI models for human services delivery rather than automation-for-automation's-sake. The framing centers on staff augmentation, not replacement.

## Why This Matters for César

Frederick City government administers or connects residents to a range of public benefits programs. This model is directly relevant to the immigrant services and social services work César tracks through Centro Hispano and SSCM -- both of which deal with clients navigating complex eligibility landscapes. It also intersects with the GovTech track: what does it look like when a small city government considers AI tools for human services staff?

Worth watching as a peer model. If Anthropic is partnering with Code for America, there may be paths for smaller municipalities to access similar tooling.
