## Summary

Colorado CIO David Edinger departed his role amid a significant restructuring of the state's Office of Information Technology. The reorganization cut more than 170 positions and was characterized by state leadership as a move toward improved service delivery under new leadership.

The GovTech digest framed this alongside a broader trend piece on internal digital service teams -- the argument being that cities and states that build internal technical ownership and workflows survive modernization better than those dependent on vendor relationships. Three unnamed cities were cited as cases where internal teams got through the hardest parts of modernization: hiring people, building workflows, and establishing technical ownership.

Also in the same digest: a pending Trump executive order on AI and cybersecurity, and an AI vendor offering free permitting-modernization support to municipalities.

## Why This Matters for César

Colorado's IT restructuring is a peer-state signal worth watching. Large-scale gov IT leadership churn often precedes either genuine modernization or vendor dependency cycles. The internal-team-vs-procurement framing is directly relevant to Frederick's GovTech and M365 Copilot deployment questions.
