# Brehm Text Amendment Filing ZTA Draft 2026-10-08

## Summary

Council President Díaz and Council Member Brehm drafted an amendment to LMC Sec. 306 (Text Amendments). Filing authority would be limited to an elected City official or the Planning Division, removing City residents and City property owners. In the October 22 packet version, every application goes to the Council at a public hearing, where the Council approves or denies it by majority vote before the Planning Commission. A later revision replaces that with a non-voting Council review at a public meeting scheduled by the Council President, with Division filings requiring only notice. The Planning Commission hearing is scheduled after the review step. "Board of Aldermen" becomes "City Council." Zoning map amendments are unchanged.

## Key points

- Stated rationale: the volume of developer-initiated text amendments, limited public visibility, lack of early Council input, and Planning staff time spent on proposals that are not viable.
- The justification describes Council approval before Planning Commission review, in both versions, though the revision provides for discussion with no vote.
- No deadline for scheduling the Council review, and no transition rule for pending private applications, including PC25-626ZTA.
- Peer practice: Frederick County Code § 1-19-3.100.2 provides that the County Council initiates zoning text amendments, and in Montgomery County the District Council introduces ZTAs.
- Scheduled for the Thursday, October 22, 2026 Council workshop.
