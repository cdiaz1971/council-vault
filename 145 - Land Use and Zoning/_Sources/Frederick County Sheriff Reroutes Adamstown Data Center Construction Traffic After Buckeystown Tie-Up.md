# Frederick County Sheriff Reroutes Adamstown Data Center Construction Traffic After Buckeystown Tie-Up

## Summary

Prompted by a traffic tie-up in Buckeystown, Frederick County Sheriff Chuck Jenkins reached a new agreement with the companies developing the Adamstown-area data center campus to route construction traffic through New Design Road instead, citing hundreds to thousands of daily truck trips that most residents don't realize occur.

## Key Points

- Sheriff Chuck Jenkins said he reached a new agreement with the companies developing the data center campus near Adamstown, following a traffic tie-up in Buckeystown several weeks before the October 17, 2026 article, to route construction traffic through New Design Road rather than through Buckeystown.
- Jenkins, in an interview: "Unless you live in Adamstown, Buckeystown, and you see the amount of truck traffic -- hundreds, even maybe thousands of truck trips a day in and out of these construction sites -- people don't have any idea how enormous this is."
- A photo caption notes trucks observed turning from Manor Woods Road onto Buckeystown Pike.
- Note on sourcing: the Frederick News-Post article is paywalled beyond the lede and this quote; the full article (additional construction-traffic routing detail, company names, and timeline specifics) was not accessible during triage and would need the News-Post subscription or e-edition to read in full.

## Relevance

A direct, named, Frederick County example of data center construction's physical community impact (truck traffic volume severe enough to require active sheriff intervention and rerouting), relevant to the [[Data Center Resource and Community Impacts]] page and to the PC25-626ZTA Data Center ZTA opposition record tracked on [[Council Watch List]]. Useful as a concrete, quantifiable (if rough) local data point -- "hundreds, even maybe thousands of truck trips a day" -- for the kind of construction-phase impact that zoning conditions or a text amendment could address directly, distinct from the more frequently cited water/power/ratepayer impacts.
