# Text Amendment Filing Submitted Version 2026-10-26

## Summary

The final version of the § 306 amendment, dated 10/26/2026, is the non-voting version presented at the October 22 workshop with one cleanup in the Commission hearing subsection and a change from the Mayor to the City Council President for receiving the Commission recommendation and scheduling the Council hearing. César confirmed it is submitted without further changes. Next stop: Planning Division, then Planning Commission public hearing.

## Key points

- Filing: "An elected City official; or The Division." Residents and property owners struck.
- An official's application goes to a public Council meeting scheduled by the Council President; no vote.
- Division applications: notice and overview to Council.
- Commission hearing after the review step; recommendation forwarded to the Council President, who schedules the Council hearing "as soon as practicable."
- No scheduling deadline, transition rule, or sponsorship disclosure in the text; these are to be handled through testimony, Council Rules, and practice.
- Supersedes the pipeline-table description in the Land Management Code page, which says "Presented at the October 22, 2026 workshop."
