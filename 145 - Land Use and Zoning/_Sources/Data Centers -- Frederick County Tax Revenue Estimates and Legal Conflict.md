# Frederick County Data Center Tax Revenue Estimates

## Summary

A Frederick County official cited an estimate of $200M+ in annual tax revenue from a single data center project -- roughly 40% of the county's overall budget -- while a landowner dispute signals an impending legal battle with the county.

## Key Points

Speaking at the state level, a Frederick County representative noted the county stands to receive estimated annual tax revenues exceeding $200 million from a single data center construction project -- a figure representing approximately 40% of the county's total budget. This is a striking number that local advocates for data center development lean on heavily when countering community opposition.

At the same time, a landowner (identified as Bauer in the article) stated she expects a legal battle with Frederick County over the project. The nature of the dispute was not fully detailed in the excerpt, but it suggests that not all property owners in affected areas are aligned with county government on development terms.

## Relevance

This is directly relevant to Frederick's active data center policy track. The $200M+ annual revenue figure is likely to come up repeatedly in council debates and public testimony as a justification for development. Understanding the basis and assumptions behind that estimate -- and who is asserting it -- matters before taking a position. The pending legal conflict with a landowner is also worth tracking as it could affect project timelines or create political complications for the county.
