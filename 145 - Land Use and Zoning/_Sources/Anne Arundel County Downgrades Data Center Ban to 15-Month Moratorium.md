# Anne Arundel County Downgrades Data Center Ban to 15-Month Moratorium

## Summary

The Anne Arundel County Council voted unanimously on Monday, October 5, 2026, to impose a 15-month moratorium on data center development, scaling back an earlier bill that would have banned the facilities outright. The moratorium expires in January 2028 unless a study group finishes its recommendations sooner.

## Key Points

The original bill, Bill 63-26, was introduced by District 3 Republican Nathan Volke as an outright ban on data centers. District 4 Council Member Julie Hummer introduced the amendment that downgraded the ban to a time-limited moratorium, at the request of County Executive Steuart Pittman. The omnibus, amended version passed unanimously.

The bill establishes a work study group that will research data center best practices in other jurisdictions and make recommendations on permanent legislation to the county executive and council. Hummer said that if the study group finishes its report before the 15-month period ends, the moratorium could lift early. Absent new legislation, it expires in January 2028.
