# Sanders and AOC Propose Federal Data Center Moratorium

## Summary

Senators Bernie Sanders and Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez proposed legislation in late March 2026 that would ban all new data center construction until federal AI regulation is enacted. This is the first major federal legislative attempt to use land use and infrastructure as a lever to force AI governance.

## Key Points

- Proposal: moratorium on new data center construction conditioned on passage of federal AI regulation
- Sponsors: Bernie Sanders and AOC
- Mechanism: infrastructure restriction as a policy lever, rather than direct AI regulation
- Context: emerges during a period of rapid data center expansion driven by AI compute demand

## Relevance

Directly relevant to Frederick's [[Data Center Policy]] work and the broader [[LMC Review]]. The Sanders/AOC proposal validates the instinct that land use and permitting are legitimate levers for shaping AI infrastructure buildout at the local level -- Frederick is already navigating this. It also signals that federal legislative attention on data centers is increasing, which may shift the political landscape for local ordinances. A federal moratorium, if passed, would obviously supersede local permitting, but more likely this proposal generates debate that gives local governments political cover to enact their own restrictions or conditions.

Gap: no note yet on the current status of federal AI regulation proposals that this moratorium would be conditioned upon. Worth tracking.
