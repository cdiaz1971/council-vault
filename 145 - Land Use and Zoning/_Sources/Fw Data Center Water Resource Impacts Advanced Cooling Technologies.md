Dear President Díaz and Members of the City Council,

In the coming months, as the City Council deliberates the proposed zoning text amendment that would allow data centers as a "by right" use in M1 and M2 zones within the City, the impact of data center operations on fresh water resources is an issue that merits careful consideration. In that regard, alternative more sustainable technologies that can significantly reduce or eliminate data center reliance on fresh water for cooling are now commercially available and in use.

The attached research memorandum summarizes the impact of data centers on water resources in the Potomac River Basin, alternative cooling technologies, and state regulatory actions to require the use of advanced liquid cooling systems. We hope that you find this information helpful. Please let us know if you have any questions.
