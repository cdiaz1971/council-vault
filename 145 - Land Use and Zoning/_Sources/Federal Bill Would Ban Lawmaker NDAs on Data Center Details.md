# Federal Bill Would Ban Lawmaker NDAs on Data Center Details

## Summary
Rep. Tom Barrett (R-Mich.) introduced the No Data Center NDAs Act, prohibiting sitting members of Congress from signing non-disclosure agreements that would prevent them from discussing possible or planned data center locations and resource consumption in their districts.

## Key Points
The bill responds to a pattern where lawmakers have signed NDAs tied to data center projects, limiting what they can tell constituents about locations and resource impact. If enacted, the House Ethics Committee and the Senate Select Committee on Ethics would implement and oversee the new restrictions. It's a transparency measure aimed specifically at elected officials, not at companies or local governments.
