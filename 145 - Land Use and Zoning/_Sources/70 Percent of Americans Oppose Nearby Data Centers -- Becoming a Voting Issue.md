# 70 Percent of Americans Oppose Nearby Data Centers -- Becoming a Voting Issue

## Summary
70% of Americans say they don't want a data center near them, and roughly $130 billion in data center projects were delayed or killed in three months, signs the issue is becoming a mainstream voting concern rather than a niche local fight.

## Key Points
- A recent poll found 70% of Americans don't want a data center built anywhere near them.
- Roughly $130 billion in data center projects were delayed or killed over a three-month span, per the same reporting.
- The AI Exchange newsletter frames this explicitly as "turning into a voting issue," a shift in the political salience of data center siting.
- This came up as a side note in a newsletter primarily about EU AI oversight law, underscoring how data center backlash is now surfacing across general AI and tech coverage, not just local or trade press.
