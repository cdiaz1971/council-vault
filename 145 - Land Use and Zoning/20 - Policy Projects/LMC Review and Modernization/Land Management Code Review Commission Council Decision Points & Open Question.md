Open questions from the [[LMC Review]]
## 1. Threshold & Intent

- Is Council aligned on **why this review is happening now**, and which specific problems in the current Land Management Code should be prioritized?
    
- What risks is Council willing to accept if no comprehensive review occurs in the next 5–10 years?
    
- How will Council define **"success"** at the conclusion of the Commission's work?
    

---

## 2. Commission Role & Authority 

[[LMC Review#Commission Role & Authority]]

- Will the Commission be **advisory only**, or advisory with **formal policy recommendations**?
    
- Is the Commission explicitly authorized to **propose new policy**, or limited to reviewing existing code provisions?
    
- What level of expectation does Council have regarding draft outputs (e.g., conceptual recommendations vs. redlined code language)?

    

---

## 3. Relationship to Existing Bodies

- How should the Commission formally interact with:
    
    - Planning Commission
        
    - Historic Preservation Commission
        
    - Board of Zoning Appeals
        
- What is the preferred cadence and format for **Council updates**?
    
- How many Council liaisons should be assigned, and what authority or role should they have?
    

---

## 4. Membership & Appointments
[[LMC Review#Membership & Composition]]

- What is the **optimal size** of the Commission to balance inclusivity with manageability?
    
- Are any **minimum qualifications or experience requirements** necessary, or is perspective-based representation sufficient?
    
- How will geographic balance across the City be ensured?
    
- What conflict-of-interest standards will apply, and how will they be disclosed and enforced?
    
- Should Council retain final approval over appointments, or delegate appointment authority?
    

---

## 5. Scope Boundaries

- Is the Commission expected to review the **entire Land Management Code**, or are certain sections presumptively out of scope?
    
- How should competing guidance documents (e.g., small-area plans versus citywide goals) be reconciled?
    
- What weight should climate, mobility, and equity plans carry relative to the Comprehensive Plan?
    

---

## 6. Process Design

- What meeting frequency is realistic and sustainable for volunteer Commission members?
    
- Should meetings be required to be **fully public and in-person**, or is hybrid participation acceptable?
    
- What level of **staff support** can realistically be committed without disrupting existing operations?
    
- Should the Commission Chair be elected internally or appointed by Council?
    

---

## 7. Housing, Equity, & Development Continuity

[[LMC Review#Housing & Equity Considerations]]

- What explicit **housing supply, affordability, and equity metrics** should be used to evaluate recommendations?
    
- How will Council ensure the review process is outcome-driven and not merely performative?
    
- Should development continue under existing rules during the review, or should Council explore:
    
    - A partial moratorium
        
    - Targeted pauses on specific approvals
        
    - No change to current development processes
        
- What legal, economic, and housing-supply risks accompany each option?
    

---

## 8. Deliverables & Accountability

[[LMC Review#Process & Timeline]]

- What is the **minimum acceptable final product**: policy report, prioritized recommendations, or draft amendments?
    
- Is an **interim report** required to inform Council decisions before the final deliverable?
    
- Should cost, staffing, and implementation impacts be mandatory components of recommendations?
    
- Under what conditions, if any, should Council extend the Commission beyond its initial term?
    
- How should minority or dissenting views be documented and preserved?
