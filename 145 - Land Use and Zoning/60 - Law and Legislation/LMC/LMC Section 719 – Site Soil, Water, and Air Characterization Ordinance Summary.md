## Purpose
- Protect future health of Frederick City residents by ensuring soils, surface and groundwaters, and air quality on properties to be developed or redeveloped meet Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE) contaminant thresholds.  
- Applies to unused, underutilized, vacant, abandoned, historically industrial/commercial, or waste disposal sites.

## Jurisdiction
- Applies within Frederick City limits, excluding Fort Detrick (US Army installation).

## Key Definitions
- **Phase I Assessment:** Initial environmental review documenting historical/current site use and possible contamination.  
  - Reference: [MDE Phase I Guidance](https://mde.maryland.gov/programs/land/MarylandBrownfieldVCP/Documents/VCP%20Guidance%20Phase%20I%20FINAL_05-31-2018.pdf)
- **Phase II Assessment:** Detailed site characterization if Phase I identifies potential contamination.  
  - Reference: [MDE Phase II Guidance](https://mde.maryland.gov/programs/land/MarylandBrownfieldVCP/Documents/VCP%20Guidance%20Phase%20II%20ESA%20FINAL_08-23-2017.pdf)
- **Response Action Plan (RAP):** Plan for remediation of contaminated sites, reviewed by MDE-VCP and City Planning, with public input.  
  - Reference: [MDE RAP Guidance](https://mde.maryland.gov/programs/land/MarylandBrownfieldVCP/Documents/VCP%20Guidance%20RAP%20FINAL_08-23-2017.pdf)
- **No Further Requirements Decision (NFRD):** MDE-VCP decision clearing a site for development or redevelopment.

## Applicability
- Properties: vacant, underutilized, abandoned, historically industrial/commercial, or waste disposal.  
- Includes areas receiving surface or groundwater from nearby sites.

## Procedural Requirements
1. **Phase I Assessment**
   - Conducted by independent consultant.  
   - Submitted to City Planning and MDE-VCP.
   - Documentation must reference historical and current uses and potential contaminants ([MDE Cleanup Standards](https://mdewin64.mde.state.md.us/LRP/index.html)).
2. **MDE-VCP Review**
   - Acceptance triggers Phase II assessment if needed.  
   - No City processing or site activity until NFRD or COC issued.
3. **Phase II Assessment**
   - Conducted by qualified independent consultant if Phase I identifies issues.  
   - Results submitted to MDE-VCP and City Planning.
4. **RAP Submission and Implementation**
   - Required if MDE-VCP identifies need for mitigation.  
   - Public review required prior to approval.  
   - Implementation overseen by MDE-VCP project manager.
5. **Application Validity**
   - Site activities and city processing halt until NFRD or COC.  
   - Withdrawal from VCP invalidates application; no site activity permitted.

## Enforcement
- Compliance ensured via MDE-VCP oversight.  
- City Planning will not proceed beyond Master Plan acceptance until MDE approvals are secured.

## Discussion / Council Considerations
- Impact on development timelines and potential delays due to MDE-VCP review.  
- Resources for City Planning staff to track compliance and communicate with applicants.  
- Public involvement in RAP review – process and transparency requirements.  
- Potential fiscal impact if applicants challenge or delay mitigation requirements.  
- Exclusion of Fort Detrick – federal vs. municipal authority considerations.
