# Land Use and Zoning - In-Progress Plans

This index gathers planning questions from existing wiki notes. It does not establish current legislative status or record new Council decisions. Legislative status for anything that moves forward lives in the [[Council Tracker]].

## LMC Review and Modernization

**Working materials:** [[LMC Review]]; [[Land Management Code Review Commission Council Decision Points & Open Question]]; [[LMC Review Commission -- Draft Resolution Analysis -- 2026-04-12]]; [[LMC Modernization -- Libby Taylor Proposal Analysis -- 2026-04-12]].

**Open decisions:** Scope of review; commission membership and relationship to existing bodies; staffing and consultant resources; regulatory model; housing, affordability, and equity measures; and whether any development pause belongs in the approach.

**Next evidence needed:** Latest proposal and resolution text; current action recorded in the Legislative Roadmap; appointment authority and the effect of housing vesting rules; available staff capacity and funding; and an evaluation framework that measures affordability and distributional outcomes.

Decision references: [[Land Management Code]], [[Zoning Reform]], and [[Council Process and Boards and Commissions]]. The December 2025 and April 2026 notes retain their original proposal context.

## Environmental Site Assessment

**Working material:** [[Brownfield Text Amendment Review -- LMC Sec. 744]], reviewing the April 2026 Council-sponsored draft.

**Open questions:** Assessment standards and qualifications; scope of the major site plan trigger; adequacy review; continuing obligations and enforcement; public disclosure; interaction with MDE programs; and cost implications for smaller projects.

**Next evidence needed:** Latest draft and section number; confirmation that identified drafting issues have been addressed; Planning and Legal review of implementation and State-program interaction; and a comparison with the earlier Section 719 proposal.

Earlier references: [[LMC Section 719 – Site Soil, Water, and Air Characterization Ordinance Summary]], [[Potential Gaps and  Concerns – LMC Section 719]], and [[Questions for Proponents – LMC Section 719]]. These describe an earlier version rather than interchangeable requirements of Section 744.
