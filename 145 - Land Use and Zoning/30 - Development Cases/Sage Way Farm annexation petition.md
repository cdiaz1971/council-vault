Property owners Ajay and Renu Prakash have petitioned for Sage Way Farm at 5509 Old National Pike to be annexed into the city of Frederick. The farm is currently zoned as agricultural under Frederick County code. The request is for approximately 25 acres near Frederick's western border for high-density residential development.
