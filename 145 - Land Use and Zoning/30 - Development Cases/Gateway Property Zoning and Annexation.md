# Gateway Property Zoning and Annexation

## Summary
Zoning Map Amendment (PC24-984ZMA) and Annexation Resolution amendment (PC24-983ANX) for the Gateway Property Special Events Center and cricket stadium both passed August 6, 2026, rezoning 24.33 acres to Institutional and revising 2009 annexation terms.

## What Passed
Zoning Map Amendment (PC24-984ZMA) rezoned 24.33 +/- acres of the Gateway Property from General Commercial (GC) to Institutional (IST), enabling a Special Events Center for cricket matches and other community and cultural events. Passed 5-1. Adkins voted no. Taylor was absent.

Annexation Resolution amendment (PC24-983ANX) amended Resolution 09-23, the original 2009 annexation of 52 +/- acres known as the Gateway Property. Passed 6-0.

## Project Detail
Location is the east side of the city, north of Route 144, south of I-70, bounded by the Monocacy River. Applicant and developer is AcerInfra, LLC, contract purchaser of four parcels totaling 48.64 +/- acres. Proposed use is a stadium with 7,000 to 10,000 seat capacity, reaching up to 25,000 for occasional professional or international matches, for cricket instruction, practice, and competition, plus cultural and community events. Operating season runs roughly April through October.

Annexation amendment terms:

Paragraph 7(a), transportation contribution, raised from the original $500,000 to $1.5 million. $500,000 due within 30 days of rezoning approval or by August 30, 2026, whichever comes first. The remaining $1 million is due within two years of the first payment. No APFO credit applies against this contribution.

Paragraph 7(b), floodplain and parkland, replaces the original 16 acre dedication requirement with a qualitative approach. Roughly 14 acres along the Monocacy River will be made available for public recreational use, including a boat launch and a 10 foot path, subject to a parkland improvement site plan approved by the Recreation and Parks Commission and the Planning Commission. The City retains discretion over fee simple dedication versus easement.

The Events Management Plan (EMP) is required for all events regardless of size. Approval authority is assigned to the Chief Operating Officer rather than Planning, with annual renewal required by April 1.

## Concerns on Record
An internal infrastructure impact analysis flagged several risks ahead of the vote. The IST floating zone removes standard site plan and conditional use oversight. FEMA floodplain mapping shows significant overlap between the proposed field and 100 year and 500 year flood hazard areas. The transportation contribution, even raised to $1.5 million, may still undervalue needed improvements at the I-70 Exit 56 interchange, which has hazardous geometry. The proposed 500 to 833 parking spaces fall well short of standard ratios for a 25,000 patron venue, and offsite parking suggestions may violate LMC 607(d)(2)(A)'s 1,320 foot limit on offsite parking distance.

## Next Action
Monitor EMP approval and annual renewal under COO authority, the transportation contribution payment schedule ($500,000 due by August 30, 2026 or the close of the appeals period, balance due within two years), and the parkland improvement site plan process before the Recreation and Parks Commission and Planning Commission.
