# Week 1: What Zoning Is and Why It Exists

This is the index note for week 1. Start here, then work through the linked notes below. Together they're built to fill the full 1-2 hour weekly budget, not just this page.

## Summary
Zoning is a legal tool that lets local governments regulate how land is used, built on, and occupied. Its constitutional basis is the police power, the authority of state and local governments to regulate for public health, safety, and general welfare. The first comprehensive US zoning ordinance was adopted by New York City in 1916, and it became the template nearly every American city, Frederick included, still traces its own zoning authority back to.

That's the short version. The full picture has more moving parts than fit in a five-minute read, which is why this week branches into five detail notes.
