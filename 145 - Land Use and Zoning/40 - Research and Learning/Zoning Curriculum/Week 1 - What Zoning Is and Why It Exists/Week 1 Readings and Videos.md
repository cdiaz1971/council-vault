# Week 1 Readings and Videos

Work through these in roughly this order. Total time, done thoroughly, lands around 75-100 minutes, which is the point.

## Primary source (15 min)
**The 1916 Building Zone Resolution**, NYC Department of City Planning
https://www.nyc.gov/assets/planning/downloads/pdf/zoning/zoning-nyc/1916_zoning_resolution.pdf
The actual adopted text. You don't need to read every article. Skim Article I (definitions) and the district/height provisions to see how plainly, and how briefly, the whole system was originally written.

## Core essay (20 min)
**"Revisiting 1916 (Part I): The History of New York City's First Zoning Resolution,"** Building the Skyline
https://buildingtheskyline.org/revisiting-1916-i/
The most rigorous account available online. Directly challenges the Equitable Building origin myth using the original commission report. This is the piece [[The 1916 NYC Zoning Resolution]] draws from most heavily.

## Overview essay (10 min)
**"One Hundred Years of Zoning,"** AIA New York
https://www.aiany.org/membership/special-projects/article/zoning-100/one-hundred-years-of-zoning/
Shorter and more architectural in focus. Good for seeing how the 1916 rules physically shaped Manhattan's skyline.

## Background essay (10 min)
**"Zoning in the United States,"** Wikipedia
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoning_in_the_United_States
Not a primary source, but a well-sourced overview that covers the pre-1916 racial ordinances and Buchanan v. Warley. Supports [[Zoning's Racial and Exclusionary Origins]].

## Framing essay (10 min)
**"A Brief History of Zoning in America, and Why We Need a More Flexible Approach,"** Manhattan Institute
https://manhattan.institute/article/a-brief-history-of-zoning-in-america-and-why-we-need-a-more-flexible-approach
Written from a deregulatory perspective, worth reading with that lens in mind. Cites Seymour Toll's Zoned America on the class motives behind the 1916 ordinance.

## Video (5-8 min)
**"A Brief History of Zoning"**
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M6pytpA1BCs
Timeline format covering the 1916 ordinance, the 1924 Standard State Zoning Enabling Act, and Euclid v. Ambler in sequence. Useful as a recap after the readings above.

## Video (short clip)
**"The Surprising History Behind America's Zoning Laws"**
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ttsFP-xZKMY
Focused specifically on the racial and socioeconomic origins covered in [[Zoning's Racial and Exclusionary Origins]].

## If you only have 30 minutes
Read the Building the Skyline essay and watch the first video. That covers the core narrative and corrects the popular myth, which is the highest-value 30 minutes available this week.
