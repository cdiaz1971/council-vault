# Zoning and Urban Planning Curriculum

**Purpose:** build a working foundation in zoning and urban planning to sharpen policy work on Frederick's housing and zoning legislation, specifically the Real Rent Ordinance, the Housing Production Target Resolution, and the EPMD Ordinance.

**Pace:** 1-2 hours a week, steady.

**Tracking:** progress and status live in Notion, [Zoning and Urban Planning Curriculum](https://app.notion.com/p/9541777715324e7fb69fe7c910f20b65). This note is the content index, Notion is the dashboard.

Links below that don't resolve yet are weeks not yet built. That's expected, they'll turn into real notes as the curriculum progresses. Week 1 is live and is the model for what every week's hub note should look like: a short synthesis, recall questions, relevance to Frederick, and links out to detail notes.
