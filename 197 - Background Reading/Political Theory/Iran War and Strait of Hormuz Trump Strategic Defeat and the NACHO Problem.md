# Iran War and Strait of Hormuz -- Trump's Strategic Defeat and the NACHO Problem

## Summary

Trump's unilateral initiation of airstrikes against Iran on February 28, 2026 has produced what Senator Jack Reed called the greatest strategic defeat in US history by measurable metrics: the Strait of Hormuz remains closed, 13 service members dead, 400+ wounded, dozens of aircraft lost, and oil prices surging. Economist Paul Krugman coined "NACHO" (Not A Chance Hormuz Opens) to describe the stalemate -- Iran has no incentive to make a deal it doesn't trust Trump to honor, and Trump's ego precludes acknowledging the failure.

## Key Points

**Approval collapse:** Trump hit -22.2 net on overall job performance and -40.3 on economy (per G. Elliott Morris/Strength in Numbers). A Reuters/Ipsos poll showed only 34% approve of the Iran strikes, 61% oppose. Even Republican opposition to Trump signature proposals (his name on paper money: 40% of Republicans oppose) signals erosion within the base.

**Economic damage:** Brent crude briefly topped $114/barrel on April 30 -- highest since June 2022. Senator Angus King estimated the Hormuz closure is costing American consumers approximately $700 million per day. Fertilizer prices also surging.

**War Powers clock:** Tomorrow (May 1, 2026) marks 60 days since Trump notified Congress of military action. Under the War Powers Act, he must end hostilities or get congressional authorization. Hegseth testified that ceasefire periods pause the clock -- Senator Kaine noted the law doesn't say that. Senate Republicans have blocked six Democratic measures to invoke War Powers Act.

**Corruption:** Eric Trump-backed startup won a $24 million Pentagon contract. Trump sons-backed drone firm signed a weapons deal with the US Air Force.

**DHS:** After a 76-day shutdown, House Republicans passed Senate funding for DHS while withholding funds from ICE and CBP.

The War Powers question is also an active constitutional separation-of-powers fight worth tracking alongside the Callais gerrymandering story.
