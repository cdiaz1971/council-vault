# Project 2026 -- Heritage Foundation's Post-2025 Authoritarian Agenda

## Summary

Project 2026 is the Heritage Foundation's follow-on to Project 2025, organized under the banner "Restoring America's Promise" (Heritage 2.0). While Heritage claims there is no formal "Project 2026," the document outlines nine coordinated priorities designed to deepen authoritarian consolidation, use the 2026 midterms as a mandate, and complete the institutional dismantling begun under Project 2025. Scot Nakagawa of The Anti-Authoritarian Playbook argues these nine priorities are not independent policy proposals -- they are components of a single strategy.

## Key Points

The nine Heritage 2.0 priorities and their practical meanings:

**1. Dismantle the "Deep State"** -- Continue Schedule F, replacing career civil servants with political loyalists. Eliminates independent bureaucratic checks on executive power.

**2. "Put Family First"** -- Restore the nuclear "mother and father" family, reduce abortion access at all stages. Targets same-sex marriage and LGBTQ+ family recognition under wholesome framing.

**3. "Education Freedom"** -- Dismantle the Department of Education, expand vouchers, defund public schools. Uses "CRT and radical gender ideology" language to justify book bans and curriculum purges.

**4. Secure the Border** -- Expand detention, deportation, and denaturalization machinery. The "illegal aliens as invaders" framing provides political cover for family separation and mass removal.

**5. Win the "New Cold War" With China** -- Decoupling, hardline trade/tech policy, great-power military posture. Used to justify tariffs that hurt working families while concentrating economic power in loyalist oligarchs.

**6. Energy Dominance** -- Frame renewable energy as causing an "electricity shortage," expand fossil fuels. A subsidy program for the fossil fuel industry framed as crisis response; dismantles environmental regulations.

**7. "Excellence" in the Economy** -- Deregulation framed as worker freedom. In practice: weakens labor protections, dismantles consumer financial protections, removes guardrails against oligarchic exploitation.

**8. Counter "Big Tech"** -- Framed as antitrust enforcement against platforms suppressing conservative speech. Actual goal: install loyalist control over information infrastructure through pressure rather than breakup.

**9. Strengthen National Defense** -- Military expansion, nuclear modernization, "narcoterrorism" designations for cartels used to justify military intervention in Mexico and expanded domestic surveillance. Blurs the line between foreign adversaries and domestic opponents.
