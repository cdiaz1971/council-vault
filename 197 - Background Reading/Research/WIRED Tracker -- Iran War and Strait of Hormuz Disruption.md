# WIRED Tracker -- Iran War and Strait of Hormuz Disruption

## Summary
WIRED's Tracker newsletter covers the ongoing US-Iran war, now more than two weeks old, and its strangling effect on global shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. Trump is isolated diplomatically and the economic ripple effects are accelerating.

## Key Points
The Strait of Hormuz remains severely disrupted, threatening oil markets, fertilizer supplies, and broad consumer goods chains. A few ships from Iran, India, and China have managed to pass, but traffic is far from normal.

Trump sought allied support over the weekend, naming China, France, Japan, South Korea, and the UK as potential partners. He was rebuffed across the board.

His counterterrorism chief resigned over the conflict, reportedly blaming Israeli influence on US decision-making.

The "Handala" hacker group, connected to Iranian state-sponsored operations, has emerged as a significant actor in the cyber dimension of the conflict, most recently linked to a breach of medical tech firm Stryker.

Former House Speaker Newt Gingrich endorsed a satirical suggestion to use nuclear detonations to create a new shipping channel.

## Relevance
Background reading on a major geopolitical event with local economic implications (gas prices, consumer goods). The cyber angle is worth tracking given the IT/sysadmin role.
