# MOHELA False Delinquency Notices to SAVE Plan Borrowers

## Summary
MOHELA sent SAVE Plan borrowers false notices of missed or late payments, prompting a Debt Collective and Student Debt Crisis Center Week of Action (Aug 10-14) demanding a federal payment pause.

## Key Points
Debt Collective reports that dozens of borrowers received false delinquency notices from MOHELA despite being properly enrolled in SAVE Plan forbearance, with some notices suggesting borrowers were close to default. Because MOHELA's error reporting to credit bureaus is automated, the false notices risk damaging credit scores and borrowers' ability to get loans, insurance, or housing.

Debt Collective frames this as part of a longer pattern. The organization has called for MOHELA's removal as a federal servicer since 2024, and argues MOHELA's own financial interest has been used in litigation, including Missouri v. Trump, to help gut the SAVE Plan earlier this year.

In response, Debt Collective and the Student Debt Crisis Center are running a Week of Action from August 10 to 14 demanding an immediate pause on federal student loan payments and interest. Planned activity includes a virtual day of action targeting Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer and other New York members of Congress, media and op-ed training for affected borrowers, and a national call covering the member-led Havens case litigation and its implications for SAVE Plan borrowers.

## Relevance
This extends the SAVE Plan Repayment Deadline crisis already tracked in the vault: the servicer-side errors documented here are a concrete, worsening symptom of the same federal repayment breakdown, now compounding into credit-reporting harm on top of the original forbearance confusion. No direct council action is indicated, but it is useful background if Frederick constituents raise MOHELA billing errors or credit impacts, and it connects to the broader affordability and federal-policy organizing threads already in Political Theory.
