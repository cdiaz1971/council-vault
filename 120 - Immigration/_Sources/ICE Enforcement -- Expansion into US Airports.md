# ICE Enforcement -- Expansion into US Airports

## Summary

The Trump administration began deploying ICE agents into major US airports in March 2026. NDLON issued an action alert framing this as an expansion of immigration enforcement into everyday public spaces, raising concerns about civil liberties, passenger safety, and the introduction of agents without aviation security training.

## Key Points

**The Policy**
- Administration announced ICE agent deployment to a targeted list of major airports
- Airports named: JFK, LGA, EWR (NY/NJ); ATL, HOU, MSY (South); ORD, CLE (Midwest); PHL, PIT (Northeast); PHX, SJU (Puerto Rico), RSW/Ft. Myers (West/Island)
- NDLON characterizes the move as introducing unchecked enforcement into transit spaces
- Specific concern: ICE agents lack aviation security training, risking confusion and unequal treatment of passengers

**NDLON Response**
- Calling for local leaders to organize "ICE Out" airport actions
- Suggested activities: peaceful protest, constitutional observer presence outside TSA checkpoints, Know Your Rights distribution
- Running a mobilization campaign via Mobilize.us

## Relevance

Directly relevant to Frederick's [[City Values Act]] and immigrant community protection work. Airport enforcement expansion is a federal action but shapes the threat environment facing immigrant residents. Community partners like [[Centro Hispano de Frederick]], [[Spanish Speaking Community of Maryland]], and Frederick Rapid Response Network may be fielding concerns about this. Worth flagging in any immigration policy briefing or community communications. Also connects to the broader pattern of enforcement expanding into previously protected or neutral public spaces.
