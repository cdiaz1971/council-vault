# NDLON -- A Song for Adelanto, Serenata at Immigration Detention

## Summary
NDLON organized a caravan of more than 300 people to the Adelanto, California immigration detention center on March 14, 2026, to hold a serenata (serenade) in solidarity with people detained inside. The email is a first-person account from Manuel Vicente describing what happened and calling for continued resistance and donations to NDLON's Immigrant Defense Fund.

## Key Points
The event drew over 300 people who traveled into the desert to stand outside the Adelanto detention facility. For nearly three hours, participants shared music, testimony, and acts of solidarity.

A powerful moment came when detainee Ramiro called his child Jax by phone, and the child reported they could hear the crowd outside. Attendees chanted "Free them all!" to carry their voices over the walls.

The migrant worker band Los Jornaleros del Norte performed "Serenata a un Indocumentado," a ballad written about families separated by detention and deportation.

NDLON lawyer Caleb Soto, who works directly with people detained at Adelanto, spoke about institutional abuse and neglect inside, and called on the community to keep fighting to empty and close the facility.

The email's central message: the administration wants to isolate detained people, but solidarity cannot be imprisoned. NDLON invokes the principle "solo el pueblo salva al pueblo" -- only the people save the people.

## Relevance
Directly connected to César's "No Kings, No Camps" speech and the No Kings 3 event. The Adelanto serenata is a powerful example of the kind of community solidarity and witness that César spoke about. The framing -- that walls cannot imprison community -- echoes themes in his remarks. Could inform future speech material, Bluesky posts, or council statements on immigration detention.
