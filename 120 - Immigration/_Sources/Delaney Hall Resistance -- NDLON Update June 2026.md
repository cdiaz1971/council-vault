# Delaney Hall Resistance -- NDLON Update June 2026

**Source:** NDLON email, June 16, 2026 | Jorge Torres, Director of Organizing

## Summary

The community gathered outside Delaney Hall detention center to celebrate José David's 18th birthday -- a volunteer with Resistencia en Acción NJ who chose to spend his milestone outside the facility in solidarity with those detained inside.

Women inside Delaney Hall are leading a hunger strike to expose abuse, neglect, and inhumane conditions. Families continue to show up outside daily, demanding accountability.

NDLON's ICE Out of New Jersey Collective is committed to:
- Supporting detained individuals and their families
- Organizing for accountability
- Amplifying voices of those inside
- Building long-term power to challenge detention and deportation

## Key Framing

> "His decision reminds us that this work is built by those directly affected by detention, and by the people who refuse to look away."

> "The women striking inside Delaney Hall are reminding us of the courage we all have: courage is contagious, and as long as they continue to resist, we will continue to organize."

> "As long as people remain detained, we will continue to fight for their freedom."

## Context

Media attention fluctuates but conditions inside remain constant. Needs continue to grow for legal support, organizing, accompaniment, and emergency assistance.

Jorge Torres also published an op-ed: *"I Witnessed the Horrors of ICE's Delaney Hall. But I Also Witnessed Resistance."*

## Relevance to Frederick

Connects to Frederick Rapid Response Network infrastructure and City Values Act work. Community-based accompaniment model mirrors what FRRN does locally. Useful framing for articulating why municipal-level immigration protection matters even when federal enforcement escalates.
