# Trump Administration's TPS Termination Pattern Spans More Than a Dozen Countries Since 2025

## Summary

El Salvador's TPS termination is not an isolated action. The administration has ended Temporary Protected Status for more than a dozen countries since returning to office in 2025, most recently Haiti, Ethiopia, and Syria, with the Salvadoran termination extending that pattern rather than starting it.

## Key Points

The article cites USCIS's list of terminated TPS designations as evidence of a sustained policy pattern rather than a country-specific decision. It also reports a human cost tied to one of those terminations: the family of a 20-year-old Haitian immigrant in Ohio, a high school honors graduate and JROTC member who had planned to study medicine, said he died after losing TPS protection following his reunification with his parents.
