# ICE Officer Shoots Man During Marble Hill Enforcement Operation, Child Present

## Summary
An ICE officer shot a man during a targeted enforcement operation in Marble Hill, Manhattan, on October 8, 2026, while the man's young child was nearby in agents' custody; DHS says the man had a prior grand larceny conviction, while local officials and the man's family dispute the agency's account and access to him at the hospital.

## Key Points
- The shooting occurred around 4 p.m. on a busy street as commuters were getting home; eyewitnesses described a chaotic scene, with one agent holding the man's child as the man lay bleeding in front of a car.
- DHS confirmed the shooting in a statement, saying ICE was "conducting a targeted enforcement operation" to arrest a man from the Dominican Republic with a prior grand larceny conviction and "multiple prior arrests for assault, robbery, possession of drugs, and weapons possession." DHS said an ICE officer discharged his weapon, the man was injured but alive, and the FBI and HSI were investigating.
- NYPD confirmed federal agents, not NYPD, carried out the shooting and said the victim was "currently conscious and receiving medical attention"; NYPD was not on scene.
- Rep. Adriano Espaillat said the man's child was safe with family, and that he was personally barred by ICE from visiting the wounded man at the hospital.
- NYC Public Advocate Jumaane Williams said he spoke with the family, who reported the man was "shot in the neck, but alive," and said he did not trust the federal government to conduct its own investigation.
- NYC Mayor Zohran Mamdani called the incident "unconscionable and incredibly serious," said he had called the President to demand an end to ICE enforcement actions in the city.
- NY Governor Kathy Hochul said she spoke with President Trump and DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin, expressed outrage, urged a halt to ICE operations in New York, and asked that body camera footage be released publicly.
- Protesters gathered at the scene and later at the hospital; NYPD made several arrests while clearing the way for a car of departing federal agents.
- The incident occurred near a rooftop parking deck that immigration activists say federal agents sometimes use as a staging area before enforcement actions in Marble Hill.

## Relevance
A national example of ICE enforcement escalating to an officer-involved shooting during a routine-seeming operation, with a child present and officials disputing DHS's version of events and access to the victim -- directly relevant background for [[Effects of Immigration Enforcement]] and for César's ongoing CVA and non-cooperation advocacy, which rests partly on the argument that aggressive federal enforcement tactics create public-safety and trust risks for the communities where they occur, as previously illustrated locally by [[ICE Confrontation in Frederick's Tasker's Chance Neighborhood]].
