# HelloFresh Newark Workers Allege ICE Threats and Abuse Through Staffing Agencies

## Summary
A class action by hundreds of former temp workers at HelloFresh's Newark warehouse alleges supervisors used threats to call ICE, slurs, bathroom denial, and an English-only rule to control immigrant workers, with three staffing agencies and HelloFresh each blaming the other.

## Key Points
The class action was filed on September 23 (HelloFresh says the suit was filed more than two years ago, which points to 2024) on behalf of hundreds of former workers at the Newark, New Jersey meal-kit facility, mostly Latino immigrants. Allegations cover discrimination, harassment, and forced labor, mostly from 2021 to 2023.

**Coercion through immigration status.** Both HelloFresh and staffing-agency supervisors allegedly threatened to report workers to ICE if they objected, sometimes telling them to tolerate abuse to avoid deportation. Workers report supervisors denied bathroom breaks, in some cases until workers urinated on themselves, and a manager allegedly used a baseball bat to drive Latino workers back to the line. Workers describe urinary tract infections from holding it through shifts.

**Other allegations.** Racial slurs aimed at Latino workers, an English-only rule enforced with "this is America" reprimands, sexual propositions and explicit comments toward women, and the hardest tasks (packing ice) assigned to this group without gloves or protective gear. Make the Road New Jersey legal director Lauren Herman, lead counsel, calls it a pattern of force and coercion.

**The staffing structure.** Workers were hired through Action Staffing Group, On Target Staffing LLC, and ProStaff Solutions Inc., all named as joint employers, while supervisors were HelloFresh employees. Workers say each side disclaimed responsibility, so there was no clear place to report abuse. Outsourcing hiring to temp agencies cuts cost and liability and weakens organized labor.

**The layoffs.** On August 4, 2023, one day before New Jersey's Temporary Workers' Bill of Rights took effect (higher wages, a path to permanent jobs), HelloFresh allegedly laid off about 400 temps with almost no notice, some by WhatsApp, despite the state's 90-day WARN requirement. HelloFresh did not address the layoffs.

**Company response.** HelloFresh says an internal investigation found the claims entirely without merit and that it maintains anti-harassment policies and a 24/7 hotline. Prior issues include a 2024 Labor Department child labor investigation at its Illinois plant (teens hired through a temp agency) and a 2021 Cal/OSHA COVID fine at its Richmond, California plant after an outbreak with 171 cases and one death.

## Relevance
Threatening to call ICE is a workplace-control tool, not just an enforcement event, and it works hardest where the staffing structure leaves no accountable employer. That is the dynamic local policy can touch: the City Values Act separates city services from immigration enforcement, but labor exploitation through status threats happens in private workplaces. Relevant to any Frederick work on wage theft, joint-employer accountability for city contractors, and worker protections tied to city procurement. The New Jersey Temporary Workers' Bill of Rights is a state model worth knowing, and the day-before layoffs show how employers route around one. Make the Road New Jersey parallels the role SSCM and Centro Hispano play locally.
