# Immigrant Protection Codification Strategy  
## City Values Act – Operational Clarity and Trust-Building Framework

## Strategic Intent

This ordinance is designed to:

- Provide operational clarity for City staff when interacting with Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE)  
- Maintain community trust by separating City services from federal civil immigration enforcement  
- Establish documentation and transparency mechanisms  
- Reduce legal and liability exposure for the City  
- Codify internal governance standards rather than symbolic positioning  

This is an administrative and procedural ordinance, not a sanctuary declaration.

---

# Ordinance Structure Overview

The ordinance creates a new Chapter ___, Article ___ in the City Code titled:

**Immigration Enforcement Guidance**

It establishes:

1. Warrant-based access standards  
2. Restrictions on use of City property for civil immigration enforcement  
3. Required executive guidance and staff training  
4. Documentation and centralized logging of ICE interactions  
5. Annual public reporting  
6. Public-facing policy summary requirements  
7. Internal administrative enforcement  
8. Federal law compliance safeguards  

---

# Core Policy Components

## 1. Warrant Requirement for Nonpublic Access

- ICE access to nonpublic areas of City facilities requires:
  - Exigent circumstances; or
  - A valid warrant issued by a federal court.
- Access to equipment or databases requires a judicial criminal warrant.
- All requests must be routed through a designated official.
- No City employee may independently grant access.

**Purpose:**  
Ensures consistent protocol and reduces frontline ambiguity.

---

## 2. Prohibition on Use of City Property for Civil Immigration Enforcement

City-owned or City-controlled parking lots, garages, and vacant lots may not be used for:

- Surveillance  
- Staging  
- Processing  
- Detention  
- Vehicle storage  

Unless required by law or authorized by a valid judicial warrant.

Mayor responsibilities:

- Identify relevant properties  
- Install signage  
- Implement access controls where appropriate  

**Purpose:**  
Protects City resources and prevents normalization of enforcement activity on municipal property.

---

## 3. Required Executive Guidance

The Mayor, in consultation with the Legal Department, must:

- Create a step-by-step ICE interaction action plan  
- Provide model scripts  
- Clarify warrant distinctions  
- Establish liability-reduction practices  
- Require that Frederick Police Department not detain individuals solely on ICE civil detainers  

Departments must:

- Train staff  
- Designate responsible supervisors  
- Post nonpublic-area signage  
- Avoid initiating immigration status inquiries except as required by law  

**Purpose:**  
Creates institutional consistency across departments.

---

## 4. Documentation and Centralized Logging

Each Department must document any ICE interaction involving:

- City facilities  
- City property  
- Equipment  
- Databases  
- Individuals in City custody  

Required documentation elements:

- Date and time  
- Location  
- Nature of request  
- Warrant type (if presented)  
- Whether access was granted or denied  
- Identity of designated official  
- Final disposition  

The Mayor must maintain a centralized administrative log.

**Purpose:**  
Creates institutional memory and operational accountability.

---

## 5. Annual Public Reporting

By March 1 each year, the Mayor must publish a report containing:

- Total documented ICE interactions  
- Categories of requests  
- Number granted or denied  
- Operational patterns observed  

No personally identifiable information may be included.

**Purpose:**  
Builds transparency without escalating legal posture.

---

## 6. Public-Facing Policy Summary

The Mayor must publish a plain-language summary explaining:

- City policy on civil immigration enforcement  
- Warrant requirements  
- Service accessibility regardless of status  
- How residents may report concerns  

The summary must be translated into commonly spoken languages in the City.

**Purpose:**  
Direct trust-building measure for immigrant communities.

---

## 7. Internal Administrative Enforcement

- Knowingly violating the ordinance may result in administrative discipline.
- Violations reviewed by Department Director in consultation with Legal.
- Corrective training required where failures stem from unclear procedures.
- No private right of action created.

**Purpose:**  
Maintains seriousness while avoiding litigation exposure.

---

## 8. Compliance with Federal Law

The ordinance explicitly preserves lawful information-sharing as permitted under federal law.

**Purpose:**  
Preemption shield and legal defensibility.

---

# Governance Impact Assessment

## Operational Effects

- Reduces frontline confusion  
- Creates uniform response protocol  
- Clarifies supervisory authority  
- Strengthens training requirements  

## Trust-Building Effects

- Establishes visible boundaries  
- Provides public reporting  
- Signals non-discrimination  
- Separates City services from civil immigration enforcement  

## Legal Posture

- Avoids sanctuary terminology  
- Avoids categorical non-cooperation language  
- Includes federal compliance clause  
- Avoids private right of action  

---

# Implementation Considerations

- Budget impact likely minimal (training + signage + administrative logging).  
- Requires coordination with HR and Legal for policy drafting.  
- March 1 reporting date should align with administrative reporting calendar.  
- Translation capacity may require allocation if not already funded.

---

# Future Codification Options (Not Included in This Draft)

- Data privacy restrictions beyond current scope  
- Community notification protocols  
- Funding non-cooperation clauses  
- Charter-level protections  
- Civilian oversight review  

These would represent a second-layer codification strategy if pursued.

---

# Political Positioning

This ordinance should be framed as:

- Governance modernization  
- Administrative clarity  
- Risk management  
- Community trust infrastructure  

It is intentionally structured as operational reform rather than ideological confrontation.
