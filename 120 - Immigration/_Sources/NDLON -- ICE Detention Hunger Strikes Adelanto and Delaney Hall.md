## Summary

NDLON issued an urgent action alert on May 28, 2026 regarding hunger strikes underway at two ICE detention centers: Adelanto (California) and Delaney Hall (New Jersey). The alert is organized around mutual aid framing -- "Solo el Pueblo Salva al Pueblo" -- and centers three direct asks for supporters outside the detention walls.

## Key Points

The three asks are concrete and low-barrier: follow and amplify ground organizers Radio Jornalera NJ, Radio Cosecha NJ, and Estamos Unidos NJ; watch and share the No Mas Mentiras video on Instagram; and sign the petition to free Ramiro Santiago Pacheco Ramirez, a man detained by ICE while picking up medication in Pomona, California.

Adelanto is one of the largest ICE detention centers in the country and has a documented history of dangerous conditions, deaths in custody, and inadequate medical care. Delaney Hall in Newark is a privately operated facility that has faced sustained community opposition. Hunger strikes are among the few tools available to people inside immigration detention to communicate conditions and make demands visible.

The framing -- "Solo el Pueblo Salva al Pueblo" (Only the People Save the People) -- reflects NDLON's political orientation toward community-led mutual protection rather than institutional rescue.

## Why This Matters for Cesar

The [[Frederick Rapid Response Network]] is the local infrastructure Frederick has been building to respond to exactly these kinds of enforcement moments. Hunger strikes in distant facilities are not disconnected from what happens locally -- they reflect the same enforcement machinery that operates in Frederick County. Staying connected to national immigration organizing signals (NDLON, Centro Hispano, SSCM) helps calibrate the local threat environment and shapes what the [[City Values Act]] is meant to protect against.

The petition ask for Ramiro Santiago Pacheco Ramirez -- detained while picking up medication -- is a direct example of the chilling effect that [[ICE Enforcement Chilling Effect -- Economic and Housing Disruption]] documents at the aggregate level.
