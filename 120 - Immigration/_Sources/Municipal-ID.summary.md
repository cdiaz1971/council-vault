 **Executive Summary**

This document provides an overview of various Municipal Identification Card programs across different cities, including New York City, Hartford, and San Francisco. These programs aim to improve quality of life, enhance inclusivity, and address the needs of unbanked and underbanked residents. The programs require proof of identity and residency, with alternative methods for those who lack a stable residence or are survivors of domestic violence. The cards can serve as pre-paid debit cards, offer various benefits, and encourage their acceptance by banks, institutions, businesses, and other entities.
