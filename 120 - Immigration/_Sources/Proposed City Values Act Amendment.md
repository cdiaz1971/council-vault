# Proposed City Values Act Amendment

Section X — Federal Immigration Enforcement Cost Recovery
(a) Purpose. The City may seek to recover costs incurred as a result of federal immigration enforcement activities conducted within its jurisdiction in order to protect local resources and ensure that City taxpayers do not bear the financial burden of federal actions.
(b) Cost Recovery Authority. The Mayor, or the Mayor's designee, is authorized to identify, document, and pursue reimbursement from the federal government, including but not limited to the U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS), U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), and U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP), for any costs incurred by the City arising from or related to federal immigration enforcement activities.
(c) Recoverable Costs. Recoverable costs shall include, but are not limited to:
	1	Personnel costs, including salaries, overtime, and benefits associated with diverted staff time;
	2	Public safety response costs, including police, fire, and emergency services;
	3	Administrative and legal costs, including documentation, compliance, and interagency coordination;
	4	Costs associated with detention, transportation, or facility use;
	5	Community impact mitigation costs, including outreach, translation, and service provision; and
	6	Any other direct or indirect costs reasonably attributable to federal immigration enforcement activities within the City.
(d) Documentation and Billing. The City may establish procedures to track and document all eligible costs. The Mayor, or designee, may submit invoices, claims, or other formal requests for reimbursement to the appropriate federal agencies and may take any administrative action necessary to pursue payment.
