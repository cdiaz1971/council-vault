# ICE -- Confirmed Use of Paragon Spyware and Congressional Pushback

## Summary

ICE acting director Todd Lyons confirmed to House Democrats that the agency is deploying Paragon's Graphite spyware as part of Homeland Security Investigations operations. Three House Democrats called the use outrageous and demanded documentation of safeguards that ICE has not provided.

## Key Points

Lyons' letter confirmed the use of "cutting-edge technological tools" for HSI operations, specifically citing fentanyl trafficking and encrypted communications interception. He did not name Paragon by name but confirmed compliance with a 2023 Biden executive order requiring certification against national security and counterintelligence risks. ICE had reactivated a previously stopped Paragon contract last year.

The Democratic response from Reps. Lee, Brown, and Ansari centered on the lack of Congressional oversight and the absence of compliance documentation. The EFF's Cooper Quintin flagged the vague language in Lyons' letter as leaving the door open for administrative subpoenas to authorize domestic use of the malware.

Paragon's Graphite technology has previously been found on the phones of journalists, and suspected deployments have been documented in multiple countries. WhatsApp disrupted a Graphite campaign targeting reporters and civil society organizations.
