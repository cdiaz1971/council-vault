In the last few months, the Immigration Support Team has joined the Frederick Rapid Response Network formed by RISE of Western Maryland, the ACLU and others.  It started from an urge to protect our immigrant neighbors from attacks by ICE. After a few months we have found that the sheriff's department's use of 287(g) to deport residents has resulted in ICE attacks being limited to small scale operations that last a few minutes and limit the effectiveness of our response. 

 This doesn't reduce the harm to our communities; it just shifted our response.  While we still respond and gather information on reported ICE activities, we also provide support to those affected by the cruel deportations, providing legal resources, mutual aid and follow up support.  We continue to work with RISE and DSA on direct know your rights canvassing to the community. 

 Other members of UUCF are working with the One Million Rising movement canvassing businesses to educate them on their rights as well as the rights of their employees If you have any questions or want to join the Immigration Support Team or the One Million Rising group, you can sign up  at the DAT table in the atrium.
