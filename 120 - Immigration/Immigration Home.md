## How to use the wiki

1. Begin with a core topic to understand the problem, authority, evidence, options, and metrics.
2. Follow links to active projects when Council is considering a concrete action.
3. Use [[Local Authority and Federal Preemption]] to check legal exposure before drafting.
4. Use research notes as supporting material, not automatically as current policy.
5. Use `_Sources` for dated snapshots and retained source material.

## Page types

| Type | Purpose | Expected maintenance |

## Maintenance rules

- Keep only this page and [[Immigration - In-Progress Plans]] as working files in the Immigration root.
- Give durable core pages undated filenames; put review dates in frontmatter.
- Preserve superseded versions in `_Sources`; do not erase the research trail.
- Distinguish enacted law, pending litigation, proposals, personal strategy, and outside examples.
- Litigation status, federal policy, and enforcement facts change quickly. Recheck them before relying on them.
