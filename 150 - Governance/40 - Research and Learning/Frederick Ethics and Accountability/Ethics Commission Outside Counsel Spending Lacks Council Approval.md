# Ethics Commission Outside Counsel Spending Lacks Council Approval

## Summary
Cathy Anderson reports that the City's FY2026 legal services line paid $81,743.87, that three outside firms advise the Ethics Commission at uncapped hourly rates of $250 to $400, and that she found no record of Council approving any of them; she asks Council to call the Commission to a public work session.

## Key Points
Per Anderson's review of city financial records: the legal services line paid $81,743.87 in FY2026. Powell billed $26,758.32 of that for March through June alone. A $37,973.84 payment on June 24 has no vendor listed. Three outside firms have been engaged to advise the Commission, all hourly with no cap: Powell at $275, Rockwell at $400, and Semmes, Bowen and Semmes at $250, the last signed September 16. Her September 21 email adds that outside counsel was hired through the Mayor's office with no scope and no bid, and that invoices remain redacted after the Nash case closed.

Her ask is procedural and deliberately avoids pending matters. Council approves every commissioner under Section 21-2(a)(1) and can remove one for neglect of duty or misconduct under Section 21-2(f), so it has standing to ask them to explain their process in public. The questions she lists: delays, choice of counsel, rules that narrow the ordinance, and missing meeting records.

## Relevance
Uncapped, unbid, unscoped legal engagements are a budget oversight question independent of the ethics dispute, and one Council can raise through the budget office without touching any confidential complaint. A public work session with the Commission is within Council's authority and would be the lowest-risk first step toward reform. The unlabeled $37,973.84 payment is a concrete, checkable question for the CFO.
