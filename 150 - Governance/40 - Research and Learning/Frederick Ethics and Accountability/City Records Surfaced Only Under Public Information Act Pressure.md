# City Records Surfaced Only Under Public Information Act Pressure

## Summary
Two incidents raised by Cathy Anderson: the City's June 18 Council meeting video download contained only the public comment about Nash until hours after her PIA request, and the state-required annual ethics reports to Council were produced only on the 30-day MPIA deadline, with metadata showing creation after Legal closed the request.

## Key Points
June 18 meeting video. On September 22 around 3 p.m., the MP4 download for the June 18 Council meeting on the City website was 1 hour 24 minutes and 1.34 GB, containing only public comment, most of it about Nash. The meeting ran nearly four hours. Anderson filed PIA request R000384-092226 at 4:19 p.m. By 8 p.m. the same link downloaded the full 8.34 GB meeting. Cesar had separately found a full-meeting Granicus link that afternoon. Anderson does not know whether his inquiry or her PIA triggered the fix.

Annual ethics reports under GP Section 5-807. State law requires the Ethics Commission to certify compliance to the State Ethics Commission by October 1 and to send the governing body a separate annual report on its operations, including meetings, agendas, minutes, and attendance. Anderson requested the Council-facing reports for 2022 to 2025 by email on July 20. She heard nothing until she asked on August 19, the statutory deadline. Within about 90 minutes the Clerk redirected her to the portal, staff opened a portal ticket, Legal marked the request fulfilled and closed at 3:55 p.m., and the document's metadata shows it was created at 4:12 p.m. The Clerk then said the records had been provided. The portal does not list Council as a records custodian.

Anderson's point is not the delay alone. These reports are supposed to reach Council every year without anyone asking. Either Council is not receiving them, or it receives them and nobody could produce a copy without going through Legal.

## Relevance
Both incidents are about who controls Council's own record. Two concrete fixes are within Council's reach: confirm whether the Section 5-807 reports have been delivered to Council for each year and require delivery on the record going forward, and ask the Clerk how the June 18 video was edited and by whom, with a policy that official meeting recordings are published complete. Adding Council as a selectable custodian in the PIA portal is a small administrative ask.
