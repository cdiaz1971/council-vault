# Governance - In-Progress Plans

Planning-stage governance ideas that have not been introduced. Legislative status for anything that moves forward lives in the Notion Legislative Roadmap.

## Planning Commission accountability

**Status:** Idea stage. Not drafted.

**The problem:** Council has no built-in way to weigh in on planning decisions before they reach it. In the 2026 cycle, the ADU ordinance draft did not carry the conditions the Planning Commission attached to its 4-0 recommendation, and the Mixed Use Vertical amendment went from a 4-0 Commission vote to introduction by the Planning Director.

**Approach:** Keep the Planning Commission and its technical review, rather than dissolve it, and add structural oversight through one of two routes:

1. A Council member serves on the Planning Commission. Maryland Land Use § 2-102 permits one member of the legislative body to serve ex officio for their legislative term.
2. The Planning Commission presents its recommendations and conditions directly to Council, rather than only through the Planning Department.

**Next evidence needed:** Current Planning Commission composition in the Land Management Code; whether an ex officio member would vote; how either route interacts with Council Member Brehm's announced, undrafted site plan review amendment; and how it fits with the proposed LMC Review Commission.
