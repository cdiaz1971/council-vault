# Drone-as-First-Responder -- NLC Partnership with BRINC

The National League of Cities (NLC) is partnering with drone technology company BRINC to help cities, towns, and villages evaluate and deploy drone-as-first-responder (DFR) programs. DFR dispatches drones ahead of human units to an incident scene, giving first responders real-time visual information before they arrive. The NLC partnership is explicitly designed for smaller governments that lack internal drone expertise or procurement capacity.

DFR programs have been running in larger departments for several years but are now being packaged for broader municipal adoption through the NLC infrastructure. The model is: drone arrives at the scene in seconds, streams live video to dispatch and responding officers, then human units arrive with situational awareness they wouldn't otherwise have.

## Why This Matters for César

The NLC framing makes this directly accessible for Frederick. As a mid-sized city, Frederick has the public safety needs but not necessarily the internal capacity to evaluate emerging technology independently. An NLC-backed program with vendor support from BRINC lowers that barrier. It also fits Frederick's pattern of using national municipal networks to pilot technology that would be harder to procure and justify alone.

Worth raising with public safety staff or the city's technology committee as a future agenda item.
