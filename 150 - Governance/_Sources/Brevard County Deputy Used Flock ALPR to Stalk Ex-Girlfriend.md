# Brevard County Deputy of the Year Used Flock ALPR to Stalk Ex-Girlfriend

## Summary
404 Media reports Brevard County, Florida deputy Michael Fultz resigned after an internal investigation found he used Flock ALPR to stalk his ex-girlfriend, among other misconduct, despite having been named the department's Deputy of the Year in 2025.

## Key Points
Fultz was hired in 2024 and named Brevard County's Deputy of the Year in 2025. The day of the award ceremony, his ex-girlfriend sent the sheriff's office a letter detailing a long list of complaints about his behavior. That letter prompted an internal investigation, which uncovered his use of Flock to stalk her, along with allegations of sexual misconduct and racism. News6 in Florida first reported the story, and 404 Media obtained additional details after getting a copy of the investigation file.

## Relevance
Another documented case for the individual-misuse evidence base already tracked in [[Flock Safety ALPR Misuse for Personal Stalking]] and [[Cop Used License Plate Reader Database to Stalk Woman]]. Notable because the misuse was happening concurrently with the officer being publicly honored, useful for countering the argument that misuse is rare or confined to problem officers.
