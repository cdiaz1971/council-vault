# Flock CEO Tells Police Chiefs Anti-Flock Backlash Is Bots

## Summary
Leaked audio from the October 2026 IACP conference shows Flock CEO Garrett Langley telling police chiefs that nationwide anti-Flock protests are driven by paid or Russian bots, not real residents, even as communities cancel contracts. Useful evidence of how the vendor frames democratic opposition to its customers.

## Key Points
Speaking at a private session of the International Association of Chiefs of Police conference in Orlando, an event drawing more than 16,000 public safety officials, Langley claimed social media criticism of Flock had dropped about 95 percent since July and attributed the drop to someone having stopped paying for it. He repeated a disproven theory that Ukrainian drone strikes on Russian data centers cut bot activity in half overnight, and described protests as the same people with the same signs, which he called inorganic.

He also told the room his wife advised him never to apologize and that he would prefer never to do press interviews again. The comments echo an earlier unsubstantiated claim that anti-Flock organizing is a far-left network acting for China.

The context matters. Residents in Norfolk, Virginia held a DeFlock rally at City Hall on October 13, 2026, and 404 Media notes that many communities have canceled contracts and bagged up cameras. This follows 404 Media's reporting that Langley falsely denied Flock's role in tracking a woman who self-administered an abortion.
