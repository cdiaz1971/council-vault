# Battle Ground Invocation Policy Opened by The Satanic Temple

## Summary
Battle Ground, Washington, a city where a Christian Nationalist-linked business has bought up property and political offices, adopted an open invocation policy. The first speaker to sign up was a Satanic Temple member, and the meeting itself went smoothly until online backlash arrived days later.

## Key Points
Owners of Maddox, an electrical equipment company tied to Christian Nationalist pastor Doug Wilson, have been buying property in Battle Ground and bought the local newspaper. A Maddox manager was elected mayor, the deputy mayor is married to a Maddox employee, and another affiliate sits on the school board. Oregon Public Broadcasting reporter Erik Neumann has covered this extensively.

In August the council passed an invocation resolution 5 to 1. Speakers are chosen first come, first served, once a year each, through a call or online form. Rules: under three minutes, "positive and uplifting," and no denigrating other faiths or nonbelievers, threatening damnation, proselytizing, or preaching conversion. The policy was built to survive a lawsuit.

Bast Sekhmet of The Satanic Temple, a Clark County resident, signed up after seeing a Reddit thread and unknowingly chose the inaugural date, October 5. She adapted an invocation by YouTuber Aron Ra about reason, compassion, autonomy, and freedom of expression. The mayor shook her hand beforehand to get her name right, and nobody reacted that night. Backlash came later online, focused on "Hail Satan" and her appearance rather than her words. Former mayor and current state Senator Adrian Cortes joked the city was going to hell in a handbasket under the current mayor.

## Relevance
Battle Ground is the contrast case to Ottumwa. The council and mayor handled the meeting professionally and followed their own policy, so the invocation was a non-event in the room. The controversy was imported from outside afterward. This shows that clean procedure by the chair contains the issue, whatever the content.
