# GovTech -- Pierce County Sheriff Shutters ALPR System

## Summary

The Pierce County, Washington sheriff shut down the county's license plate reader program. This is notable as a voluntary rollback by a law enforcement agency, coming in the same news cycle as a new Washington state law regulating ALPRs.

## Key Points

The Pierce County shutdown represents an unusual case: rather than being compelled by legislation or court order, the sheriff's office chose to discontinue ALPR deployment. The specific reasons were not detailed in GovTech's reporting, but the timing alongside the new Washington state ALPR law suggests either preemptive compliance or policy reconsideration in light of growing scrutiny.

Two Washington state ALPR stories in two days -- the new state regulation law (April 1 GovTech) and now a county-level shutdown (April 2) -- signal that the Pacific Northwest is becoming a meaningful policy laboratory for ALPR governance.
