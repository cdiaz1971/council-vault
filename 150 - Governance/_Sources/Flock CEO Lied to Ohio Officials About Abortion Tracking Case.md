# Flock CEO Lied to Ohio Officials About Abortion Tracking Case

## Summary
404 Media reports Flock CEO Garrett Langley told Ohio law enforcement and government officials on an August call that the outlet's reporting on a woman tracked via Flock after self-administering an abortion was entirely false, despite court records and police reports documenting the case.

## Key Points
On a call with Ohio law enforcement and government officials, Langley disputed both Flock's role in tracking the woman and the surrounding context of the case, calling 404 Media's story "entirely false." 404 Media states its reporting is backed by court records, police reports, and its own prior documentation of the case in detail.
