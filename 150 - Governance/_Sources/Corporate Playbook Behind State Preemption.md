# Corporate Playbook Behind State Preemption

## Summary
PowerSwitch Action's report The Hidden Heist traces how corporations and industry groups use the same playbook across states to pass preemption laws banning local affordable housing, wage, and workplace measures. Case studies cover California, Colorado, Missouri, Tennessee, and Texas.

## Key Points
The report treats preemption as a deliberate strategy rather than a series of one-off fights. It traces two centuries of corporate and elite efforts to override local democracy, then documents state bans on affordable housing solutions, higher wages, and livable workplaces in five states.

It identifies recurring tactics, summarized as buying, bullying, and bamboozling legislators, and a recurring cast of industry groups, corporations, and think tanks that move model bans from state to state.

## Relevance
Maryland preemption is a live constraint on Frederick's affordability work, from rent stabilization to fee rules. Recognizing the industry pattern helps anticipate where pushback on local measures will come from and frames preemption as a democracy issue rather than a technical legal question.
