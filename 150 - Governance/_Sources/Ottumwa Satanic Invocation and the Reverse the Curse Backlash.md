# Ottumwa Satanic Invocation and the Reverse the Curse Backlash

## Summary
A Satanic Temple member delivered an invocation at an Ottumwa, Iowa council meeting under an open policy, a pastor rushed the mic to demand the council reverse the curse, and the mayor capitulated with a moment of silence. The one dissenting council member had predicted it when the policy passed.

## Key Points
Ottumwa adopted an invocation policy on January 20, 2026. Its text says invocations "include our Creator into our decision making," but anyone may request a slot. On October 6, former council member Matt Dalbey of the Satanic Temple of Iowa delivered a short invocation about reason, accountability, and resisting arbitrary authority, ending with "Hail Satan."

After roll call, New Life Center pastor Rick Bick went to the microphone and demanded the council "reverse the curse." Council member Cara Galloway told him he could speak during public comment, but Mayor Benjamin Foote overruled her and called a 30-second moment of silence. Foote later told Fox News Digital the invocation "does not represent the faith or the values of families who call this city home."

Galloway, the only vote against the policy, said at the end of the meeting that she had warned exactly this would happen. Responding to one invocation invites objections to all of them and creates financial liability for taxpayers, and the council's job is to stay neutral. The city then issued a statement saying it cannot discriminate among denominations, does not review invocations in advance, and does not endorse them. Next year's calendar is full and Dalbey is scheduled again, including November 3.

Mehta notes other corrective-prayer incidents in Florida and the Arizona legislature in 2017.

## Relevance
The procedural failure here was the chair's, not the speaker's. A presiding officer who lets a member of the public interrupt the agenda and then grants a corrective ritual has taken a side on viewpoint and exposed the city legally. As Council President, the useful takeaways are to send objections to public comment every time and to never offer a response ritual to one speaker that would not be offered to all.
