# April McClain Delaney Wins MD-6 -- Frederick-Area Federal Delegation Picture

## Summary

Rep. April McClain Delaney (D-MD-6) held her seat in the June 23, 2026 primary against David Trone, who spent $25M of his own money in a second attempt to reclaim the district. The AP called the race for McClain Delaney with 92% of precincts reporting. She faces a competitive general election in November; MD-6 has a 39D/33R registered voter split.

## Key Points

The Trone-McClain Delaney race was defined by money and negative campaigning. Trone spent $25M; McClain Delaney loaned her campaign $7.2M. The endorsement from Rep. Jamie Raskin was the deciding factor for at least some Frederick-area voters.

Trone's campaign tried to characterize McClain Delaney as aligned with Republicans on immigration, citing her vote for the Laken Riley Act, which makes it easier to detain undocumented immigrants on minor charges. That vote is a real policy marker -- worth noting for any future engagement with her office on immigration issues, since the City Values Act and Frederick's non-cooperation posture may create points of tension.

The general election field: Robin Ficker (longtime candidate), Chris Burnett (Marine veteran, newcomer), and Mariela Roca (Air Force veteran, ran in 2024). Ficker appeared to be leading Republican primary returns. Most analysts expect the district to favor the Democratic nominee in November, but the margin is not wide.

MD-6 covers western Maryland including Frederick County. McClain Delaney's office is the federal delegation contact for most of Frederick's council work.
