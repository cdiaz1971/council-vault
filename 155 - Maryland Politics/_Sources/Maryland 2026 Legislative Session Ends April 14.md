# Maryland 2026 Legislative Session Ends April 14

## Summary

The Maryland General Assembly's 2026 legislative session concluded on April 14, 2026, after 90 days. Lawmakers returned to districts and other employment. Member base salary is $55,526; the Senate president and House speaker earn $72,119.

## Key Points

The 90-day session ended April 14 with passage of the state budget among its final acts. Many legislators hold other jobs outside of lawmaking -- the article profiles several, including union workers, farmers, and lawyers. The dual-employment reality shapes legislative priorities in ways that reflect members' professional backgrounds.

## Relevance

Useful date anchor for tracking Maryland legislative timelines and the window for any follow-up action, regulations, or local implementation requirements stemming from bills passed this session. The session end also resets the clock on constituent and intergovernmental outreach to state legislators.
