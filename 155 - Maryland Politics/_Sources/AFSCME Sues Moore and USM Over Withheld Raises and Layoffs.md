# AFSCME Sues Moore and USM Over Withheld Raises and Layoffs

## Summary

AFSCME Council 3 filed an unfair labor practice charge and lawsuit against Governor Moore and the University System of Maryland after USM laid off 100+ union members and withheld negotiated pay raises, citing a funding dispute between the governor's office and the university.

## Key Points

The dispute centers on a gap in the FY2027 appropriations bill: USM says Moore "failed to act consistent with law to appropriate all the funds necessary to honor the negotiated wage increases." Moore's office says the system had sufficient funds with what was appropriated. Neither party would force the other's hand, so USM did both: laid off 100+ union members in May and June, and withheld the raises.

AFSCME President Patrick Moran filed an unfair labor practice charge Wednesday and a separate lawsuit in Maryland District Court Thursday. The unfair labor practice charge details the February-March dispute over appropriations.

Key data points:
- University of Maryland College Park: 73 members lost jobs
- Bowie State University: 21 members lost jobs
- Employees lost include mental health services workers, IT and administrative staff, event planners, and animal care workers
- USM hired new non-union workers after the layoffs and maintained a list of 300+ employees earning $300K+ totaling $24M -- none of whom were touched

The union's position is that if USM would honor a contract with a construction firm or a basketball coach, it should honor its contract with workers.
