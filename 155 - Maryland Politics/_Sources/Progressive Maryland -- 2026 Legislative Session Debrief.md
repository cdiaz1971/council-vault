## Summary

Progressive Maryland's debrief of the 2026 Maryland legislative session, covering wins and losses across housing, immigration, and progressive priorities — and next steps for the movement.

## Key Points

The 2026 Maryland legislative session concluded in mid-April. Progressive Maryland is holding a debrief event on Tuesday, April 28 to review what passed, what failed, and where organizing energy should go next. The email frames the session as containing both genuine wins and unfinished fights, and invites members to join the conversation and plug into volunteer opportunities.
