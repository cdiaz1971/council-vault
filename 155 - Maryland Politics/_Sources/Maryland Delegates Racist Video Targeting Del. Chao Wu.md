# Maryland Delegates Racist Video Targeting Del. Chao Wu

## Summary

Republican Dels. Mark Fisher and Brian Chisholm posted a video accusing Del. Chao Wu, a Chinese-born Democratic delegate, of being a Chinese spy -- prompting swift condemnation from leadership across both parties and surfacing the Maryland Freedom Caucus's pattern of targeting immigrant legislators.

## Key Points

On April 30, 2026, Dels. Mark N. Fisher (R-Calvert) and Brian Chisholm (R-Anne Arundel) posted a 13-minute podcast accusing Del. Chao Wu (D-Howard and Montgomery) -- a native of China who has served in the House since 2023 -- of being affiliated with the Chinese Communist Party, based on a nine-month-old Washington Examiner story about a campus organization Wu belonged to as a college student 20 years ago.

The video also mocked Wu's appearance and manner of speaking. House Speaker Joseline Peña-Melnyk wrote both delegates demanding they delete the video and apologize. The Asian American and Pacific Islander Caucus, Maryland Legislative Black Caucus, and even House Minority Leader Jason Buckel condemned the video. Fisher and Chisholm are both members of the Maryland Freedom Caucus, which responded to the Speaker's letter by accusing her of speech policing rather than addressing substance.

Fisher offered a late-video "caveat" that "being Asian doesn't mean you're a Chinese Communist" while continuing to imply Wu was a spy. Wu, who holds a doctorate in electrical and computer engineering from UMD and served on the Howard County School Board, said it was the first time he'd been called a Chinese spy and connected the incident to a long history of xenophobia targeting Chinese Americans.

## Why This Matters

This is relevant to Frederick's immigrant protection work on multiple levels. It illustrates the kind of rhetoric that creates the chilling effect documented in [[ICE Enforcement Chilling Effect -- Economic and Housing Disruption]] -- when elected officials publicly question the loyalty of immigrant colleagues based on national origin, it signals to immigrant communities that public participation carries personal risk. The Maryland Freedom Caucus is the state-level analog to the federal far-right caucuses whose rhetoric César's council work on the City Values Act was designed to counter locally. Fisher is also a Carroll/Frederick-adjacent figure worth tracking.
