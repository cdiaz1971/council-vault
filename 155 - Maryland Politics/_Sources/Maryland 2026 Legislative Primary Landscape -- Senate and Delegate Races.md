# Maryland 2026 Legislative Primary Landscape -- Senate and Delegate Races

## Summary

At least five state senators and 19 House delegates are retiring or running for other offices ahead of Maryland's June 23, 2026 primary, creating significant legislative turnover in Annapolis for 2027. The most competitive races are in Prince George's County, Baltimore City, and Montgomery County, with ideological contests between progressive insurgents and established incumbents a recurring theme.

## Key Points

The highest-profile race is Senate President Bill Ferguson's primary challenge from Bobby LaPin, a progressive with a strong grassroots operation. Ferguson is well-funded and community-embedded but LaPin's campaign signals that presiding officers are not immune to the current anti-establishment current.

In Baltimore City (District 41), appointed Sen. Dalya Attar faces challenger Malcolm Ruff while contending with extortion charges from her 2022 campaign. The outcome turns on racial, ethnic, and factional dynamics in a diverse district.

Prince George's County has the most competitive cluster: three contested Senate races (Districts 23, 24, 26) and multiple delegate races with crowded fields, high turnover, and ideological diversity.

Montgomery County's District 39 features a repeat of the 2022 intra-delegation conflict around Del. Gabriel Acevedo, with three other incumbents backing a challenger.

Western Maryland's District 1A features five Republicans competing to replace retiring Del. Jim Hinebaugh, including a MAGA-aligned candidate testing whether the region's pragmatic conservative tradition holds.

## Relevance

Frederick's state legislative delegation will be shaped by what happens in June. Turnover in Annapolis affects the relationships and leverage points available on Frederick-relevant legislation, including the Maryland Housing Certainty Act, any data center legislation, and immigration-related bills. Worth monitoring who wins seats that could affect Frederick's policy priorities in 2027.
