# AFSCME Reaches Deal With Moore and USM to Restore Withheld Pay Raises

## Summary

AFSCME Council 3 reached a deal with Governor Wes Moore and the University System of Maryland in which USM will cover previously withheld negotiated pay raises for about 5,700 non-faculty employees across nine campuses using $13.8 million from its FY2027 fund balance, with Moore's office committing to build that amount into the FY2028 base budget.

## Key Points

- Resolves the dispute that led AFSCME to sue Moore and USM in July 2026 over withheld raises (see [[AFSCME Sues Moore and USM Over Withheld Raises and Layoffs]]). The union had rallied outside USM's Baltimore headquarters July 23 and earlier protested roughly 100 layoffs (73 at UMD College Park, 21 at Bowie State) at a June 12 Board of Regents meeting.
- USM had already given all 40,000 system employees a 1.5% cost-of-living increase but withheld the separately negotiated merit/contract raises specifically covered by the AFSCME agreement.
- AFSCME Maryland Council 3 executive director Sabeela Ally called the deal "a win for everyone": "When university system leaders attempted to treat our legally binding union contract as an option rather than an obligation, AFSCME members did not back down." The union's public statement adds that "work continues" on reinstating the laid-off workers.
- USM Chancellor Jay Perman and Board of Regents Chair Linda Gooden issued a joint statement calling it a resolution reached "with our budget under intense pressure."
- Comes about a week after former AFSCME Maryland president Patrick Moran was elected president of AFSCME International, the 1.4-million-member national union.
