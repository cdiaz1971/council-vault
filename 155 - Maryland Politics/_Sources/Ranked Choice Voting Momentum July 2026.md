# Ranked Choice Voting Momentum July 2026

## Key Developments

Since July 14, city officials in **Honolulu**, **Irvine, CA**, and **Longmont, CO** all voted to place ranked choice voting on their November ballots. Together these cities cover approximately 1.4 million Americans. Honolulu is home to nearly 70% of Hawaii's population; Longmont is one of the fastest-growing cities in the country.

## Congressional Action

Rep. Kelly Morrison (MN) reintroduced the **Voter Choice Act** in Congress, which would expand RCV nationally. FairVote issued a press release and called on constituents to contact their members of Congress in support.

## Primaries Data

Maine and Washington, DC released cast vote records from their RCV primaries. FairVote analyzed results from both jurisdictions, providing insight into voter preference patterns across rounds. Maine Democrats also used RCV to select Troy Jackson as their Senate nominee, replacing Graham Platner.

## Institutional Support

The bipartisan US Conference of Mayors, National Association of Counties, and National Governors Association all issued statements affirming local leadership of elections -- a signal FairVote highlighted as meaningful cross-partisan validation.

## Why This Matters

RCV is gaining traction at the city level as election reform advocacy shifts toward municipal proving grounds. The November ballots in Honolulu, Irvine, and Longmont will be watched as data points on whether reform translates from policy to practice. Connects to the democratic resilience arc -- RCV reduces spoiler dynamics and incentivizes coalition-building over base mobilization.
