# Maryland Mail-In Ballot Vendor Error -- June 2026 Primary Resend

## Summary

The Maryland State Board of Elections announced that a vendor error caused some voters to receive the wrong party's mail-in primary ballot for the June 23 primary. Although the number of affected voters may be small, the board decided to resend replacement ballots to all mail-in requesters to maintain the integrity of the process. The vendor, Taylor Print and Visual Impressions Inc., acknowledged the error and said it has implemented additional quality control measures.

## Key Points

Affected ballots were those requested and mailed before May 14. Voters who requested web-delivered printable ballots were not affected. The June 22 deadline for web delivery requests was unchanged.

State Administrator of Elections Jared DeMarinis framed the full resend as a proactive integrity measure rather than an acknowledgment that widespread fraud occurred. The board's statement noted that safeguards exist to ensure only corrected replacement ballots will be counted, though those safeguards were not specified in the vendor's public statement.

The Maryland Freedom Caucus, a group of hard-right House conservatives, used the error to question the integrity of the resend process itself, demanding details on how the first and second ballot printings would be distinguished. This is consistent with the national pattern of using administrative errors to seed voter fraud narratives regardless of actual impact.

The primary is June 23, 2026. Voters who haven't yet submitted a ballot should use the replacement mailing when it arrives.

## Relevance

Relevant to council work primarily as local political context -- the June 23 primary affects Frederick legislative races. The vendor error story is also a useful data point when discussing election administration and the costs of contracted ballot production. The Freedom Caucus response is worth tracking as an indicator of how Maryland Republicans are positioning on voting rights issues heading into the primary.
