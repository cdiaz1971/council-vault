# Maryland 2026 Legislative Turnover -- Key Departures and Races

## Summary

25 Maryland legislators will not return to their current seats after the 2026 cycle -- some retiring, some running for higher office. The Frederick-specific item: Del. Barrie Ciliberti (R-Frederick) is not seeking reelection, opening his seat.

## Key Points

The June 2026 primary will be consequential for the General Assembly's composition. Three lawmakers are running for the open 5th Congressional District seat left by Rep. Steny Hoyer: Del. Nicole Williams (D-Prince George's), Del. Adrian Boafo (D-Prince George's), and Sen. Arthur Ellis (D-Charles). Boafo received Hoyer's endorsement.

Frederick-specific: Del. Barrie Ciliberti (R-Frederick) is not seeking reelection. His departure creates an open seat race in Frederick's district.

Other notable departures include Sen. Pamela Beidle (D-Anne Arundel, retiring after 20 years), Del. Adrienne Jones (D-Baltimore County, House Speaker, not seeking reelection), and Del. C.T. Wilson (D-Charles, running for state Senate).

## Relevance

Ciliberti's departure is worth tracking for Frederick's legislative delegation composition going into the next session. The open seat will affect who Frederick's city government is working with on state legislation -- housing, transportation, and any Frederick-specific bills. The shift in the 5th District race is also relevant given federal funding and congressional relationships.
