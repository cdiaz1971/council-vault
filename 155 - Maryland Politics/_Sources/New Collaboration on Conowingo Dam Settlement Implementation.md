# New Collaboration on Conowingo Dam Settlement Implementation

## Summary
Cecil County and state officials, formerly the Clean Chesapeake Coalition, have shifted to a collaborative advisory group implementing the 340 million dollar Conowingo Dam settlement, though FERC approval and a federal Clean Water Act rule change remain open risks.

## Key Points
Rural Maryland counties that once challenged the state's water quality certification for the Conowingo Dam, under the banner of the Clean Chesapeake Coalition, have replaced that adversarial posture with a collaborative advisory group working on implementation of a 340 million dollar settlement with Constellation Energy. The settlement funds environmental projects over several decades, including a possible 18.7 million dollar sediment dredging study by the Army Corps of Engineers, expected in June 2027. Two risks remain open. FERC still has to approve the water quality certification and issue a new 50 year operating license, and a proposed EPA rule change could narrow what counts as a regulable pollution source under the Clean Water Act, though state officials believe their filing predates the rule change enough to be shielded.

## Relevance
Tangential to Frederick's direct work, this is a Cecil and Harford County water quality and dam issue with no Frederick nexus. Kept as general Maryland state-county relations awareness from the same MACO conference as the budget pressure piece above.
