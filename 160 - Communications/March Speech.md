We are here today to honor a murder victim, Renee Good. 

She wasn't shot by a gang member.  She wasn't shot by the radical left, or antifa or an immigrant.


She was the victim of state sponsored violence.  Let's make that clear. That ICE agent wasn't rouge.  He wasnt a bad apple.  He IS ICE.  He is what they want from their agents. His actions are what they want to get away with. How do we know? Soon after Renee Good was killed two other people  were shot by ICE in Portland.  Look at the words on the podium the DHS secretary stood behind after the shooting, I won't repeat them. 

ICE is a gang of thugs. And their gang leader is Donald Trump. They attack our neighbors, they terrorize our communities  and now they kill bystanders

We are here not only to honor Renee Good, but to send the message that ICE is not welcome in Frederick.  No masked thugs taking our friends and neighbors off the streets. No families wondering why someone didn't come home.  No innocent bystanders shot by agents of the state.

After today we can continue to honor Renee, by protecting and supporting the immigrant community. Join our ICE response team.  Or our Western Maryland Care Network. 


Call your Elected federal officials and tell them to stop funding ICE and to never again praise ICE agents.  If you are in the city, call your state leaders and let them know you support their stance on a 287(g) ban.  If you aren't in the city, call them to support it. Reach out to your county and city officials, let them know you support local pro immigrant legislation and expect them to stand against these attacks on your neighbors.


Let's continue to honor Renee Good by fighting for those she gave her life to protect.
