# UMD URSP708 Guest Lecture -- CVA and Building Political Support

## Summary
Planning notes for César's guest lecture at UMD's URSP708 Community Development Planning Studio on the City Values Act, how to build political support for immigrant-protective policy, and lessons for planners.

## Engagement Details
- Course: URSP708, Community Development Planning Studio, Planning, Immigrants, and Immigrant Communities
- Instructors: Gerrit Knaap and Reemberto Rodriguez
- Date: Tuesday, September 29, 2026, 5:30 to 6:30 p.m.
- Format: virtual or in-person, to be determined closer to date
- Course context: this date falls in Week 5, Topic 5, municipal and county immigration policies
- Also invited to join as Advisory Panel member at midterm (Thursday, October 15, 4:30 to 6:30 p.m.) and final presentations (Tuesday, December 15)
- Course will produce a guidebook or policy report shared with the wider planning and local government community
- Need to confirm attendance by reply email to Gerrit

## Talk Structure (Draft)
Roughly 30-35 minutes of remarks plus open Q&A within the one-hour slot.

1. **The CVA as case study.** What the City Values Act does, why it mattered, the 7-0 vote, and what that vote actually required politically given the makeup of the council.
2. **The mechanics of building support.** How César sequenced relationships, who he brought along and how, what arguments worked with which colleagues, the difference between public-facing advocacy and the quieter work of building votes.
3. **Open questions and risk.** The FPD compliance monitoring gap, the limits of what one ordinance can guarantee, what "win the vote, then what" looks like.
4. **Lessons for planners.** Tie back to planning practice since this is a planning studio audience. Land use, public engagement, and immigrant communities intersect constantly, and these students will be writing the policy memos.
