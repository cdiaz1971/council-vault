I want to thank my fellow council members for placing their trust in me to serve as city council president.  I will work as hard for the success of this council as we deliver for the residents of Frederick as I did during my campaign. I will be a champion for the independent legislative branch and work hard for all of our success.

I want to quickly address some of what has happened in the last couple of weeks.

When I was considering running for city council a lot of people warned me that politics is dirty.  That it could get nasty.  Then I ran and nothing like that happened.

On the contrary, I met some amazing people including the other people up here on the dais with me.  Some of the people I met I can call my friends. Even when others had opponents either in the primaries or the general election, their campaigns never got nasty.  The differences in opinions were discussed in a civil manner.

Then after the general election things changed.  About two weeks ago we started getting emails and phone calls about the council leadership that got progressively more negative.  These quickly escalated and got meaner and personal.  Attacks rose to gross and insulting levels on random blogs not connected to any candidate.   

This nastiness wasn't from council members.  It became obvious that these attacks were directed by people with agendas.  Some were agendas of political payback for past defeats.  Or folks who had served up here many years ago trying to get back some relevance.  Some were from people who wanted to serve up here, but had always just missed. others were groups who fear that the changes in city government were  going to minimize their political influence.

The agenda those people behind these attacks DIDNT have is the agenda of the members of this council.  And that agenda is to make Frederick an even better place to live.  To address the real issues affecting the people of this wonderful city.

That is the work we want to do.  Make life better for the residents of Frederick.  Find ways to make life more affordable.  Protect those who are targeted by outside forces, to be the voice for those without a voice.

All 7 of us up here are ready to  put that nastiness behind us and  roll up our sleeves and get the work done.  Join us and let's all work together and do great things.
