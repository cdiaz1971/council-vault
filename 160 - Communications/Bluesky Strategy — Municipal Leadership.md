# Bluesky Strategy — Municipal Leadership

## Purpose

Build a steady, credible social media presence on Bluesky as a progressive municipal leader (Frederick City Council President) that attracts:

- Frederick residents
- Maryland / regional observers
- National followers interested in progressive municipal governance
- Policy professionals, journalists, and civic leaders

Goal is **durable influence and engagement — not virality.**

---

## Positioning

**Pragmatic progressive leader doing real governing.**

Tone should be:

- Calm, authoritative, accessible
- Evidence-oriented
- Transparent about tradeoffs
- Non-performative
- Focused on implementation realities
- Human but not overly personal

---

## Core Policy Areas

Primary:

- Housing & affordability (dominant topic)
- Cost-of-living issues

Secondary:

- Immigrant protection and inclusion
- Public participation in government
- Transparency and accountability

---

## Target Audience (Blended)

Content should resonate with at least one of:

### Local
- Residents affected by city decisions
- Community organizations
- Local media

### Regional
- Maryland policy watchers
- State/local government observers

### National
- Urbanists and housing advocates
- Progressive municipal leaders
- Policy professionals
- Civic-minded followers

---

## Voice: Hybrid Leadership Model

Professional authority + controlled human perspective.

### Include

- Real governance insight
- Community-grounded observations
- Decision explanations
- Leadership reflections (selective)

### Avoid

- Oversharing personal life
- Campaign-style messaging
- Emotional venting
- Generic inspirational content
- Culture-war hot takes

---

## Content Mix (Recommended)

- **50% — Governance & Policy**
- **20% — Civic Education / Explainers**
- **20% — Community Connection**
- **10% — Leadership Perspective**

---

## Housing Content Strategy (Top Priority)

Key themes to emphasize:

- Tradeoffs (depth of affordability vs total units)
- Legal and financial constraints
- Implementation realities
- State vs local authority
- Why processes take time
- What residents misunderstand
- How public input influences outcomes

Avoid framing debates as good vs evil.

## Topic Intake Guidelines

Capture anything that might be educational or revealing, such as:

- Policy debates
- Resident concerns

- Legislative developments
- Misconceptions worth correcting
- Implementation challenges
- Meaningful leadership moments

---

## Engagement Strategy

Reply selectively:

### Engage when

- Questions are in good faith
- Clarification is helpful
- Constructive discussion is possible
- Trust can be strengthened

### Avoid engaging when

- Bad-faith attacks
- Endless debates
- Personal insults
- Situations likely to escalate conflict

---

## Risk Guardrails

Do NOT:

- Commit to future votes prematurely
- Share confidential information
- Criticize staff publicly
- Personalize disputes
- Escalate local controversies unnecessarily
- Misstate facts or authority

Prefer system-level explanations over project-specific conflicts.
