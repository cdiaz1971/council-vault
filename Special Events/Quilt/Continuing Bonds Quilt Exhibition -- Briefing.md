# Continuing Bonds Quilt Exhibition -- Event Briefing

## Summary

Briefing for the Art in City Hall opening reception for The Continuing Bonds Quilt Exhibition on June 9, 2026, presented by Living Through Loss, a grief community for families who have lost loved ones to substance use disorder.

## What It Is

The Continuing Bonds Quilt is a traveling exhibition launched on International Overdose Awareness Day, August 31, 2025. It travels to more than 13 locations across Maryland over the course of a year. Each block of the quilt was created in memory of a loved one lost to substance use by members of Living Through Loss, a grief community for surviving family and parents.

The central concept: rather than viewing grief as a process of letting go, continued bonds emphasize that grief is about finding ways to continue the connection through visual art. Personal symbols, storytelling, and community are stitched together to form a continued bond.

## The Quilt

30 individual blocks, each made by a family member. The blocks represent people lost to SUD across all kinds of lives -- soldiers, cosmetologists, college graduates, kids who loved sports and music and the outdoors. Parents describe their loved ones sending signs through daisies, crows, feathers, and color.

## The Organizers

Living Through Loss was founded by Jamie Eaton. The quilt was planned and assembled by Courtney Prahl, Krissy Benson, and Whitney Dahlberg, who operate under the name Lend a Hand. This is their twelfth community project.

Website: living-through-loss.org | @lthroughloss

## Frederick's Role

The quilt is on display at Frederick City Hall, 101 N. Court Street, for the month of June during business hours Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 4 PM, and during public meeting times.

Other exhibition locations include The Delaplaine Arts Center, The MOSAIC Center at Frederick Community College, Notre Dame of Maryland University, Washington County Arts Council, Frederick Health Crestwood, and Hood College School of Behavioral Health and Sciences.
