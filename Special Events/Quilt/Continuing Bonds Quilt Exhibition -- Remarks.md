# Continuing Bonds Quilt Exhibition -- Opening Remarks

## Summary

3-minute opening remarks for César to deliver at the Art in City Hall reception for The Continuing Bonds Quilt Exhibition on June 9, 2026 at approximately 5 PM.

## Remarks

Thank you to Living Through Loss, to Courtney, Krissy, and Whitney, and to everyone who brought this quilt to Frederick.

When I walked through and looked at these blocks, I kept stopping. Not because the art stopped me, though it is beautiful. I kept stopping because of the stories.

A mother who planted a tree in her backyard because her son had red hair and loved soccer. A parent who gets pictures of daisies in their memory. A family that traced four hearts from a piece of jewelry their daughter bought as a child. A crow that showed up outside a window the morning after a death and would not stop talking.

These are not statistics. They are our neighbors. Our children. Our community.

The organizing idea behind this work is continued bonds. Grief is not about letting go. It is about finding ways to stay connected, to carry the people we have lost forward with us. That is what every person who made one of these blocks is doing. They are saying: my child was here. My child mattered. Look.

I think that is also what we are called to do as a city. Not to look away. To bring it into City Hall and say: this is part of our community. We see you.

To the families here today, thank you for trusting us with this. Your loved ones are welcome here. Their stories belong here.

Delivery target: 3 minutes. Spoken at approximately 5 PM during the opening reception. No policy ask -- register of witness and community solidarity.
