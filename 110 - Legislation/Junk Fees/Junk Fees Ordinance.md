# Rental Junk Fee Elimination Ordinance

# ORDINANCE NO. ___-26

## AN ORDINANCE TO ESTABLISH ALL-IN RENT PRICING, PROHIBIT NON-RENTAL FEES IN RENTAL HOUSING, AND CONDITION RENTAL LICENSING ON COMPLIANCE IN THE CITY OF FREDERICK

---

## WHEREAS

The Mayor and Board of Aldermen find that the proliferation of mandatory rental fees separate from base rent undermines price transparency, distorts the housing market, and imposes unjust and unpredictable financial burdens on tenants; and  

WHEREAS, such practices function as hidden rent increases and evade meaningful regulation; and  

WHEREAS, it is the intent of the City to establish a clear, enforceable standard that the price of rental housing shall be represented and charged as a single, all-inclusive amount;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ENACTED...

---

## Section 1. Definitions

**(a) "Total Rent"**  
The full monthly amount required to occupy a rental unit, inclusive of all mandatory charges of any kind.

**(b) "Separate Fee"**  
Any charge imposed on a tenant that is not included in Total Rent.

**(c) "Prohibited Fee"**  
Any Separate Fee that is required, automatic, or a condition of tenancy.

**(d) "Optional Service"**  
A service that:
1. Is affirmatively requested by the tenant in writing  
2. Is not required for tenancy  
3. Can be declined without penalty, delay, or adverse treatment  

---

## Section 2. All-In Pricing Requirement

(a) A landlord shall charge and advertise **only Total Rent**.

(b) It shall be unlawful to:
- Advertise rent that is less than Total Rent  
- Require payment of any charge not included in Total Rent  

(c) All mandatory costs of tenancy shall be consolidated into Total Rent.

---

## Section 3. Prohibition on Separate Fees

(a) All Prohibited Fees are unlawful.

(b) The following are prohibited, including but not limited to:

- Administrative fees  
- Move-in or move-out fees  
- Mandatory technology or platform fees  
- Payment processing fees for any standard payment method  
- Mandatory pest control, trash, or common area maintenance fees charged separately  
- Utility administration or billing fees  
- Lease renewal fees  
- Inspection or compliance fees  
- Any fee designed to recover ordinary costs of doing business  

---

## Section 4. Strict Limits on Remaining Charges

**(a) Application Fees**
- Shall not exceed actual out-of-pocket screening costs  
- Maximum of $25 per applicant  
- Must be refunded if no screening is performed  

**(b) Security Deposits**
- Subject to Maryland law  
- No non-refundable deposits permitted  

**(c) Late Fees**
- Limited to amounts authorized by state law  
- No compounding or additional penalties  

---

## Section 5. Anti-Circumvention

(a) It shall be unlawful to:

- Mischaracterize mandatory charges as optional  
- Condition lease approval on purchase of any service  
- Use third-party vendors to impose prohibited fees  
- Offer rent discounts contingent on separate fees  

(b) Any fee shall be presumed mandatory if:
- More than 25% of tenants are charged the fee; or  
- Refusal results in delay, denial, or inferior lease terms  

---

## Section 6. Utility Charges

(a) Utilities may be charged separately only if:
- Based on actual metered usage; or  
- Allocated through a lawful and transparent formula  

(b) Flat utility fees are prohibited unless included in Total Rent  

(c) No administrative, service, or billing fees may be added to utility charges  

---

## Section 7. Licensing Enforcement

(a) Compliance with this ordinance is a condition of holding a rental license  

(b) The City may:
- Deny issuance or renewal of a license  
- Suspend or revoke a license for violations  

(c) Each unlawful lease or unit constitutes a separate violation  

---

## Section 8. Penalties

(a) Civil penalties:

- $500 per unit for first violation  
- $1,000 per unit for second violation  
- $2,500 per unit for subsequent violations  

(b) Each month of noncompliance constitutes a separate offense  

---

## Section 9. Private Right of Action

(a) Tenants may recover:

- Three times the amount of unlawful fees paid  
- Statutory damages of not less than $1,000  
- Reasonable attorney's fees and costs  

(b) Burden of proof shall be on the landlord to demonstrate compliance  

(c) Any lease provision in violation of this ordinance is void and unenforceable  

---

## Section 10. Presumption of Illegality

Any charge not explicitly authorized by this ordinance shall be presumed unlawful  

---

## Section 11. Effective Date

This ordinance shall take effect 120 days after adoption and shall apply to all new and renewed leases thereafter
