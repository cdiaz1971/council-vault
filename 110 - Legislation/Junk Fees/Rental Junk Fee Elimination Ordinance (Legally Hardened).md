# Rental Junk Fee Elimination Ordinance (Legally Hardened)

# ORDINANCE NO. ___-26

## AN ORDINANCE TO ESTABLISH ALL-IN RENT PRICING, REGULATE RENTAL HOUSING FEE PRACTICES, AND CONDITION RENTAL LICENSING ON COMPLIANCE IN THE CITY OF FREDERICK

---

## WHEREAS

The Mayor and Board of Aldermen find that the proliferation of mandatory rental fees separate from base rent undermines price transparency, distorts the housing market, and imposes unjust and unpredictable financial burdens on tenants; and  

WHEREAS, such practices function as hidden rent increases and obscure the true cost of housing; and  

WHEREAS, the City has a substantial interest in preventing unfair and deceptive trade practices in the rental housing market; and  

WHEREAS, the City is authorized to regulate rental housing through licensing and to protect the public health, safety, and welfare through its police powers;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ENACTED...

---

## Section 1. Authority and Intent

(a) This ordinance is enacted pursuant to the City's home rule authority and police powers to protect the public health, safety, and welfare, including the authority to regulate rental housing through licensing and to prevent unfair and deceptive trade practices.

(b) This ordinance regulates the manner in which rental housing is advertised, priced, and offered to tenants, and establishes conditions for rental licensing.

(c) Nothing in this ordinance shall be construed to:

1. Regulate or limit the amount of rent that a landlord may charge;  
2. Conflict with or preempt any provision of Maryland landlord-tenant law; or  
3. Prohibit charges expressly authorized by state law  

(d) It is the intent of this ordinance to ensure that all mandatory charges associated with tenancy are disclosed and incorporated into a single total price for purposes of transparency, fairness, and enforcement.

---

## Section 2. Definitions

**(a) "Total Rent"**  
The total periodic charge required to be paid by a tenant for the right to occupy a rental unit, inclusive of all mandatory charges, however denominated, that are required as a condition of tenancy.  

The term is used solely for purposes of disclosure, advertising, and licensing compliance, and shall not be interpreted to limit the amount a landlord may charge.

**(b) "Separate Fee"**  
Any charge imposed on a tenant that is not included in Total Rent.

**(c) "Prohibited Fee"**  
Any Separate Fee that is required, automatic, or a condition of tenancy.

**(d) "Optional Service"**  
A service that:

1. Is affirmatively requested by the tenant in writing  
2. Is not required for tenancy  
3. Can be declined without penalty, delay, or adverse treatment  

---

## Section 3. All-In Pricing Requirement

(a) A landlord shall charge and advertise only Total Rent.

(b) It shall be unlawful to:

- Advertise rent that is less than Total Rent  
- Require payment of any charge not included in Total Rent  

(c) All mandatory costs of tenancy shall be consolidated into Total Rent.

(d) The requirements of this section regulate advertising, disclosure, and billing practices and shall not be interpreted as limiting the lawful amount of rent a landlord may establish.

---

## Section 4. Regulation of Separate Fees

(a) Prohibited Fees are unlawful.

(b) Without limitation, the following charges shall be considered Prohibited Fees when not included in Total Rent:

- Administrative fees  
- Move-in or move-out fees  
- Mandatory technology or platform fees  
- Payment processing fees for standard payment methods  
- Mandatory pest control, trash, or common area maintenance fees charged separately  
- Utility administration or billing fees  
- Lease renewal fees  
- Inspection or compliance fees  
- Any charge that recovers ordinary costs of property ownership or management  

(c) Nothing in this section shall prohibit:

1. Charges expressly permitted under Maryland law, including security deposits and late fees  
2. Optional services requested by the tenant  
3. Utility charges compliant with Section 6  

(d) A charge shall not be considered separate from rent if it is incorporated into Total Rent.

---

## Section 5. Limited Permissible Charges

(a) **Application Fees**

- Shall not exceed actual out-of-pocket screening costs  
- Must be documented upon request  
- Must be refunded if no screening is performed  

(b) **Security Deposits**

- Governed by Maryland law  
- No additional non-refundable deposits permitted  

(c) **Late Fees**

- Limited strictly to amounts authorized by Maryland law  
- No additional or compounding penalties permitted  

---

## Section 6. Utility Charges

(a) Utilities may be charged separately only if:

- Based on actual metered usage; or  
- Allocated through a lawful, transparent, and consistently applied formula  

(b) Flat utility fees are prohibited unless included in Total Rent.

(c) No administrative, service, or billing fees may be added to utility charges.

---

## Section 7. Anti-Circumvention

(a) It shall be unlawful to:

- Mischaracterize mandatory charges as optional  
- Condition lease approval on purchase of any service  
- Use third-party vendors to impose charges that would be prohibited if charged directly  
- Offer rent discounts contingent on payment of separate fees  

(b) Any fee shall be presumed mandatory if:

- More than 25% of tenants are charged the fee; or  
- Refusal results in delay, denial, or inferior lease terms  

(c) In any enforcement action, the form or label of a charge shall not control. The City may consider the substance, function, and effect of the charge in determining compliance.

---

## Section 8. Rental Licensing Compliance

(a) Compliance with this ordinance is a material condition of:

- Issuance of a rental housing license  
- Renewal of a rental housing license  
- Continued eligibility to operate rental housing within the City  

(b) The City may deny, suspend, or revoke a rental license for:

- Any violation of this ordinance  
- Any pattern or practice of noncompliance  

(c) The City may condition continued licensure on restitution to affected tenants.

(d) The remedies provided in this section are in addition to any other enforcement authority available to the City.

---

## Section 9. Penalties

(a) Civil penalties:

- $500 per unit for first violation  
- $1,000 per unit for second violation  
- $2,500 per unit for subsequent violations  

(b) Each unit and each month of noncompliance shall constitute a separate violation.

---

## Section 10. Private Right of Action

(a) Tenants may recover:

- Three times the amount of unlawful charges paid  
- Statutory damages of not less than $1,000  
- Reasonable attorney's fees and costs  

(b) The burden of proof shall be on the landlord to demonstrate compliance.

(c) Any lease provision in violation of this ordinance is void and unenforceable.

---

## Section 11. Presumption of Illegality

Any charge not explicitly authorized by this ordinance shall be presumed unlawful unless incorporated into Total Rent.

---

## Section 12. Severability

If any provision of this ordinance is held invalid or preempted by state law, such invalidity shall not affect the remaining provisions, which shall remain in full force and effect.

---

## Section 13. Effective Date

This ordinance shall take effect 120 days after adoption and shall apply to all new and renewed leases thereafter.
