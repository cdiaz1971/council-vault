## Housing Production and Affordability

### Real Rent Ordinance
**Vehicle:** Ordinance
**Owner:** César
**Status:** Ready to Introduce
**Notes:** Section-by-section comparison of March 30 draft vs. newer draft completed. Pet provisions added in revised draft. Saved as `Real Rent Ordinance -- Revised.docx`. FY2027 budget closed -- no remaining reason to delay. Next action: set introduction date. Fee rate decision on Rental Licensing rewrite has downstream implications for threshold. Drive file ID: `1W-JbxIeeE4gTpGNxcLL2UeYGEnSn4ih2`.

### Housing Production Target Resolution
**Vehicle:** Resolution
**Owner:** César
**Status:** Ready to Introduce
**Notes:** 1,400 units/year through 2035. 15% affordability at 80% AMI or below, 33% sub-target at 60% AMI or below. Austin 2017 Strategic Housing Blueprint is closest structural parallel. Frederick would be among first Maryland municipalities to formally respond to state 2030 targets. César has full discretion to move whenever he chooses.

### Inclusionary Zoning Reform
**Vehicle:** Ordinance (LMC amendment)
**Owner:** César
**Status:** Pre-Legislative
**Notes:** Frederick has existing MPDU framework under Chapter 19 with 12.5% baseline rate and payment-in-lieu fee at $2.00/sq ft -- both identified as underperforming. Thread open to track MPDU reform path, peer city comparisons, and near-term legislative opportunity. Next action: determine whether the Section 19-6.1 three-year recalculation trigger can be accelerated and confirm City Housing Fund balance.

## Tenant Protections

### Rental Licensing Ordinance Rewrite
**Vehicle:** Ordinance
**Owner:** Brehm (lead) / Public Works, Community and Culture, Government Operations
**Status:** In Committee
**Notes:** Brehm-authored comprehensive rewrite of Article V (Chapter 12.5). Workshop completed May 26. Fee revision would cut rental licensing fee from $240 to ~$150 per license (biennial). Key tensions: fee cut reduces Housing Fund capacity; 8-year new construction exemption is a significant carve-out; LIHTC interaction with government-funding exemption needs clarification. Fee rate decision has downstream implications for Real Rent Ordinance threshold. Reference: [[Public Works Committee Brief -- 2026-05-26]].
