# Housing Production Target Resolution (2026-05-13)

CITY OF FREDERICK

RESOLUTION

A Resolution Establishing a Housing Production Target and Mixed-Income Affordability Framework

Introduced by: Council President Cesar Diaz

Date of Introduction: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

---

WHEREAS, the City of Frederick is committed to the health, safety, and economic well-being of all residents, and access to stable, affordable housing is foundational to those goals;

WHEREAS, the City commissioned and received in February 2026 the TPMA Housing Data Report (the "TPMA Report"), a comprehensive analysis of current housing conditions and projected demand in Frederick;

WHEREAS, the TPMA Report documents a current housing shortage of 8,133 units and projects that the City will require 13,774 new housing units by 2035 to meet anticipated demand;

WHEREAS, based on the TPMA Report's projections of household growth and local housing demand, meeting that projected demand would require producing an average of approximately 1,400 units per year through 2035, a figure the Council adopts as a planning benchmark to guide land use, development review, and housing policy decisions;

WHEREAS, the TPMA Report's projections reflect a combination of external demand from anticipated household growth and internal demand from local turnover patterns, and the Council acknowledges that projections of this nature carry inherent uncertainty and will be subject to periodic review as conditions and data evolve;

WHEREAS, the TPMA Report further documents that 50 percent of renter households in Frederick are cost-burdened, spending more than 30 percent of household income on housing, and that 25 percent are severely cost-burdened, spending more than 50 percent of household income on housing;

WHEREAS, 14.5 percent of all Frederick households are severely cost-burdened, reflecting a structural gap between housing costs and the incomes of lower-wage workers and families;

WHEREAS, the State of Maryland, pursuant to its 2030 Housing Production Targets, has set a target of 5,194 new units for Frederick by 2030, providing an external accountability benchmark and alignment with statewide housing policy;

WHEREAS, without deliberate affordability requirements, market-rate production alone will not address the housing needs of households at or below 80 percent of Area Median Income (AMI), and particularly those at or below 60 percent AMI, who face the most severe cost burdens;

WHEREAS, preserving existing affordable housing units and preventing displacement of long-term residents are essential complements to new production and cannot be separated from a comprehensive housing strategy;

WHEREAS, the City's Comprehensive Plan and smart growth principles call for housing development that is well-located, transit-accessible, and integrated with infrastructure capacity;

WHEREAS, the Council finds that a formal statement of production targets and affordability standards is necessary to provide direction to city departments, align future land use and financing decisions, and establish a baseline against which progress can be measured and reported;

NOW, THEREFORE,

BE IT RESOLVED that the Mayor and City Council of Frederick hereby establish an annual housing production target of 1,400 units per year through 2035, consistent with the TPMA Report projections, as a guiding benchmark for land use, development review, and housing policy decisions;

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the City establishes an affordability framework requiring that at least 15 percent of new residential units produced annually be affordable to households at or below 80 percent of Area Median Income (AMI), with a sub-target that at least 33 percent of those affordable units be affordable to households at or below 60 percent AMI;

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the City commits to developing and implementing preservation and anti-displacement strategies to protect existing affordable housing stock and minimize involuntary displacement of current residents, particularly lower-income renters;

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that all housing production, affordability, and land use decisions by the City shall be guided by smart growth principles, align with the City's Comprehensive Plan, and account for infrastructure capacity and equitable geographic distribution;

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that beginning in Fiscal Year 2027, the Mayor shall transmit to the City Council an Annual Housing Report to be published publicly, documenting: (a) total residential units permitted, under construction, and completed in the prior fiscal year; (b) the number and percentage of units meeting the affordability targets established herein; (c) the number of affordable units preserved or lost; (d) displacement indicators including renter eviction rates and housing cost trends; and (e) recommended policy adjustments where production or affordability targets are not being met;

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that this Resolution shall serve as the Council's statement of legislative intent and the foundational basis for subsequent ordinances, zoning amendments, and programmatic initiatives necessary to implement these targets.

PASSED AND ADOPTED by the Mayor and City Council of the City of Frederick, Maryland, this \_\_\_\_\_ day of \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_, 2026.
