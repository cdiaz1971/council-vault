# Housing Production Target Resolution

## Summary

A resolution establishing a formal housing production target for the City of Frederick, tied to the documented 8,133-unit shortage. As of March 20, 2026, the resolution has been deferred twice. The housing study timeline is being used as a potential justification for further delay.

## Open Questions

- What is the current draft language and who is the primary sponsor?
- Is the resolution framed as a goal, a mandate, or a reporting requirement?
- Is the housing study release a genuine prerequisite or a deferral mechanism?
- How does the $250k affordable housing RFP line in the FY2027 budget relate -- does it feed this resolution or stand independently?
- What is the council vote alignment? Who are the holdouts and why?

## Key Dates

- Deferred twice as of March 20, 2026
- April 9 budget session (Housing and Human Services) is the next relevant pressure point
- May 14 is the hard deadline for council leverage on the FY2027 budget
