## Version and document discipline

- Confirm the exact text, source document, and dated action before using a version for a meeting or decision.
- Distinguish ordinance or resolution text from a tracking note with a similar title.
- Keep sample legislation, placeholder templates, and draft findings distinct from introduced or enacted measures.
- Read briefs against the version they actually reviewed.
- Preserve draft variants and bill-specific research; topic-wide background belongs in the appropriate subject wiki.
