# Frederick Renter Data Baseline

All figures are for the **City of Frederick** unless marked otherwise. "District 2" means City Council District 2, not Frederick County Election District 2.

## How many renters

The 2020 Census counted **13,724 renter-occupied homes, 45.8 percent** of the City's 29,945 occupied homes.

The 2020 to 2024 American Community Survey estimates **13,849 renter households (±786), 41.2 percent** of households. The two numbers differ because the Census is a full count from one point in time, while the ACS is a sample spread over five years with a larger household base.

**Use in public:** "About 14,000 Frederick households rent, between four and five of every ten households in the City."

## What renters pay and earn

| Measure | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Median gross rent (rent plus utilities) | $1,764 a month (±$39) | ACS 2020 to 2024 |
| Income needed to keep that rent at 30% of income | About $70,560 a year | Calculation |
| Renters paying 30% or more of income on rent | About 52.5% (6,985 of 13,308 households) | ACS 2020 to 2024 |
| Renters paying 35% or more | 42.1% (5,608 households, ±610) | ACS 2020 to 2024 |
| Median household income, all households | $97,069 (±$4,612) | ACS 2020 to 2024 |
| Median family income | $112,741 (±$5,324) | ACS 2020 to 2024 |
| Poverty rate, all people | 9.2% (±1.4%) | ACS 2020 to 2024 |
| Households below the ALICE threshold, City | 43% (32% countywide) | United Way 2026 report (2024 data) |
| County children in ALICE or poverty households who live in rentals | 72% | United Way 2025 report (2023 data) |

**Use in public:** "More than half of Frederick renters spend over 30 percent of their income on rent and utilities, and four in ten spend more than 35 percent."

A citywide median income figure for renters alone is not in hand yet. ACS table B25119 has it, and it would make the affordability case sharper.

**Use in drafting:** Spanish is the clear first language for translated forms and the lease translation rule. Additional languages should come from ACS table B16001 or C16001 for Frederick city, and ideally from the language data for renter households specifically.

## Rental licensing

- The license fee is **$240 per unit for two years**.
- **Exemption:** buildings with certificates of occupancy eight years old or newer (§ 12.5-76(a)(5)).
- Licensing revenue ran about **$1.73 million against a $300,000 projection** in FY2026 through the third quarter (Affordability Agenda).
- An earlier licensing rewrite draft proposed cutting the fee to about $150 for two years. The Mayor's Affordable Housing Task Force names licensing revenue as a source for the City Housing Fund. The two positions conflict.

## HUD income limits

Frederick is part of HUD's Washington-Arlington-Alexandria metro area. The FY2026 HUD median family income for that area is **$166,100**. Frederick's own median family income is $112,741, about 68 percent of that figure. See [[Frederick City AMI - Analysis and Options]].

## Missing data and where to get it

These gaps matter most for the package. Each one would turn a general argument into a Frederick fact.

1. **Eviction filings, judgments, and completed evictions for City addresses.** Maryland Judiciary data (District Court landlord-tenant case data, or the Judiciary's public data requests). Eviction Lab may have Frederick County figures. Ask for counts by year and case type, plus the share of filings that ended in a warrant executed by the sheriff.
2. **Code complaints and violations for rental properties.** The City's code enforcement records. Ask for complaints by type (mold, heat, pests, water), time to correction, and repeat properties.
3. **Licensed rental units by building age, size, and owner.** City rental licensing database. This sizes the eight-year exemption gap and shows how many units belong to large or out-of-state owners.
4. **Fee prevalence.** A sample of current Frederick listings, comparing advertised rent with the full monthly cost. Staff or a volunteer can do this from public listing sites.
5. **Properties using algorithmic pricing software.** Match Frederick's large complexes against management companies named in the U.S. Department of Justice RealPage case.
6. **License revocations, denials, and unfit-unit findings per year.** City code enforcement. This sizes the relocation cost exposure for the fiscal note.
7. **Renter household income and language for renters specifically.** ACS tables B25119 and B25010 for income, and the language tables limited to renters.
8. **Eviction Access to Counsel use in Frederick.** The state program provider, through 211 or Maryland Legal Aid's Frederick office.
