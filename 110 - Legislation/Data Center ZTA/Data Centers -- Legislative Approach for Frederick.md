# Data Centers -- Legislative Approach for Frederick

From the December 19, 2025 council discussion. The core legislative question is how Frederick City establishes its authority over data center siting before any applications arrive, while keeping the county appropriately involved in zoning decisions.

## The Annexation and Zoning Split

Frederick City Council determines property annexation for data center sites. However, final zoning authority rests with the County Council, and the December 19 discussion reflected public preference for County Council involvement specifically because of transparency and accessibility concerns. The legislative approach needs to account for this split -- Frederick City can establish siting requirements and permit conditions, but the zoning decision is not solely within city control.

## Sequencing: Establish Authority Before Applications Arrive

The clearest guidance from the discussion: Frederick City should enact legislation establishing its authority over data center siting before any applications are filed. Reactive legislation -- written in response to a specific project -- is weaker, more politically difficult, and more likely to be challenged. The window to act proactively is now, while no specific application is pending.

That said, the legislation itself should not be enacted until the Data Center Impact Study findings are presented. The right sequencing is: pass a resolution or ordinance establishing the council's intent to regulate, wait for the study, then enact the full regulatory framework informed by the study's site suitability and infrastructure analysis.

## Key Legislative Requirements to Include

Rigorous permit requirements should be established covering: demonstrated power supply availability, cooling technology disclosure, water consumption projections, escrow funding for damage and treatment facility upgrades, and scale category definitions distinguishing server rooms from hyperscale facilities. Transparency requirements should ensure the approval process is publicly accessible at both city and county levels.

## Open Legislative Questions

The Data Center Impact Study has not yet been delivered. Until it is, specific siting criteria, numerical thresholds for water use and power draw, and recommended locations cannot be established with the specificity needed for durable ordinance language. These are the study's core deliverables.

## Why This Matters for César

The action item from December 19 is to move on the authority-establishing ordinance now, before applications force reactive legislating. The study timeline is the pacing constraint. Coordinating with the county on zoning transparency is the relationship management task.
