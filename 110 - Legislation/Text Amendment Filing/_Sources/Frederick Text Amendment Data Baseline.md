| ADU amendment PC25-614ZTA | City | Staff | Moved in 2026 |

**What the table shows.** Recent private filings were made by developers, a hospital system, and their land use counsel. The one resident-originated amendment in the record did not go in as a private application. The resident sent it to Council members, who carried it, because the fee "would have been a showstopper for us as residents" (Sellner, FNP December 30, 2025). The submitted text writes that path into the code and removes the fee from it.

## Cost to file

- Private application fee: **$4,400** (FNP October 23, 2026, and December 30, 2025). An FNP report on February 10, 2026 gave $5,000. G-26-8, the fee schedule passed August 20, 2026, may have changed it. Confirm the current figure with Planning before quoting.
- A Council member acting in an official capacity pays no fee under the 2026 Rules of Procedure.
- Applicants also pay for counsel, consultants, renderings, traffic and engineering work (Graditor, FNP October 23, 2026).

## Missing numbers (request from Planning before the Commission hearing)

1. Text amendments filed per year, 2020 to 2026, by filer type: private developer or business, institution, resident, Council member or Mayor, staff.
2. Outcome of each: approved, approved with changes, denied, withdrawn, pending.
3. Staff hours per private text amendment, and review rounds (PC25-626ZTA is on at least its third submittal).
4. Fee revenue from text amendment applications per year. This is the fiscal impact figure.
5. How many private filings were tied to a specific site or project.

Fiscal impact formula, once item 1 and the fee are confirmed: lost fee revenue = private filings per year x fee, set against staff hours no longer spent on non-viable applications.
