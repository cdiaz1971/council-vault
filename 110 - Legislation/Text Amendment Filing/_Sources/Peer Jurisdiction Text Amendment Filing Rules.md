# Peer Jurisdiction Text Amendment Filing Rules

Maryland municipalities first, then counties. Each row says what was verified and what still needs checking.

| Jurisdiction | Who may file or initiate | Private party route | Verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rockville** (city, Montgomery County) | Mayor and Council only. Sec. 25.1.4.2(a): "The Mayor and Council may make an application for an amendment to the text of this Chapter." The City Manager submits it to the City Clerk | None stated in the section. Private parties would ask the Mayor and Council | Yes, Zoning Ordinance effective August 1, 2026. Prior rule under old Sec. 25.06.02 not confirmed; Rockville's recent text amendments were filed by "Mayor and Council of Rockville, Applicant" |
| **Gaithersburg** (city, Montgomery County) | The application form requires a "Requested Sponsor": the Planning Commission or the Mayor and City Council | Private applicant fills out the form but must name a sponsoring body | Form verified. Code section not pulled; verify in City Code Chapter 24 |
| **Montgomery County** | District Council (the County Council sitting on land use) introduces ZTAs | The Planning Board and others request introduction | Yes, Montgomery Planning ZTA guide. Exact rule on private requests not stated in the guide |
| **Frederick County** | § 1-19-3.100.2: "The County Council may initiate a zoning text amendment with no particular form required." | Not confirmed whether a separate section accepts private applications | Section verified. Check neighboring sections before saying private filing is barred in the County |

## How to use this

- **Rockville is the headline peer.** A Maryland city with a professional planning staff, a recent comprehensive rewrite, and a Mayor and Council structure chose in 2026 to make the elected body the only applicant. Frederick's amendment is less restrictive: it lets any elected City official file and keeps a Division route.
- **Gaithersburg is the sponsorship model.** Private ideas enter through a named public sponsor, which is the practice Frederick's amendment formalizes.
- **Counties.** Frederick County and Montgomery County both put initiation with the council. Prince George's County (District Council) is a likely third example; not verified this session.
- **Do not overstate.** Say "Rockville, Montgomery County, and Frederick County put text amendments in the hands of the elected body." Do not say "no Maryland jurisdiction lets residents file," which has not been checked.
