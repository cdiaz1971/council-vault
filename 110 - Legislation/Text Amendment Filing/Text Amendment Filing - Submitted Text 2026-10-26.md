Text Amendment: Application Filing. 10/26/2026. Submitted by Council President César Díaz and Council Member Peter Brehm.

## Clean text

[LMC Sec. 306](https://library.municode.com/md/frederick/codes/land_management_code?nodeId=ART3PR_S306TEAMZOMAAM) Text Amendments.

**Authority to File.** An application to amend the text of this Code may be filed by:

- An elected City official; or
- The Division.

**Application Review.**

**Submitted by City Official.** Upon receipt of a complete text amendment application from a City official, the Division shall submit the application to the City Council for review at a public meeting scheduled by the Council President. At that meeting, the submitting City official(s) shall provide a broad overview of the text amendment concept, including its alignment with the City Comprehensive Plan, as well as potential benefits and areas of concern that will require further analysis as the amendment is refined. Council Members will discuss the proposal and offer recommendations but will not take a formal vote.

**Submitted by Division.** Upon preparing a complete text amendment application, the Division shall provide notice to the City Council that the application is being submitted and present a broad overview of the text amendment concept, including its alignment with the City Comprehensive Plan, as well as potential benefits and areas of concern that will require further analysis as the amendment is refined.

**Commission hearing.** Upon completion of the application review of a text amendment application, the Division shall schedule the proposed text amendment for a public hearing of the Commission to be held as soon as practicable.

**Commission Recommendation.** At the conclusion of the public hearing, the Commission shall recommend the approval, denial, or approval with modifications of the proposed text amendment. The Division shall forward the proposed text amendment, along with the Commission's recommendation, to the City Council President.

**City Council Hearing.** After receipt of the Commission's recommendation, the City Council President will schedule a public hearing by the City Council to be held as soon as practicable.

**City Council Decision.** At the conclusion of the public hearing, the City Council shall approve, approve with modifications, or deny the proposed text amendment in the same manner as it takes action on any other ordinance.

## Five changes, in plain terms

1. Residents and property owners can no longer file a text amendment application directly. Elected City officials and the Planning Division can.
2. A new review step: an official's application goes to a public Council meeting, scheduled by the Council President, where the sponsor presents the concept and Comprehensive Plan fit and members comment. No vote.
3. A Division application needs only notice and an overview to Council.
4. The Planning Commission hearing is scheduled after that review step, not on receipt of a complete application.
5. The Commission recommendation goes to the Council President instead of the Mayor, the Council President schedules the Council hearing, and "Board of Aldermen" becomes "City Council."

Unchanged: zoning map amendments, the Planning Commission's public hearing and recommendation, and the Council's final decision by ordinance.
