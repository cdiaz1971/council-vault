## Political Profile
Etzler is not an elected official -- he is a career City Hall staffer who moved from covering Frederick city government as a journalist to running its communications and, later, its government-relations function. His path (News-Post reporter and editor, 2016-2020, then straight into the Mayor's Office as spokesman) is a notable "poacher turned gamekeeper" trajectory worth remembering when assessing how the administration frames stories to press.

By spring 2026 he was Deputy Chief of Staff under Gayon Sampson. When Sampson left in June 2026 to become chief of staff to Comptroller Brooke Lierman, Mayor O'Connor's office did not hire an interim chief of staff -- instead, Etzler took on expanded responsibilities as part of the existing staff absorbing Sampson's duties. His current title, Director of Government Relations, reflects that reorganization, making him one of the administration's primary points of contact for Council-facing legislative and intergovernmental matters -- relevant to César's role as Council President for scheduling, agenda coordination, and state-legislative testimony sign-off.
