# Nash Ethics Hearing -- Decision Expected in Two Weeks

## Summary

[[Katie Nash]] attended a closed Ethics Commission hearing on March 25, 2026, regarding an ethics complaint alleging improper employment ties to data centers while she worked on related legislation. A final decision is expected within two weeks. Nash characterized the hearing as hostile and suggested commissioners may have already formed conclusions.

## Key Points

- The complaint centers on whether Nash had a conflict of interest due to her work as a lobbyist in the energy sector while introducing and advancing data center regulation on the City Council.
- The hearing was closed to the public. Nash's husband was not permitted to attend, and Nash was not allowed to record the proceedings.
- Questions from commissioners focused on the timing and process of her data center legislation, as well as her Maryland lobbying registrations.
- Nash maintains that working in the energy sector does not equate to working for data centers.
- She has two weeks to submit additional testimony or evidence following the hearing.
- The Ethics Commission is a five-member body with authority to issue fines up to $5,000, cease-and-desist orders, and referrals to legal counsel for court enforcement. Courts may void official actions taken by officials found to have a conflict of interest.
- Leslie Powell, the private attorney hired to investigate the complaint, declined to comment, citing confidentiality rules.
- Nash was notified of the complaint on December 19, 2025, around the time the new council administration began.
- Nash has filed a separate ethics complaint against Mayor O'Connor, alleging he has misused his office in connection with the complaint against her. O'Connor has stated he is comfortable allowing the Ethics Commission to operate independently.

## Relevance

This is an active ethics proceeding involving a council colleague. As Council President, César should be aware of the timeline (decision expected by approximately April 8, 2026) and the potential consequences, including the possibility of a court voiding legislative actions taken under a conflict of interest. The data center legislation Nash was working on remains a live policy area. The Nash-O'Connor dynamic adds a layer of political complexity to the council's relationship with the mayor's office.
