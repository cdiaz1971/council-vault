## Political Profile

Benford sits at the left edge of the Frederick County Democratic spectrum — a democratic socialist in the DSA mold, distinct from the moderate progressive lane occupied by most sitting county Democrats. His ideological markers are explicit: he has described himself as young, Mexican American, and queer, saying his "entire life has been politicized." He frames affordability, worker rights, and immigrant protection as interconnected economic justice issues, not siloed concerns.

His community base is rooted in Frederick DSA, where he led the immigrants rights and electoral working groups. This gives him an organized volunteer and voter contact infrastructure unusual for a first-time candidate. He has been consistently present at public forums — County Council hearings, city town halls, legislative town hall meetings — for at least a year before filing, building name recognition in civic spaces before announcing his candidacy.

His public communication style is direct and uncompromising, especially on immigration. He does not hedge on 287(g) or rent stabilization the way some of his primary opponents do. This distinguishes him from Natasha Valencia (District 3 Democratic primary competitor) who described rent stabilization as "worth further consideration" while Benford flatly endorses it.

His campaign website is bilingual (English and Spanish), signaling intentional outreach to Latino voters in District 3, particularly in the Golden Mile corridor, which he has called historically underserved.

## Legislative Record

N/A — not yet in office. Documented public positions include:

- **287(g) statewide ban:** Advocated repeatedly at public forums and in a Frederick News-Post letter to the editor for state legislation barring Maryland counties from participating in the federal immigration enforcement partnership program. Called the program "vile" and "discriminatory" in print.
- **Rent stabilization:** Explicit supporter. Attended January 2026 forum with Montgomery County officials and reiterated support on the record.
- **Data centers:** Opposed to unchecked growth; supported the Planning Commission's narrower recommended overlay zone (~1,585 acres) ahead of the County Council's December 2025 vote. Supports levying a business personal property tax on data centers, with revenue directed toward green energy, transportation, school construction, and affordable housing.
- **Transit:** Supports expansion of Transit-Plus hours, Sunday service, and improved route coverage.
- **Childcare:** Supports county subsidies for childcare center operating costs and co-op/in-home provider models.
- **Worker protections:** Supports county wage-theft ordinance, worker cooperative incentives, and collective bargaining protections.
- **Westside Regional Park / West Frederick Library:** Committed to completing long-deferred capital projects in District 3.
- **LGBTQ+ students:** Supports preserving and strengthening Frederick County Schools Policy 443.
