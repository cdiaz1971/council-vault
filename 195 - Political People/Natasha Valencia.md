## Political Profile

Valencia presents as a community-centered Democrat with a civic entrepreneurship lane: small business owner, arts nonprofit co-founder, appointed commissioner on two city bodies, now running for Frederick County Council. Her campaign tagline is "Leadership You Can Trust. Community-focused solutions for Frederick County," which signals a moderate, non-ideological pitch. She has not publicly identified with progressive or left-of-center policy language in available materials.

Her civic footprint is heavily city-of-Frederick-based despite running for county office. The Planning Commission and Education Commission seats were city appointments. Her Artspiration work and prior bakery were city-situated. This creates an implicit jurisdictional gap in her county-level credibility that opponents or primary challengers could exploit.

Her business background as a Black woman who opened during the pandemic and navigated COVID relief was covered sympathetically in the Frederick News-Post in 2020. That narrative will likely form her personal story on the trail. She has shown interest in land use and development through active Planning Commission participation, including votes on major projects like Brickworks.

Her co-candidacy with Christian Benford in the District 3 Democratic primary means she faces an intra-party fight before the general. Benford has raised more money and has positioned himself as explicitly not taking money from corporate PACs, which could draw a contrast with Valencia's donor base.

## Vulnerabilities and Controversies

### Planning Commission Absence / Quorum Failure -- LOW value
In June 2025, Valencia was absent from a Planning Commission meeting scheduled to vote on the Frederick Health Village master plan. Her absence (along with commissioner Llewellyn's) caused a quorum failure, delaying the vote. No documented criticism of Valencia specifically, but pattern of absences from a body she was appointed to six months prior is worth monitoring.

### Artspiration Bond Bill / Concurrent Roles -- MEDIUM value
Valencia co-founded Artspiration Frederick, a nonprofit arts space, and is listed as co-contact on a $2 million Maryland state bond bill request (2026 legislative session) sponsored by Sen. Karen Lewis Young and Del. Simpson. She is simultaneously a Planning Commissioner for the city (a land use role) and a candidate for County Council. If Artspiration seeks county funding, zoning relief, or capital investment, her roles create compounding conflict-of-interest optics. The bond bill is not a conflict per se but combines with her other roles to create a pattern. Evidence: bond bill fact sheet, mgaleg.maryland.gov, 2026 session.

### Zoning Inquiry / 900 E. Patrick Street -- LOW value
In April 2025, Valencia and O'Connor submitted a zoning determination request to the city for 900 E. Patrick Street, a Light Manufacturing (M1) zoned property. The city responded in May 2025 noting the property is also in the Airport Impact Overlay. This may relate to potential Artspiration expansion. No documented controversy but worth tracking if they pursue the site -- a city Planning Commissioner requesting zoning determinations for a property she may have personal/organizational interest in requires scrutiny.

### Correction in FNP Coverage -- VERY LOW value
The Frederick News-Post issued a correction on its initial Planning Commission appointment article, noting Valencia was one of two applicants, not the only applicant as originally reported. No direct implication for Valencia, but FNP had to correct the record.
