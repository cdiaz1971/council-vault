## Political Profile

Sampson is a Democratic establishment figure with a hybrid identity: a deeply rooted Frederick native with strong progressive credentialing who operates inside the O'Connor mayoral administration. His ideological lane is center-left institutionalist -- committed to equity and racial justice work (he oversees the Office of Equity and Inclusion among his portfolio) while operating comfortably within mainstream Democratic governance structures.

His career trajectory is deliberately credentialed. The O'Malley gubernatorial appointments while still a student, the Capitol Hill internship, the GBC and Hopkins stops before landing in Frederick city government -- this is a planned civic resume, not an accidental one. The MPA completion in 2019 while already working in the mayor's office signals continued professional investment.

His 8th-generation Frederick identity is politically potent and he deploys it. In a city where newer arrivals dominate much of the progressive activist landscape, Sampson represents a different kind of authority -- inherited belonging. He has publicly used his family's roots to frame his commitment to the community as ancestral, not ideological.

His fraternity membership (Alpha Phi Alpha, XiXi Lambda Chapter) connects him to one of the most politically networked Black fraternities in the country, with deep ties to civic leadership pipelines. His Sons of the American Legion membership (Francis Scott Key Post 11) reflects a multi-dimensional public identity that spans Black progressive networks and traditional American civic institutions simultaneously.

## Legislative Record
Not applicable -- Sampson is an appointed city official, not an elected legislator. His public policy footprint is executive: he co-signed a 2023 letter to the Maryland House Judiciary Committee in support of HB640, which repealed a Jim Crow-era provision requiring courts to assign offenders from Frederick to road work duty. The letter explicitly named the provision as rooted in "indentured servitude" and "racist ideologies."
