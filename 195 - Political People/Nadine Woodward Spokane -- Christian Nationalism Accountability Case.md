# Nadine Woodward Spokane -- Christian Nationalism Accountability Case

In August 2023, Spokane mayor Nadine Woodward appeared on stage at a prayer rally hosted by Christian nationalist Sean Feucht, where she received a public blessing from both Feucht and former state lawmaker Matt Shea -- a figure who has been associated with domestic terrorism, white nationalism, and advocacy for a Christian-only 51st state.

Woodward initially claimed she hadn't known Shea would be present. Shea publicly contradicted her, saying she had been invited before the fires that prompted the rally even began.

The Spokane City Council passed a 4-3 resolution condemning her actions as associating her with "an alleged domestic terrorist" and "known anti-LGBTQ extremist." Woodward lost her re-election bid to Lisa Brown 48-37% that November.

She has since filed an amended legal claim seeking $10 million from the city, arguing the council resolution violated her free speech and constitutional rights and caused irreparable harm to her professional and personal reputation.

A parallel lawsuit by Sean Feucht making similar claims was dismissed in 2025 for lack of legal merit.

## Why this matters for César

This case is useful political intelligence on two levels.

First, it illustrates what accountability from a city council looks like -- and what the legal exposure of that accountability is. A resolution condemning a colleague's or official's association with extremists is a legitimate council act, but it will be litigated. The Spokane case suggests the legal theory is weak (Feucht's parallel suit was dismissed), but the harassment and political cost are real.

Second, it is a case study in how Christian nationalism operates at the mayoral level -- through events that are framed as apolitical (prayer, community healing) but are organized by political actors with explicit nationalist agendas. The lesson for local elected officials: vet every public appearance. The framing of an event is not the politics of the people running it.
