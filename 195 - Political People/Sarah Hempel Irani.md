## Education and Early Career

- **BA, Fine Art / Classical Studies (magna cum laude):** Hillsdale College, Hillsdale, MI, 2000. Studied under sculptor Anthony Frudakis.
- **Sculptor's Assistant:** Jay Hall Carpenter, former Artist-in-Residence, Washington National Cathedral, 2000–2001.
- **Studio founding:** Established Hempel Studios, Frederick, MD, 2001. Began taking religious/liturgical commissions.
- **Pennsylvania period (2008–2015):** Relocated to Grove City, PA. Founded a nonprofit arts council and opened a community art center with studios, gallery, and black box theater under the Office of Economic Development.
- **MA, Medieval and Renaissance Studies (Humanities):** Hood College Graduate School, Frederick, MD, 2011. Thesis: "Piero Della Francesca's Misericordia Polyptych: Artist, Patron, and Community."
- **Return to Frederick (2015):** Re-established Hempel Studios, continued commissions, began teaching sculpture at Frederick Community College.
- **Cancer diagnosis:** 2017; publicly cancer-free since.
- **Claire McCardell monument:** Commissioned by Frederick Art Club; unveiled October 2021 at Carroll Creek Park. 7.5-foot bronze monument. High-profile civic collaboration.
- **Adjunct Faculty, Art, Frederick Community College:** Current.

## Political Profile

Hempel Irani is the most ideologically progressive member on an already all-Democratic Frederick City Council. Her platform centers on housing affordability, pedestrian safety and multimodal transportation, arts economic development, and immigrant inclusion. She ran explicitly as the voice of the arts and creative communities, and has framed herself as a deep listener representing constituencies -- immigrant communities, minority communities, artists -- that she argues have historically been "solved for" without genuine participation.

She is a first-time officeholder with no prior electoral experience, which makes her a genuine political newcomer. Her power base is rooted in arts networks, Good Shepherd Lutheran Church, and neighborhood-level organizing in District 5. She has no known ties to business associations, real estate interests, or traditional Democratic Party machine infrastructure in Frederick.

Her public communication style is warm, collaborative, and community-centered. She leans on her biography -- Navy family, starving artist beginnings, cancer survivor, 20+ years in Frederick -- to establish authentic local roots. She has articulated a values-explicit framework, describing her sculpture work as intentionally subverting the visual language of war monuments to honor women, peacemakers, and innovators.

Ideologically she is to the left of Mayor O'Connor (a moderate Democrat) and aligned with the council's newer, more progressive members. She has been willing to name structural tools like rent stabilization and land management code overhaul that O'Connor has historically been cautious about.

## Vulnerabilities and Controversies

### No Documented Controversies -- LOW concern

No ethics complaints, controversial votes, constituent service failures, or negative media coverage identified in research. She has been in office less than four months as of this profile date, so the record is very thin by design. No prior public roles to mine for controversy.

### Hillsdale College Background -- LOW concern, worth monitoring

Hempel Irani holds a BA from Hillsdale College (Class of 2000), a private conservative liberal arts college in Michigan known for its opposition to federal funding and its relationship with conservative political networks. This is not a controversy, but it is a biographical note worth tracking in the context of Frederick's political environment. She has not surfaced any conservative policy positions that would suggest it is politically operative, and her platform is unambiguously progressive. Still, opposition could try to weaponize it in a future cycle. Her stated values, public record, and voting behavior show no alignment with Hillsdale's current political orientation.

### Immigration Stance -- Monitor for blowback

Hempel Irani has stated clearly and on record that she supports non-citizen voting in municipal elections and opposes the 287(g) immigration enforcement program, calling it "really unjust." As a council ally, these positions align well with your advocacy priorities. As a monitoring note: these are the most politically exposed positions she holds in a district that still had 30% Republican vote share in 2025. Any future conservative mobilization in District 5 will likely target these statements.
