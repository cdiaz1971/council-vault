## Political Profile
Angie Lollar is a nonprofit housing professional, not an elected official. Her public profile is collaborative and community-oriented, framed around economic self-sufficiency and affordable housing development. She has been the primary public spokesperson for HACF's major development projects -- Lucas Village relocation (2024) and Catoctin View renovation (2023). Her professional network includes the Maryland DHCD, SCG Development, New Harbor Development, and Frederick County government. She appeared on the Frederick County Affordable Housing Council as of 2022.

Her relationship with Mayor O'Connor's administration appears close. Mayor O'Connor spoke at the September 2024 Foundry Square grand opening alongside Kevin Lollar. The HACF Board of Commissioners is appointed by the Mayor of the City of Frederick, meaning O'Connor's appointees are the board that governs Angie's tenure and that oversaw her elevation to ED. This is the most important structural fact for oversight purposes: accountability for HACF governance runs through the Mayor's office, not the Council.

## Key Projects and Decisions

**Lucas Village Redevelopment (2022–present)**
Angie was the public face of HACF's Section 18 application to HUD for demolition authority at Lucas Village. Upon approval, HACF relocated 88 households. Angie stated publicly the authority initially struggled to find placement for families but ultimately relocated all 88 with federal vouchers, approximately half to Foundry Square and Crestwood Manor. The Planning Commission approved the 222-unit redevelopment plan in August 2025. The City Council voted April 16, 2026 on three enabling actions: a $1 land donation (1.2-acre SWM parcel), a right-of-way vacation (Vermont Court / Pennsylvania Avenue, 0.37 acres), and a parkland dedication waiver (Resolution 26-8). Settlement deadline is May 2026 with New Harbor Development as LIHTC partner.

Open questions on relocation: no public documentation confirms right-of-return commitments for the 92 displaced households in writing.

**Catoctin View Homes Renovation (2008–2023)**
Long-running renovation of a senior living public housing property. Received the 2023 Maryland DHCD Excellence in Affordable Housing Preservation Award. Kevin Lollar was ED and Angie was Director of Economic Development during the full arc of this project.

## Vulnerabilities and Controversies

### Marital Relationship with Former Executive Director -- HIGH value
Angie Lollar is married to E. Kevin Lollar, who served as HACF Executive Director from August 2013 until his transition to Director of Development (exact transition date not in public record). Angie's entire rise at HACF occurred while Kevin held supervisory authority over her as sitting ED. She is now his organizational superior. This raises questions about whether the board conducted independent oversight of her promotions, whether a conflict of interest policy existed and was applied, and whether the ED succession was competitive and arms-length.

As of April 2026, no press coverage, no ethics complaint, and no HUD Inspector General findings address this arrangement. The absence of coverage does not mean the concern is unfounded. The HACF Board of Commissioners, appointed by Mayor O'Connor, governs this arrangement. Any governance failure here implicates the Mayor's oversight of his own appointees.

Key open questions:
- Was there a competitive search for the ED role when Kevin stepped back?
- Did the board have independent counsel review the transition?
- Was Angie's promotion history reviewed against any conflict of interest or anti-nepotism policy?
- Who was on the board during each of Angie's promotions?

### Lucas Village Relocation Handling -- MEDIUM value
The relocation of 88 households was publicly described by Angie as coming together "at the last minute." No right-of-return documentation is publicly available. Constituent concerns about the treatment of residents during relocation are unverified but plausible given the timeline pressure Angie acknowledged publicly.
